Xi tightens party discipline ahead of leadership reshuffle

China: CCP Discipline 2026-10-27 • 7 articles au total

En Bref
  • China's Communist Party held a four-day plenary session focused on strict self-governance and ideological unity under Xi Jinping.
  • The meeting emphasized an active anti-corruption campaign with 73 cases this year to enforce discipline and loyalty.
  • Observers view these moves as efforts to consolidate Xi's power and influence leadership succession amid secretive sessions.

China's Communist Party has convened a four-day plenary session led by Xi Jinping stressing ‘full and rigorous’ party self-governance and ideological unity. The meetings emphasize intensified anti-corruption work and stricter internal discipline as central tools to enforce loyalty across the party. Official reporting highlights a substantive campaign—including 73 corruption cases opened this year—framed as necessary for accountability and governance. External observers and commentators interpret these moves as efforts to consolidate Xi's authority and to shape upcoming leadership transitions, with some warning of purges or reshuffles. The closed-door tone of some sessions underlines the leadership's focus on unity and central control as it sets policy direction and personnel priorities.
