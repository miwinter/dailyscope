Archdiocese of New York Agrees to Major Abuse Settlement

New York Catholic Abuse Settlement 2026-10-25 • 3 articles au total

En Bref
  • The Archdiocese of New York agreed to pay about $800 million to settle historic clergy sexual abuse claims.
  • The settlement aims to compensate victims and end long-running lawsuits.
  • The deal is seen as progress for accountability but critics call for institutional reforms beyond money.

The Archdiocese of New York has reached a landmark agreement to pay approximately $800 million to resolve numerous historic clergy sexual abuse claims, reported as RM3.3 billion by one outlet. The settlement is intended to compensate hundreds of victims and to bring long-running lawsuits to closure. Media accounts frame the deal as a significant step toward accountability within the church and a catalyst for potential reforms and greater transparency. Victims' advocates welcomed the payment as meaningful progress, while critics urged additional structural changes beyond the financial settlement.
