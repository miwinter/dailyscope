Mass protests against Netanyahu ahead of elections

Israel Protests: Netanyahu 2026-10-25 • 8 articles au total

En Bref
  • Thousands protested in Tel Aviv against Prime Minister Netanyahu before the national election.
  • Protesters demanded a formal commission to investigate the October 7 events for accountability.
  • Opposition leaders joined the protests highlighting political unity without major clashes.

Thousands of Israelis gathered in Tel Aviv in the days before the national election to voice broad opposition to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his government. Demonstrations drew participants from across society and, in some reports, opposition leaders, signaling coordinated mobilization against Netanyahus policies. Several protests also specifically demanded a formal commission to investigate the October 7 events, framing accountability as a central pre-election issue. Police monitored crowds and no major clashes were widely reported, but commentators warned the protests could influence turnout and campaign dynamics amid heightened political polarization.
