Student movement topples Vučić in historic Serbian vote

Serbia Elections 2026-10-26 • 69 articles au total

En Bref
  • A student-led movement won Serbia’s snap parliamentary elections, ending Aleksandar Vučić’s long rule and sparking mass celebrations.
  • The election saw record voter turnout and some violence, raising legitimacy concerns despite Vučić’s concession.
  • The new government faces challenges in coalition-building, domestic reforms, and shifting Serbia’s foreign policy orientation.

A student-led movement achieved a decisive victory in Serbia’s parliamentary snap elections, prompting longtime leader Aleksandar Vučić to concede and triggering mass celebrations across Belgrade and other cities. Turnout was unusually high, with multiple reports describing record participation that observers say lent legitimacy to the result. While supporters hailed the vote as a generational and democratic turning point, election day also saw isolated incidents of violence and heavy security measures that underscored tensions during the transition. Media from many countries emphasize immediate challenges ahead: coalition-building, reform agendas, and how the new assembly will reposition Serbia’s foreign-policy orientation. Analysts warn the incoming leadership faces steep domestic and international tests as it translates protest energy into governable policy.
