Brazil extends polling hours amid logistical disruption and legal controversy

Brazil Polling Hours Extension 2026-10-26 • 9 articles au total

En Bref
  • Brazil extended polling hours due to rain and transport problems, authorized by authorities to ensure voters could cast ballots.
  • The extension caused confusion, delays in vote counting, and legal disputes with some critics warning of annulment risks.
  • The events revealed tensions between voter access and maintaining clear, timely election procedures amid political disputes.

Brazil extended polling hours by two hours in several locations after disruptions from rain, bus shortages and other transport problems; a Supreme Court minister and electoral authorities authorized closing at 19:00 to ensure voters could cast ballots. Officials and campaigns requested the move, while President Lula accused deliberate obstructions of public transport for some of the disruptions. The extension prompted confusion and delays in tallying, with counts paused and later restarted under judicial oversight in some states. Critics and international observers flagged legal and procedural uncertainty—some outlets warned of chaos and even raised the possibility of annulment—while the head of the Electoral Court criticized the extension yet acknowledged Lula’s victory. The events highlighted tensions between guaranteeing voter access and maintaining timely, uncontested election administration.
