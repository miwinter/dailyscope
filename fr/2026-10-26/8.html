Runoff in Bulgaria amid low turnout, security and East–West stakes

Bulgaria Presidential Runoff 2026-10-26 • 15 articles au total

En Bref
  • Bulgaria's presidential election requires a runoff due to record-low turnout and no clear winner.
  • The vote is framed by rising Black Sea security issues and a key choice between Western alignment and pro-Russian stance.
  • The runoff's outcome will impact Bulgaria's EU and NATO relations and the next president's domestic legitimacy.

Bulgaria's presidential contest has moved to a runoff after record-low turnout left no candidate with a clear mandate. The campaign is unfolding against heightened Black Sea security concerns, with recent drone strikes pushing defence and regional stability onto the front pages. Polls and exit projections show a narrow lead for incumbent Iliana Iotova while a pro‑Russian challenger has made notable early gains, framing the vote as a test of Bulgaria's East–West alignment. Analysts warn the runoff outcome will influence Sofia's relations with the EU and NATO and determine the domestic legitimacy of the next head of state.
