Escalation, deep strikes and diplomatic fallout in the Russo‑Ukrainian war

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-16 • 78 articles in total

In brief
  • Ukraine and Russia continue intense cross-border attacks, with drone swarms hitting Moscow and missile strikes targeting Ukrainian cities.
  • NATO and allies respond to airspace breaches while Russia deepens military ties with North Korea and pursues covert operations in neighboring states.
  • The conflict causes rising civilian casualties, infrastructure damage, and global geopolitical tensions including regional reactions and Kremlin instability.

Reporting across outlets shows a sharp escalation in cross‑border attacks and drone swarms striking Moscow and Russian infrastructure while Russia continues heavy missile barrages against Ukrainian cities. Kyiv is pressing Western partners for more air‑defence and strike capabilities even as reporting highlights use of Western drones and growing long‑range Ukrainian operations inside Russia. NATO and allied air policing have been tested by multiple airspace breaches and interdictions, and Warsaw and other capitals report covert threats linked to Moscow. Parallel developments include deeper Russia–North Korea military ties, allegations of Russian subversion in neighbouring states, and repeated warnings from humanitarian agencies about rising civilian casualties and food‑security risks.

Moscow's official stance

Russian government spokespeople and ministries frame Western and Turkish transfers of arms to Ukraine as destabilising and demand transparency or retaliation. Moscow also rejects limited ceasefire proposals and presents diplomatic messages that seek to justify continued operations and push back against Western involvement.
