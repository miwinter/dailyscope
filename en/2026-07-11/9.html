Israel alerts US to alleged Iran plot against Trump, prompting tighter coordination

US-Israel Security Briefing 2026-07-11 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • Israeli intelligence warned the U.S. about an alleged Iranian plot to assassinate President Trump, though details remain limited.
  • The reports highlight strong intelligence-sharing and increased security cooperation between Washington and Jerusalem amid regional tensions.
  • The alleged plot targeted a NATO summit, raising concerns about international event security and NATO's protective measures.

Multiple international reports say Israeli intelligence warned the United States about an alleged Iranian plot to assassinate President Donald Trump, though details and public confirmations remain limited. The disclosures, repeated across outlets, emphasize intelligence-sharing between Washington and Jerusalem and underline elevated tensions with Iran. One report attributes the claim to Mossad and alleges the plan targeted a NATO summit, raising wider security concerns for international gatherings. In response, officials are said to have increased coordination and planning, reflecting precautionary measures despite unanswered questions about credibility and timing.
