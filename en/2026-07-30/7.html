Russian influence, propaganda and hybrid threats across Europe and beyond

Russia Influence Campaigns 2026-07-30 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • Moscow claims Western opposition is unified but ineffective, promoting resilience and tougher stances.
  • Russian influence operations include hybrid threats, espionage, and global recruitment exploiting vulnerable youth.
  • Western and regional actors respond with expulsions, vigilance, and defensive measures against Kremlin-backed propaganda.

Across these reports Moscow projects a defiant narrative: senior officials frame the West as united against Russia and portray external pressure as failing to subdue Moscow. Parallel information flows include unverified accusations from defectors and social‑media reactions that shape public perceptions inside and outside Russia. European security services report hybrid threats and espionage incidents tied to Russian influence operations, while Western countries take actions against Kremlin‑aligned propagandists. Outside Europe, concerns surface about Russian recruitment networks targeting vulnerable youth, illustrating the global reach of Moscow's influence efforts.
