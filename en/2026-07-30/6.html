Arrest Warrant Issued for Evo Morales

Bolivia: Morales Arrest Warrant 2026-07-30 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • Bolivia's prosecutor issued an arrest warrant for former president Evo Morales over protests, charging him with terrorism and armed insurrection.
  • Morales denies the charges and calls them politically motivated amid ongoing national and regional political tensions.
  • International media frame the case as a significant legal escalation with regional implications, while unrelated coverage mentions Russia's warrant for Telegram's founder.

Bolivia's public prosecutor has issued an arrest warrant for former president Evo Morales linked to widespread May–June protests and roadblocks, with authorities accusing him of crimes including terrorism, armed insurrection and criminal association. Domestic reports highlight that the demonstrations paralysed parts of the country and say the move reflects deepening political tensions; Morales denies the allegations and calls the actions politically motivated. International outlets frame the development as a major legal escalation with potential regional implications and emphasize the formal charges such as "armed uprising" and "terrorism." Coverage collectively signals heightened polarization in Bolivia's post-presidential politics while raising questions about legal process and political intent. One included article is unrelated to Bolivia and instead reports on Russia issuing a warrant for the Telegram founder, reflecting a broader international context of high-profile legal actions against prominent figures.
