Morocco names first female prime minister

Morocco: First Female PM 2026-09-30 • 21 articles in total

In brief
  • Fatima-Zahra El Mansouri became Morocco's first female head of government, marking a historic milestone for women's leadership.
  • King Mohammed VI appointed her, highlighting the monarchy's key role in selecting the government.
  • She faces challenges in coalition-building, restoring trust, and leading economic reforms after recent elections.

King Mohammed VI has appointed Fatima-Zahra (Fatima Ezzahra) El Mansouri as Morocco’s first female head of government, a development widely reported as a historic milestone for gender representation. Coverage highlights both the symbolic breakthrough for women’s leadership and the formal role of the monarchy in selecting the head of government. Many outlets note the immediate political context — a recent election and the need to form a coalition — and cite analysts who flag challenges such as coalition-building, restoring public trust, and economic reforms. International observers differ mainly in emphasis, but most frame the appointment as a potentially consequential step for Morocco’s domestic governance and regional profile.
