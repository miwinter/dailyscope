Le Pen–Bardella antisemitism dispute escalates

France: Le Pen Bardella Antisemitism Lawsuit 2026-09-30 • 6 articles in total

In brief
  • Jordan Bardella sued to deny antisemitism allegations, causing internal conflict in Marine Le Pen's far-right movement.
  • The controversy has sparked international debate and affected the party's electoral and institutional standing.
  • Marine Le Pen pledged to counterattack, highlighting tensions and competing views on free speech and extremism.

Jordan Bardella has launched legal action denying allegations of antisemitic writings, a move that has intensified internal tensions within Marine Le Pen’s far-right movement and prompted a pledged counterattack from Le Pen. Media accounts highlight a damaging quoted passage attributed to Bardella that has overshadowed the presidential campaign and sparked debate over discrimination and political rhetoric. International reactions range from defenses by foreign officials to broader concern about the party’s growing institutional influence, including the formation of a Senate bloc. The controversy raises questions about electoral prospects, party accountability, and the balance between free expression and the fight against extremism.
