Widespread student protests disrupt French schools

France Student Protests 2026-09-30 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • Mass protests by French high school students caused closures of hundreds of schools and clashes with police across multiple cities.
  • Police used tear gas and batons, making about 400 arrests, many minors, amid injuries to school staff during confrontations.
  • The unrest reflects broader public-sector strikes and adds political pressure on President Macron, disrupting education and civic order nationwide.

Mass demonstrations by French high school students have forced the closure of hundreds of schools across multiple cities, with blockades and clashes spreading from the Paris region to other areas. Police used crowd-control measures including tear gas and batons, and authorities reported around 400–440 arrests, many of those detained being minors. Reports also document injuries to school staff amid confrontations and highlight protester grievances over class sizes, teacher shortages and school conditions. Observers link the student unrest to broader public-sector discontent and strikes, creating added political pressure on President Macron and his reform agenda. The situation reflects a nationwide escalation of social tensions with significant disruption to education and civic order.
