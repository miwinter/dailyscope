In brief

World leaders engaged in a flurry of bilateral and multilateral diplomacy this week, from a high-profile U.S.–China summit that produced AI safety channels and symbolic gestures to a wide set of regional meetings addressing security, resource disputes and cooperation. Across regions, leaders combined cultural outreach, state visits, and development cooperation with firm stances on national interests, illustrating how diplomacy mixes confidence‑building with contestation over strategic priorities.
