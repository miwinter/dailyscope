France in International News

Summary of articles about France in the international press on 2026-09-30, based on 43 articles collected.

In brief
  • France faces widespread social unrest with student protests, public sector strikes, school closures, and clashes with police.
  • Political controversies include antisemitism allegations against far-right leaders affecting elections, Eiffel Tower director's resignation over gender exclusion, and Catholic Church abuse issues highlighted by Pope's visit.
  • Legal and diplomatic challenges involve investigations into the Gabonese Bongo family, political trials of former officials, and various international cooperation efforts.

France is currently experiencing significant social unrest marked by widespread student protests and public sector strikes, leading to school closures, numerous arrests, and clashes with police. Political tensions are heightened by antisemitism allegations affecting far-right figures, notably Marine Le Pen and her ally Jordan Bardella, complicating the upcoming elections. Institutional challenges also surfaced with the resignation of the Eiffel Tower director following a scandal involving the exclusion of female staff, while the Catholic Church faces scrutiny over systemic abuse issues highlighted by Pope Leo's recent visit. Additionally, legal and diplomatic issues persist, including asset recovery cases involving the Gabonese Bongo family and ongoing judicial proceedings against former political figures.
