Zambia 2026: Tight Race, Economic Stakes and Counting Under Way

Zambia Election Watch 2026-08-14 • 10 articles in total

In brief
  • Zambia's 2026 election is a close race centered on President Hichilema's economic record and slow vote counting causes uncertainty.
  • Non-traditional candidates are gaining seats amid a crowded field, with debates on gender representation and coalition strategies.
  • Polling was calm with high turnout and orderly conduct, reflecting a credible election process.

Zambia’s 2026 general election is shaping up as a tight, high-stakes contest with incumbent President Hakainde Hichilema seeking a second term against a strong challenger. Vote counting has begun but slow tallies and localized delays are creating uncertainty around early results. Many reports frame the ballot as a referendum on Hichilema’s economic record—debt restructuring, IMF engagement, growth and cost-of-living pressures are central voter concerns. At the same time the campaign shows signs of change with non-traditional candidates winning seats, a crowded field and debates over gender representation, while observers report generally calm polling and strong turnout.
