NATO downs unidentified drone over Latvia, prompting regional alerts

NATO Baltic Airspace Violation 2026-08-14 • 17 articles in total

In brief
  • NATO and allied jets shot down an unidentified drone in Latvian airspace showing readiness and deterrence without casualties.
  • Latvia and Finland implemented temporary air and maritime alerts to ensure regional safety amid the incident.
  • Italian Eurofighter units were credited for the intercept and received thanks from Riga, highlighting allied cooperation in Baltic air defence.

NATO and allied fighter jets intercepted and shot down an unidentified drone that entered Latvian airspace, according to multiple reports. Latvia issued and later rescinded air-threat or air-raid alerts while neighboring states, notably Finland, imposed temporary air and maritime restrictions in affected areas. Some outlets specifically credited Italian Eurofighter forces for the intercept and noted public thanks from Riga, while other reports framed the event as routine NATO defensive action and deterrence. The drone's origin and intent remain unclear, no casualties were reported, and the incident has sharpened vigilance across the Baltic amid wider regional tensions linked to the Russia-Ukraine conflict.
