Security Controversies, Policy Shifts and Legal Battles Around Trump

US: Trump Presidency 2026-08-14 • 50 articles in total

In brief
  • Security controversies surround alleged plots against Trump and secret operations after a NATO summit, raising doubts about intelligence and protective protocols.
  • Trump's policies include tariffs on drones, reverting aircraft carriers to steam catapults, changes to infant vaccination, and legal setbacks affecting his political standing.
  • Domestic and international critiques focus on legal challenges, staff turnover, privatized diplomacy, and turbulent messaging impacting Trump's reputation and governance.

International coverage centers on a series of security and intelligence controversies: unverified warnings about plots targeting Donald Trump, secret aircraft swaps and dramatic exfiltration reports after a NATO summit that have provoked scrutiny of U.S. protective protocols. At the same time, policy moves — from steep tariffs on imported drones to orders to revert aircraft carriers to steam catapults and changes to infant vaccination guidance — signal an assertive and sometimes unilateral policy posture. Courts and legal challenges have produced notable setbacks for the administration, including dismissal of the Harvard antisemitism suit, while domestic governance questions (visa revocations, lawsuits over social media commerce, debates over term limits and capital punishment) continue to shape his political standing. Media clashes, staff turnover and critiques of privatized diplomacy further complicate messaging and international reception, leaving allies and rivals to weigh operational, legal and reputational consequences.
