Escalation of strikes, deep strikes and allied alarm

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-14 • 59 articles in total

In brief
  • The Russo‑Ukrainian war has intensified with frequent drone and missile strikes targeting ports, infrastructure, and inland sites on both sides.
  • Ukraine seeks enhanced Western air-defence support amid expanding deep-strike operations and rising civilian casualties documented internationally.
  • NATO and regional allies report increased airspace violations, tighten security, and express concern over widened military support for Russia, including from North Korea.

The coverage shows a marked intensification of the Russo‑Ukrainian war with frequent drone and missile strikes striking ports, refineries and inland targets on both sides, and Ukraine expanding deep‑strike operations. Ukraine repeatedly requests enhanced Western air‑defence capabilities, notably Patriot systems, while Russia publicly vows to destroy Western supplies and accuses the West of hostile acts. NATO and regional allies report increased airspace incursions and shootdowns, and Baltic and border states are tightening security amid hybrid‑threat warnings. International concern also centers on widening support networks for Moscow, including reported North Korean assistance, and mounting civilian casualties documented by the UN and multiple sources.
