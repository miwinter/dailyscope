Reports indicate Nvidia’s founder and CEO Jensen Huang has stepped down amid revelations about extramarital relationships and alleged links to espionage, triggering intense scrutiny of corporate governance. Nvidia’s board moved quickly, naming CFO Colette Kress as interim leader while discussions about ousting and succession continued. Markets reacted sharply, with shares and regional indexes showing volatility as investors reassess risk to the company’s AI roadmap. The episode has drawn political attention and broader industry concern about the future direction of a key AI hardware provider and the oversight of major tech firms.
