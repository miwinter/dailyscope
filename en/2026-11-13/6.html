Washington DC shooting near White House

Washington DC Shooting 2026-11-13 • 24 articles in total

In brief
  • A shooting near the White House and a local gym left one dead, four wounded; suspect, a physician involved in housing dispute, died by suicide.
  • Investigators review footage, interview witnesses, and collect evidence to clarify motive and event sequence amid conflicting early casualty reports.
  • Attack raised security near presidential complex and fueled concerns about gun violence in Washington, D.C.

A shooting in downtown Washington, D.C., near the White House and at a local gym left one person dead and four others wounded, triggering a large police response. Authorities say the suspected shooter later died by suicide and has been identified amid reports he was a physician involved in a long-running housing dispute. Investigators are reviewing surveillance footage, interviewing witnesses and collecting evidence to determine motive and the sequence of events. The attack prompted heightened security around the presidential complex and renewed concern about gun violence in the capital. Some outlets reported differing casualty counts in the immediate aftermath as officials continued to clarify details.
