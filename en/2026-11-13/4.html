Civilian toll in Gaza

Israel Gaza Casualties 2026-11-13 • 27 articles in total

In brief
  • Repeated airstrikes on Gaza hospitals kill patients, medical staff, and children, severely disrupting essential health services.
  • UN inquiry alleges deliberate targeting of children, prompting calls for war crimes investigations and accountability.
  • Blockades, bad weather, and ongoing clashes worsen shelter and medical shortages; UN urges unrestricted humanitarian aid access.

Reporting documents a heavy civilian toll in Gaza, with repeated strikes on hospitals killing patients, medical staff and children while undermining essential health services. UN officials and regional figures are urging unfettered humanitarian access as blockades, damaged logistics, heavy rains and the approaching winter exacerbate shelter and medical shortages. A UN inquiry and some sources allege deliberate targeting of children and record extremely high casualty figures, prompting calls for investigations and accountability. Ongoing clashes on the ground continue to produce casualties and hinder relief efforts.
