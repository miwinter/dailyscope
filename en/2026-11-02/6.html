Florida private jet crash kills nine

US Plane Crash: Florida 2026-11-02 • 40 articles in total

In brief
  • A Learjet 60 crashed near Mar‑a‑Lago, Florida, killing all nine onboard and sparking investigations into the cause.
  • Videos and crew audio capture frantic final moments, increasing public and media interest, especially due to proximity to Trump's residence.
  • Investigators are examining flight data and maintenance records; no immediate ground injuries reported but safety near residential areas is questioned.

A private Learjet 60 crashed in Palm Beach County, Florida, near the Mar‑a‑Lago area, killing all nine people on board. Multiple videos and released audio of the final crew communications circulated online, showing frantic moments before impact and intensifying public attention. Federal and local investigators have secured the scene and are reviewing flight data, maintenance records and witness accounts to determine probable causes. The crash has raised safety concerns about private aviation operations near residential and high‑profile areas while families and officials await further findings.
