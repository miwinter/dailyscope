US–Iran Strikes Watch

US–Iran Strikes Watch 2026-11-02 • 29 articles in total

In brief
  • US signals possible strikes on Iran mix threats and caution amid midterm politics and Israeli calls for swift action.
  • Iran vows open-ended retaliation, mobilizes defenses, raising risk of wider regional escalation.
  • US and allies reposition forces, manage damaged bases, and engage in diplomacy amid heightened tensions and uncertainty.

Washington’s signals of possible strikes on Iran have combined forceful rhetoric with caution, producing mixed messages ahead of U.S. midterm politics. Israeli leaders are reported to be pressing for swift action, while Tehran vows open-ended retaliation and is mobilizing defenses. At the same time the U.S. and allies are repositioning forces, managing damaged bases, and debating costs and timing. Diplomatic channels and live media coverage underscore the fragility of deterrence and the high risk of escalation in the coming weeks.
