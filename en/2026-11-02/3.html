Escalation of attacks: strikes, drones and cyberwar

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-02 • 26 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian forces intensify multi-domain attacks, causing blackouts, civilian casualties, and infrastructure damage in Ukraine and Russia, escalating humanitarian needs.
  • Repeated local ceasefires collapse amidst ongoing frontline fighting, exposing civilians to risk and undermining protection systems via cyber disruptions.
  • International efforts highlight humanitarian impact, while analysts note Russia's heavy losses and uncertain future offensive capabilities.

Russian offensives continue to escalate across multiple domains — aerial, missile, drone and cyber — causing widespread damage to civilian infrastructure and challenging regional stability. Large-scale strikes have triggered blackouts across hundreds of cities, produced casualties in Kyiv and prompted mass drone barrages that deepen humanitarian needs. Attacks have also hit energy infrastructure inside Russia, while satellite imagery and reports suggest expanding drone capabilities and launch sites. Local ceasefires in frontline areas have repeatedly collapsed, leaving civilians exposed as cyber disruptions further undermine air-raid warning systems and civil protection.

Strikes and power outages across Ukraine

Russian missile and drone strikes have repeatedly targeted Ukrainian power and civilian infrastructure, producing widespread blackouts, casualties and complicating repair efforts. Large drone barrages and targeting of cities such as Kyiv and Chernivtsi underline the operational intensity and humanitarian impact of the campaign.

Drone and refinery attacks on energy infrastructure

Fragile ceasefires and frontline civilian preparedness

Local truces in areas such as Kherson and Oleshky have repeatedly ended, forcing civilians to prepare for renewed fighting and underscoring the fragility of pauses in hostilities. Ongoing frontline clashes and routine war reporting reflect the persistent risk to residents and the difficulty of securing lasting humanitarian pauses.

Cyber warfare targeting warning systems

Cyberattacks have targeted air-raid warning and emergency apps in both Ukraine and Russia, disrupting civilian protection measures at critical moments. Attribution remains uncertain, but the incidents illustrate how cyberspace has become an active front that can directly endanger civilians during kinetic attacks.
