Escalation, foreign‑involvement claims and regional alarm over the Russo‑Ukrainian war

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-09-29 • 87 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian forces escalate conflict with drone and missile strikes causing civilian and infrastructure damage in Ukraine.
  • Neighbouring countries face regional security threats from cross-border drone incidents and raise military alerts.
  • International bodies call for stronger defense and support for Ukraine amid information warfare and diplomatic challenges.

Reporting across multiple countries shows an intensification of the conflict: heavy drone and missile strikes have hit Kyiv and other Ukrainian targets, causing civilian casualties and damage to scientific and medical infrastructure. Kyiv and several international outlets allege Russian mobilization measures and growing military ties with North Korea and Iran, including claims of North Korean troop deployments and increased Russian defense spending. Neighbouring states have reported cross‑border drone incidents, maritime drone concerns and raised military alerts, prompting NATO and EU calls to bolster deterrence and coordinate responses. The coverage also highlights information‑war elements — bounties, disinformation and foiled intelligence operations — alongside humanitarian stories such as abducted children and returns, underlining both the military and human dimensions of the crisis.
