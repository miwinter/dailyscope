Widening Bilateral Web: Security, Investment and Climate Deals

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-09-02 • 83 articles in total

In brief
  • Bilateral diplomacy advances with deals on security, investment, climate, and trade across regions, involving defense cooperation, economic ties, and development finance.
  • Diplomatic tensions surface in Europe and Russia over Ukraine relations, while strategic partnerships deepen in Asia, Africa, and the Middle East through high-level visits and agreements.
  • Countries diversify partnerships for trade, security, and development, with significant roles for China, Gulf states, the EU, and the US in global diplomatic and economic engagements.

This set of reports shows an active phase of bilateral diplomacy across regions, with states concluding deals and reaffirming commitments on security, investment, infrastructure, and climate cooperation. Several partnerships emphasize defense and security cooperation — from regional pacts and aircraft deployments to capacity-building in policing and counterterrorism — while others focus on economic ties, credit lines, energy agreements, and large-scale investments. Climate and development cooperation recur in Asia and Africa through REDD+, net-zero partnerships, infrastructure lending, and reconstruction financing. High-level visits and ambassadorial appointments underline the diplomatic momentum, even as some agreements and projects provoke controversy or implementation questions.
