Multiple reports document a deadly heatwave in France during June and July, with over 2,000 to 3,700 excess deaths attributed to extreme temperatures. The heatwave has also led to widespread wildfires in southern France, causing evacuations and significant damage. Additionally, French law enforcement collaborates with Monaco on terrorism-related investigations, highlighting cross-border security coordination. France also condemns terrorism acts abroad, such as the Damascus bombing, reaffirming its commitment to counter-terrorism and regional stability.
