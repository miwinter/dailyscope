Monaco parcel bombing investigation

Monaco Bombing Investigation 2026-07-04 • 10 articles in total

In brief
  • Monaco prosecutors identified a Ukrainian woman disguised as a man as the main suspect in the parcel bombing.
  • International agencies conduct a manhunt and search in Germany for the suspect with ongoing judicial investigation.
  • The blast injured a Ukraine-born multi-millionaire and raised security concerns for high-profile Monaco residents.

Monaco prosecutors have identified a main suspect in the parcel bombing as a Ukrainian woman who allegedly disguised herself as a man. Some reports name Anastasiia Berezovska and note searches of a German residence while Interpol and other international agencies assist an active manhunt. The blast injured a Ukraine-born multi-millionaire and two others, prompting heightened scrutiny of security for high-profile residents. Authorities say the judicial investigation is ongoing, possible accomplices and motives are being explored, and public assistance may be requested.
