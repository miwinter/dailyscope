Keiko Fujimori Officially Proclaimed President-Elect

Peru: President-Elect Proclamation 2026-07-04 • 10 articles in total

In brief
  • Peru's National Electoral Jury certified Keiko Fujimori as president-elect and started the official transition.
  • Fujimori's narrow victory reflects deep political polarization and raises concerns about democratic stability.
  • Her team has appointed a technical lead to coordinate the governmental handover.

Peru's National Electoral Jury has formally proclaimed Keiko Fujimori as president-elect and certified the results, initiating the official transition process. Her team has begun organizing the handover, naming a technical lead to coordinate the transfer of power. The result was narrowly decided and has prompted celebrations among supporters and criticism and concern from opponents, reflecting deep political polarization. International coverage situates the outcome within broader regional shifts to the right and raises questions about democratic stability given Fujimori's family legacy and the contentious nature of the vote.
