Trump Scales Back US–South Korea Military Drills

South Korea-US Military Drills 2026-08-17 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • President Trump ordered a major cut in U.S.-South Korea military drills to ease tensions with North Korea and expressed grievances with Seoul.
  • Experts warned the decision could weaken deterrence and strain U.S.-South Korea alliance coordination.
  • South Korea reacted promptly with reviews while some exercises continued amid regional concern over stability.

President Donald Trump ordered a substantial reduction in joint U.S.-South Korea military exercises, citing his rapport with North Korean leader Kim Jong Un and a desire to lower provocative signaling. Several reports also frame the decision as driven by grievances with Seoul—pointing to its stance on Iran, perceived lack of cooperation, or the cost and scale of drills. Analysts and allied governments warned the move could undermine regional deterrence, complicate readiness, and shift alliance signaling. South Korea responded quickly and reviews were launched even as some exercises proceeded, while regional partners expressed concern about implications for stability on the peninsula.
