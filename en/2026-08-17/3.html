Escalation of strikes, regional security alarms and political strains

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-17 • 98 articles in total

In brief
  • Ukraine and Russia conduct intensified cross-border missile and drone strikes causing heavy civilian and military casualties.
  • NATO and neighbors warn of Russian espionage, secret drone bases, and assassination plots risking regional destabilization.
  • Domestic strain in Russia and sustained international military support shape the conflict's political and strategic dynamics.

Recent reporting shows a sharp intensification of cross‑border operations: Ukraine has conducted large drone and missile strikes deep into Russian territory while Russia continues attacks on Ukrainian ports, cities and infrastructure. Coverage emphasizes heavy civilian and military tolls, contested casualty figures and investigative efforts to account for losses on both sides. NATO members and regional neighbours warn about clandestine Russian drone bases, espionage and alleged assassination plots that heighten fears of spillover and destabilisation. At the same time, signs of domestic strain in Russia—dismissals of outspoken economists, unrest in political corridors and debate about Putin’s future—appear alongside sustained international military support for Ukraine.
