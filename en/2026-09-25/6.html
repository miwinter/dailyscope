Global backlash and protests met by Netanyahu's defiance

Israel Netanyahu Boycott 2026-09-25 • 18 articles in total

In brief
  • Dozens of delegations walked out during Netanyahu's UN address, signaling diplomatic rejection and growing international strain.
  • Large street protests and high-profile arrests in New York showed widespread public opposition to Netanyahu's policies.
  • Some countries warn of long-term diplomatic costs, while a few commentators defend Netanyahu's firm security stance.

At the UN General Assembly, dozens of delegations walked out during Benjamin Netanyahu’s address, a visible diplomatic rebuke that many outlets framed as a sign of eroding international support. Netanyahu met the walkouts with defiant rhetoric — calling critics “moral cowards” — and sought to defend Israel’s security posture. Outside the UN and in New York, large street protests and high-profile arrests, including celebrity participants, underscored broad public mobilization against his policies. State-level pushback and analytical coverage from several countries highlighted long-term diplomatic costs even as a few commentators insisted he would not relent.
