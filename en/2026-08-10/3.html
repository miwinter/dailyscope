Fractured Consensus on Gaza Plan amid Wide-ranging Diplomatic Activity

World Leaders Dialogue 2026-08-10 • 108 articles in total

In brief
  • Israel rejects the U.S.-backed Gaza roadmap, demanding Hamas disarmament before any concessions.
  • Palestinian actors, the U.S., and regional mediators support or conditionally accept the roadmap, focusing on ceasefire and humanitarian access.
  • Diplomatic activity spans beyond Gaza, covering Kosovo disputes, ASEAN-Myanmar relations, cultural diplomacy, security, and development aid debates.

Global diplomatic coverage is dominated by sharp divisions over a U.S.-backed Gaza roadmap: Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has publicly rejected the plan in numerous reports, insisting on Hamas disarmament as a precondition for any withdrawal or concessions. Palestinian actors and some regional voices signal support or conditional acceptance of aspects of the roadmap, deepening the diplomatic split. The United States and allied advocates press the proposal as the best path to a ceasefire and stability, while Egypt, Turkey and other regional mediators emphasize immediate humanitarian access and full implementation of agreed provisions. Beyond the Gaza row, leaders pursued a broad set of bilateral and multilateral dialogues on cultural diplomacy, Kosovo recognition, ASEAN–Myanmar relations, UN leadership bids, development aid cuts and nuclear disarmament concerns.
