Aircraft Intercepted Near Bedminster Golf Club

Bedminster Intercept 2026-08-10 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • US fighter jets intercepted two aircraft near President Trump's Bedminster golf club enforcing a temporary flight restriction with no injuries reported.
  • Reports highlight the incident as routine airspace enforcement and also as politically sensitive due to high-profile figure protections.
  • Conflicting accounts mention video evidence of jets and disputed claims about EU fighter jet involvement, showing some reporting discrepancies.

Across multiple international reports, US fighter jets (including F-16s) were scrambled to intercept two aircraft that entered a temporary restricted airspace near President Donald Trump's Bedminster, New Jersey golf club. NORAD and local authorities escorted the aircraft out of the zone, reported no injuries or damage, and launched investigations into the incursions. Most outlets frame the event as routine enforcement of temporary flight restrictions and air-defense vigilance, while some emphasize political implications and heightened scrutiny of airspace around high-profile figures. A few sources add alternative details—video evidence of jets taking off or contested claims that EU fighters were involved—highlighting minor discrepancies as authorities review flight records.
