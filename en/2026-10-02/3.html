Bilateral deals: a mixed picture of cooperation, investment and diplomatic strain

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-10-02 • 79 articles in total

In brief
  • Governments and businesses worldwide expanded bilateral deals covering trade, infrastructure, energy, security, and capacity building.
  • Diplomatic tensions coexist with cooperation, including embassy closures, defensive military upgrades, and regional training initiatives.
  • Multiple countries pursue targeted economic partnerships, development projects, and strategic alliances amid shifting geopolitical challenges.

Across regions, governments and businesses advanced a wide range of bilateral deals focused on trade, infrastructure, energy, security and capacity building. Several countries announced investment, loan or cooperation frameworks—from airport and port projects to energy loans and trade working groups—highlighting a push to deepen economic ties and connectivity. Parallel to constructive engagements, notable diplomatic ruptures and tensions emerged, especially in the Horn of Africa where expulsions and embassy closures mark a sharp deterioration. Training, technical assistance and defense deliveries also featured prominently, underscoring that bilateral ties now span commercial, developmental and strategic domains.
