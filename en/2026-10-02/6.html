Widespread student unrest across France turns violent and disrupts schooling

France Education Protests 2026-10-02 • 19 articles in total

In brief
  • Nationwide student protests in France have closed hundreds of schools and spread to university campuses.
  • Clashes between pupils and police have caused injuries, nearly two thousand arrests, and damage including arson.
  • The unrest reflects grievances over education issues and broader political dissent, pressuring government responses.

Nationwide student protests in France have rapidly expanded, closing hundreds of schools and spreading onto university campuses. Demonstrations have frequently turned confrontational, with clashes between pupils and police resulting in many injuries, nearly two thousand arrests, and reports of arson and damaged school property. The unrest highlights deep grievances over education funding, teacher shortages and dilapidated facilities, while some youth leaders and opposition figures frame the movement more broadly as social and political dissent. Government officials have faced pressure to respond to safety concerns and policy demands even as authorities emphasize maintaining public order.
