Attempted in‑flight attack on Flydubai flight foiled

Flydubai Diversion Incident 2026-10-02 • 60 articles in total

In brief
  • A Flydubai flight was disrupted by a co-pilot who stabbed the captain and tried to crash the plane, but passengers and crew restrained him and diverted to Saudi Arabia.
  • Multiple countries launched investigations into the incident's motive and potential extremist links while political leaders expressed strong reactions.
  • Coverage varies by region: Israel stresses security risks and radicalization, Gulf states focus on law enforcement and probes, India highlights the captain's heroism, the US discusses geopolitical implications, and many report eyewitness accounts and forensic analyses.

A Flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv was disrupted when the co‑pilot allegedly stabbed the captain and attempted to crash the aircraft; passengers and crew restrained the attacker and the plane made an emergency diversion to Saudi Arabia. The injured Indian captain, widely praised as a hero, and several passengers who intervened are being recognized for averting catastrophe. Authorities from the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Israel and other countries have launched coordinated investigations to determine motive and whether extremist links or other factors were involved. Political leaders and commentators have reacted strongly—some invoking radicalization or regional actors—while investigators and aviation experts continue to review flight data, footage and testimony.
