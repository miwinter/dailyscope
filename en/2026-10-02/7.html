China's assertive posture and global responses

China Geopolitics Watch 2026-10-02 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Beijing focuses on national rejuvenation, reunification, military readiness, and stronger security under Xi's leadership.
  • Western officials see a low Taiwan invasion risk before 2028 and doubt the EU's ability to effectively counter China.
  • Independent analysts warn China's assertiveness threatens regional autonomy and challenges established views on its rise.

Across these reports Beijing is projecting a mix of domestic consolidation and external deterrence: Xi emphasizes national rejuvenation and reunification while China schedules military drills and elevates security leadership. Western actors register that posture with caution—U.S. officials judge a Taiwan invasion unlikely before 2028, while EU analysts question Brussels’ ability to exert meaningful leverage. Independent commentators warn this assertiveness risks eroding regional autonomy and challenges prevailing narratives about China’s rise. Taken together, the pieces show a balance of Beijing’s confident signaling and persistent international unease about future trajectories.
