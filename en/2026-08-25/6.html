Deadly gang attacks deepen Haiti’s security and humanitarian crisis

Haiti Gang Violence 2026-08-25 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • Gang attacks near Port-au-Prince have killed around 40 people and caused widespread fear and displacement.
  • The violence disrupts daily life and essential services, damaging infrastructure on the capital's outskirts.
  • Authorities are investigating and responding amid calls for stronger protection and international aid.

A new wave of gang attacks near Port-au-Prince, including Kenscoff, has left around 40–42 people dead and caused widespread fear and displacement. The violence has disrupted daily life and access to essential services, damaging vehicles and infrastructure on the capital’s outskirts. Haitian authorities and security forces have launched investigations and operations while communities urge stronger protection. International and regional outlets emphasize both the immediate humanitarian needs and the broader governance and security failures that allow such attacks to persist. Calls for aid and stabilization measures are mounting as the death toll and insecurity continue to rise.
