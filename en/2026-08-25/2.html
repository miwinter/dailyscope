Global Bilateral Push: Investments, Security and Influence

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-08-25 • 97 articles in total

In brief
  • China expands influence in Africa, the Pacific, and Central Asia through infrastructure, media, and military cooperation.
  • Western countries strengthen defense and diplomatic efforts with Ukraine amid ongoing hostilities and symbolic prisoner exchanges.
  • Gulf, European, and Pacific states pursue economic diversification, investment partnerships, and development assistance amid regional tensions and strategic competition.

This set of reports shows an active phase of bilateral diplomacy worldwide, with states pursuing strategic investments, security partnerships and development assistance across regions. China is deepening ties in Africa, the Pacific and Central Asia through infrastructure, media and military engagement, while Gulf states and Europe sign high-profile cultural and investment pacts. Western countries are intensifying defense cooperation with Ukraine even as prisoner exchanges and diplomatic de-escalation steps occur between Moscow and Kyiv. Trade frictions—especially between the United States and Canada—sit alongside numerous economic agreements and development deals in Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Pacific, highlighting competing influences and pragmatic cooperation.
