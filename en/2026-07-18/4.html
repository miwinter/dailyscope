Leaders navigating security, rivalry and multilateral engagement

World Leaders Dialogue 2026-07-18 • 71 articles in total

In brief
  • World leaders use security coalitions, bilateral ties, and multilateral efforts to manage geopolitical competition and regional crises.
  • The US leads counter-extremism coalitions while China advances AI governance and regional influence through diplomacy.
  • Europe pursues strategic unity and balances Russia engagement amid policy debates on Ukraine and migration.

This set of reports shows world leaders pursuing a mix of security coalitions, bilateral statecraft and multilateral initiatives to manage rising geopolitical competition and regional crises. Washington is actively convening partners on counter-extremism and strengthening bilateral ties, while Beijing advances AI governance and deepens regional influence through diplomacy. Europe emphasizes strategic unity and cautious engagement with Russia even as EU institutions and member states debate policy on Ukraine and migration. Meanwhile, the Middle East remains a focus of normalization efforts, regional rivalry and humanitarian concerns, and smaller states press for development, constitutional and disaster-cooperation diplomacy while public backlash over unconventional 'superyacht' diplomacy highlights reputational risks.
