China's Soft Power Push: Governance, AI and Global Outreach

China Soft Power Diplomacy 2026-07-18 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • China promotes soft power through governance, cultural diplomacy, and global AI leadership under Xi Jinping.
  • International polls show rising favorable views of China linked to its diplomatic and economic outreach.
  • Western media criticizes China's motives and highlights strategic messaging alongside its soft-power efforts.

China is intensifying soft-power diplomacy through a mix of governance promotion, technical cooperation, and high-profile image projects. Events such as the multimedia promotion of the fifth volume of Xi Jinping: The Governance of China in Astana sit alongside training programs for ASEAN journalists, environmental showcases like Saihanba, and state-led AI conferences that cast Beijing as a standards-setter. International polls reported in multiple pieces show rising favorable views of China and Xi in many countries, a trend analysts link to diplomatic outreach, economic ties, and media presence. At the same time, Western commentary remains critical and some regional coverage highlights sharper strategic messaging from Beijing.

AI leadership and governance diplomacy

Chinese and international coverage frames Beijing as asserting leadership in global AI governance, promoting multilateral initiatives and standards under Xi's direction. These pieces present China as an active alternative to US models, courting the Global South with training, policy proposals, and high-profile conferences.

Cultural and development outreach

Host-country and partner media emphasize China's use of cultural diplomacy, capacity-building, and ecological projects to bolster its international image. Reports highlight promotional events for Xi's governance book, journalist training programs, and environmental showcases as practical expressions of Beijing's soft-power outreach.

  • China hosts ASEAN women journalists for AI and digital media training
  • Saihanba Mechanized Forestry Farm: The Chinese 'Green Miracle'
  • Astana hosts a promotional event for the fifth volume of Xi Jinping: The Governance of China

Improving global perceptions

Survey reports interpret rising favorable views of China and Xi as evidence that Beijing's diplomacy, economic influence and media presence are shifting international opinion. The coverage notes this trend varies by region but treats the polls as indicative of growing soft-power leverage.

  • China and Xi are seen more favorably than the US and Trump in many nations, survey shows
  • Pew Research Center poll: China surpasses the United States in global public opinion

Skeptical Western critique

Western commentary expresses mistrust of China's motives and actions, challenging positive narratives and urging scrutiny of Beijing's influence efforts. This perspective foregrounds critical analysis rather than descriptive reporting of Chinese outreach.

  • VG commentator: 'Throws China Under the Bus'

Assertive regional messaging

Some coverage highlights sharper strategic signaling from China, portraying certain actions and commentary as warnings that complement or complicate soft-power activities. This viewpoint frames Beijing's outreach alongside displays of military or geopolitical messaging in regional contexts.
