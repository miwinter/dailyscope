14-year-old opens fire at French school

France School Shooting 2026-10-13 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • A 14-year-old student brought a rifle into a French middle school hidden in a guitar case and wounded the principal and teachers.
  • A school staff member disarmed the student who was then arrested, triggering a police investigation into the motive, weapon source, and victims' conditions.
  • The incident sparked public debate and authorities' review of school security and minors' access to firearms.

A 14-year-old pupil opened fire at a middle school in France, wounding the principal/headmaster and one or more teachers. Multiple reports say the weapon—a hunting rifle or shotgun—was brought into school concealed in a guitar case, and a staff member intervened to disarm and restrain the student. Police arrested the minor and launched an investigation into the motive, the weapon's origin, and the victims' conditions. The incident has intensified scrutiny of school security measures and concerns about how minors obtain firearms, prompting reviews by authorities and public debate on youth violence.
