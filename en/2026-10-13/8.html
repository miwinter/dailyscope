Protests Target Election Commission as Security Tightens

India: Election Protests 2026-10-13 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • Protests in India challenge disputed voter list revisions and demand the election chief's resignation through marches and legal petitions.
  • Authorities respond with heavy police presence, public transport closures, traffic restrictions, and mass detentions.
  • The 'cockroaches' movement's leaders face arrests but vow to continue protests amid accusations of police heavy-handedness.

Widespread protests in India have escalated into repeated marches and demonstrations targeting the Chief Election Commissioner and disputed voter list revisions, drawing thousands into the streets of Delhi and other cities. Authorities have responded with large police deployments, metro station closures, traffic restrictions and mass detentions, prompting criticism from journalists and opposition leaders. Opposition parties are pursuing parallel legal strategies, filing petitions and marching to the Supreme Court to challenge electoral roll updates and demand the election chief's resignation. Leaders of the defiant 'cockroaches' movement have been arrested and released, vowing to continue protests despite repression. The coverage collectively highlights a contested pre-election environment marked by civic mobilisation, policing measures and judicial contestation.
