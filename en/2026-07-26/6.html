Berlin Pride Vehicle Attack: Death, Injuries and Manhunt

Germany Pride Vehicle Attack 2026-07-26 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • A vehicle attacked participants at a Berlin Pride event, killing one and injuring several, causing the demonstration to end early.
  • Police identified a suspect, launched a manhunt, and are investigating possible terror motives but have not confirmed them.
  • Reports mention the driver fled on foot and allegedly committed stabbings, raising security concerns at public gatherings.

A vehicle ploughed into participants at a Berlin Pride event, killing one person and injuring multiple others, forcing the demonstration to be cut short as emergency services responded. Police have launched a manhunt after identifying a suspect and are reviewing footage and witness accounts while urging the public to remain calm. Some outlets and officials have raised the possibility of a terror motive or Islamist links, though investigators have not confirmed motives and inquiries remain ongoing. At least one report describes the driver fleeing on foot and additional stabbing victims, heightening concerns about security at large public gatherings.
