France in International News

Summary of articles about France in the international press on 2026-10-20, based on 133 articles collected.

In brief
  • France faces widespread protests and violent unrest over economic issues and government policies.
  • The government responds with curfews, increased policing, and debates over emergency powers.
  • The unrest affects domestic politics, boosts extremist groups, and draws international attention including criticism from the US.

France is experiencing significant social unrest marked by widespread protests, violent clashes, and looting across multiple cities, primarily driven by grievances over the cost of living and government policies. Authorities have responded with curfews, increased police presence, and considerations of emergency measures, while President Macron calls for calm and accountability. The unrest has intensified political tensions ahead of upcoming elections, with far-right and far-left factions seeking to capitalize on the situation, and international reactions including commentary from US President Trump linking the unrest to immigration issues.
