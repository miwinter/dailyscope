Bilateral Deals: Diversified Partnerships, Security Pacts and Implementation Gaps

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-10-20 • 78 articles in total

In brief
  • China leads infrastructure and technology partnerships across Asia and Africa, shaping development and defense-related projects.
  • Central Asian states and others diversify partnerships with multiple global actors for economic, security, and diplomatic cooperation.
  • Bilateral relations mix practical agreements, symbolic summits, and occasional disputes with delays affecting delivery of announced projects.

Recent reporting shows a flurry of bilateral engagements across regions, from China’s push into infrastructure and technology partnerships to Western-led trade, connectivity and development initiatives. Security and defence cooperation is rising alongside diplomatic and energy diplomacy in the Gulf, with multiple states signing training, procurement and production agreements. Central Asian states are clearly diversifying partners—engaging with China, Iran, the US and Europe—while African and Pacific countries pursue both ceremonial reaffirmations and practical investment summits. At the same time, some high-profile projects and missions have been postponed or abandoned, exposing gaps between announcements and delivery.
