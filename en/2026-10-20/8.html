Midterm Forecast: Texas, Latino Voters and GOP Headwinds

US Midterm Forecast 2026-10-20 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • Texas is a crucial battleground with early voting that could decide control of Congress.
  • Latino voters are a key swing group influenced by immigration and can shift longstanding Republican areas.
  • Low Trump approval and high-profile GOP efforts create uncertainty about Republican prospects in the midterms.

Coverage ahead of the U.S. midterms highlights Texas as a decisive battleground where early voting and mobilization could shape control of Congress. Latino voters are portrayed as a key swing group, often disenchanted with both major parties and particularly sensitive to immigration policy. Polling that shows historically low approval for Donald Trump has analysts warning of Republican vulnerability, even as Trump rallies and high-profile donors try to energize the GOP. These competing dynamics create uncertainty: Democrats see realistic opportunities to flip seats while Republicans push elite backing and ground operations to avert losses.
