Latvia election: pro‑European lead, coalition talks ahead

Latvia Elections Coalition Talks 2026-10-04 • 21 articles in total

In brief
  • Pro-European and pro-Ukraine parties led by Andris Kulbergs’s United List are set to remain dominant but likely lack a clear majority.
  • Security concerns, Russian threats, and the Russian-speaking minority significantly influenced voter priorities and campaign focus.
  • High turnout and a large candidate field highlight a competitive election requiring coalition negotiations to form the government.

Early and exit polling shows the pro‑European, pro‑Ukraine United List and the prime minister Andris Kulbergs’s allies leading the parliamentary vote, with several outlets projecting the governing party as the largest force. Many reports stress that while the ruling camp performed strongly, it often falls short of an outright majority, leaving coalition negotiations inevitable. Security concerns — including drone incidents, Russian threats and the role of the Russian‑speaking minority — dominated the campaign and shaped voter priorities. Observers note a high turnout and a large candidate field, underlining a competitive contest whose final parliamentary composition and government will be decided through post‑election bargaining. Analysts expect the result to reaffirm Latvia’s pro‑EU/NATO stance but warn that coalition dynamics will determine concrete policy directions on defense and social issues.
