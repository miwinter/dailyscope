Albanian student protests demand Rama's resignation amid violence and blame

Albania Rama Resign Protests 2026-10-17 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • Student-led protests demanding Prime Minister Edi Rama's resignation have spread across Albania, occupying public spaces and involving mass marches.
  • Clashes between police and demonstrators have turned violent, with tear gas, water cannon, injuries, and fires reported in multiple cities.
  • The government blames foreign actors for the unrest and rejects resignation demands, while international coverage highlights political tensions and concerns over crowd-control methods.

Nationwide student-led demonstrations have erupted across Albania, with protesters demanding Prime Minister Edi Rama's resignation and occupying public spaces. Clashes between demonstrators and police have turned violent in Tirana and other cities, prompting the use of tear gas, water cannon and reports of fires and injuries. The government has rejected the demands and publicly blamed foreign actors and political opponents for stoking the unrest, framing the crisis as external interference. International coverage highlights both the domestic political stakes—including Rama's presence abroad—and wider concerns over crowd-control tactics and regional stability.
