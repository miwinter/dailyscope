US diplomacy under strain: tech controls, Trump–Zelensky rifts and global recalibration

US Diplomacy Watch 2026-10-17 • 65 articles in total

In brief
  • Allegations of White House restrictions on Starlink services raise concerns about private tech influence on Ukraine's defense.
  • Public conflicts between Trump and Zelensky strain US-Ukraine ties and prompt Europe to increase support for Kyiv.
  • US foreign policy faces challenges from contested diplomacy, regional criticisms, and domestic controversies affecting global influence.

Reporting this cycle highlights multiple strains on US foreign policy: allegations that the White House or allied actors curtailed Starlink/Starshield services for Ukraine have sparked debate about private tech influence and operational impacts on Kyiv’s defense. At the same time, repeated public confrontations between Donald Trump and President Zelensky — and associated talk of deals with Russia — have frayed US–Ukraine ties and pushed Europe to shore up support for Kyiv. Beyond Europe, Washington’s actions on travel, visas and health restrictions, new flight policies, and quiet diplomacy with contested regimes (from Myanmar to regional partners) signal a pragmatic but contested global recalibration. Latin American and Caribbean actors are publicly contesting US access and influence, while domestic political and intelligence controversies complicate the messaging and coherence of US diplomacy abroad.
