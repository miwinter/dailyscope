France braces for large protests amid unrest and political tension

France Protests Clashes 2026-10-17 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • France faces large weekend protests driven by cost-of-living anger, student unrest, and fears of Yellow Vest revival.
  • Authorities have increased security and prepared crowd-control measures to manage public order amid expected disruptions.
  • Government officials claim the radical left exploits the protests politically ahead of elections.

France is preparing for large nationwide demonstrations this weekend, driven largely by cost-of-living anger, student unrest and fears of a Yellow Vest revival. Authorities expect significant turnout in major cities and have stepped up security planning to limit disruption to transport and services and to protect public order. President Emmanuel Macron and other officials have urged calm as police prepare crowd-control measures, while some outlets warn of possible clashes and heavy-handed responses. The protests are also being framed politically, with government figures accusing the radical left of exploiting unrest ahead of upcoming elections.
