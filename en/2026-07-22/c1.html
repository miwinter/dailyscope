Social media ban for under-15s

France has introduced and approved a groundbreaking law banning social media access for users under 15 years old, marking the first such measure in the European Union. The policy is framed as a child protection initiative to shield minors from online risks, including exposure to harmful content and data collection. While supporters emphasize enhanced safety and digital regulation, critics raise concerns about enforcement feasibility, privacy implications, and potential impacts on freedom of expression. The law requires platforms to implement age verification and restrict access, with ongoing debates about practical implementation and effects on families and schools.

Wildfires and emergency incidents

France is currently facing severe wildfires in southern and southeastern regions, prompting large-scale evacuations and deployment of firefighting resources including water-bombing aircraft. These fires have caused significant disruption, including power outages and flight delays, and tragically resulted in the deaths of two volunteer firefighters near Bordeaux airport. The incidents highlight the challenges posed by extreme weather conditions and the strain on emergency services. Authorities continue to coordinate containment efforts and investigate causes to improve future responses.
