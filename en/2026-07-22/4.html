Shocks at home and on the front: leadership changes, strikes and wider diplomatic tensions

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-07-22 • 84 articles in total

In brief
  • President Zelensky reshuffled Ukraine's military leadership amid protests, marking a major political crisis.
  • Heavy Russian strikes on Ukrainian cities cause civilian casualties and infrastructure damage, while Ukraine attacks Russian territory and logistics.
  • Russia demonstrates military power via naval exercises and bomber flights, escalating regional tensions and threatening shipping routes amid diplomatic maneuvers.

Reports cluster around two immediate dynamics: a major political shock in Kyiv as President Zelensky dismissed and reshuffled senior military leaders amid street protests, and continuing high-intensity combat marked by large Russian missile and drone strikes on Ukrainian cities with significant civilian harm. Ukrainian forces have stepped up strikes inside Russian territory and against logistics hubs, while Moscow has signalled power abroad with long-range bomber patrols and naval live-fire exercises near Western waters. The Black Sea and regional shipping routes have become flashpoints after attacks on tankers and intensified naval operations, prompting allied support measures such as U.S. production of naval drones. Behind the fighting, diplomatic manoeuvres and allied rhetoric — from secret meetings to Kremlin ultimatums and outside backers’ declarations — underline the conflict’s wider geopolitical and economic costs.
