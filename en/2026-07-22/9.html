Indian youth protests over exams and jobs

India Youth Protests 2026-07-22 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • Youth protests in India demand jobs, end to exam fraud and education reforms amid structural problems and student suicides.
  • Authorities use tear gas, batons and detentions to disperse protests, raising civil liberties and political concerns.
  • The unrest highlights deep systemic issues in education and employment, prompting calls for government accountability and policy changes.

Youth-led protests have erupted across India following alleged exam frauds and long-standing unemployment, with students and graduates mobilising in major cities to demand jobs, educational integrity and reforms. Authorities repeatedly used tear gas, baton charges and detentions to disperse demonstrations near sites such as Jantar Mantar, escalating tensions. Reports link the unrest to deeper structural problems — exam leaks, inadequate job creation for millions of graduates and even student suicides — which have amplified public anger. The confrontations have prompted domestic and international concern about civil liberties and put pressure on the government to investigate irregularities and address employment and education policy failures.
