Bilateral deals advance regional security, development and strategic partnerships

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-09-14 • 54 articles in total

In brief
  • States increase bilateral deals for security, development, and economic gains through diverse regional and external partnerships.
  • China, Canada, and the EU play major roles in investment, training, and financial support across multiple regions.
  • Regional actors pursue pragmatic, project-based agreements focusing on navigation, reconstruction, energy, and trade without major geopolitical shifts.

The collection shows a surge in bilateral agreements and initiatives across regions as states seek security, economic and development gains through targeted partnerships. Small island and Pacific states are deepening ties with regional and extra‑regional partners to bolster maritime security, development projects and capacity building. China, Canada and other external actors appear as major partners: China with investment, training and medical diplomacy in Africa and Central Asia; Canada and the EU negotiating strategic arrangements and financial support mechanisms. Meanwhile, regional actors from the Middle East, Africa and Eurasia are pursuing pragmatic deals on navigation, reconstruction, energy and trade without necessarily signaling major geopolitical shifts.
