Exposed networks and foiled plans: Russian recruitment in Germany

Russia Espionage Recruitment 2026-09-14 • 2 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian intelligence actively recruits agents in Germany using sophisticated methods revealed by an undercover journalist.
  • German authorities successfully disrupted a Russian espionage operation, highlighting strong counterintelligence efforts.
  • These cases show rising European security risks and demand better cooperation among media, law enforcement, and intelligence services.

Two recent reports reveal active Russian intelligence recruitment efforts in Germany: an undercover German journalist spent a year embedded in a recruiting network, and Die Zeit reported that German authorities thwarted an operational plan by Russian security services. The undercover investigation details recruitment methods, tradecraft and the personal risks faced by sources and reporters. The Die Zeit piece emphasizes German intelligence assessments, countermeasures and the broader pattern of Moscow attempting to operate on European soil. Together they underscore both the sophistication of Russian recruitment techniques and the vigilance of Western services tracking and disrupting these networks. The accounts highlight rising security concerns across Europe and the need for closer cooperation between media, law enforcement and intelligence agencies to expose and counter clandestine recruitment.
