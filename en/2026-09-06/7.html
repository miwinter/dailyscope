Mladić's burial ignites domestic praise and international rebuke

Serbia Mladić Burial Diplomacy 2026-09-06 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • Ratko Mladić's burial in Belgrade featured state honors and nationalist support, portraying him as a hero.
  • The ceremonies caused diplomatic backlash, with international officials condemning the glorification and canceling visits.
  • The events risk harming Western Balkans reconciliation and may be used for political gain in Serbia.

The burial and memorial events for convicted Bosnian Serb commander Ratko Mladić in Belgrade have reopened deep regional wounds, combining public glorification with official recognition and strong diplomatic fallout. Serbian state participation and nationalist crowds portrayed him as a hero, while his family and some supporters framed his death as a politicized or victimizing event. European and other international actors condemned the ceremonies, with several officials canceling visits and vocally opposing any honours for a convicted war criminal. Commentators warn the episode risks undermining reconciliation in the Western Balkans and may be exploited for domestic political gain ahead of elections.
