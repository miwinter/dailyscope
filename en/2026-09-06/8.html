Athens air show crash prompts fatalities report and safety probe

Athens Air Show Crash 2026-09-06 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • A Greek F-4 Phantom jet crashed near Athens during an air show, killing both crew members.
  • Authorities are investigating the crash and reviewing air display and military flight safety procedures.
  • The incident prompted national mourning and public calls for transparency about air-show safety.

A Greek F-4 Phantom fighter jet crashed during an air show near Athens, with most reports confirming both crew members were killed. Emergency services secured the site and witnesses described a sudden loss of altitude before the impact, while spectators were largely reported unharmed. Authorities have launched investigations and prompted safety reviews of air display procedures and military flight operations. The incident triggered national expressions of mourning and calls for transparency as investigators work to determine cause and responsibility.
