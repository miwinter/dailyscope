France is currently grappling with an unprecedented wildfire crisis, with over 200,000 people evacuated across multiple regions, particularly in the southwest. The government, led by President Emmanuel Macron, has mobilized the armed forces and sought European Union assistance to combat the fires, which are exacerbated by extreme heat, drought, and wind conditions. Concurrently, France has enacted a pioneering law banning social media use for children under 15 to protect minors online, marking a significant step in digital regulation. These developments highlight France's dual challenges of managing severe environmental emergencies and advancing social policy reforms.
