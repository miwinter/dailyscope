Escalation across Gulf and Levant threatens wider regional war

Middle East Conflict Escalation 2026-07-25 • 98 articles in total

In brief
  • The U.S. and Iran engage in repeated military strikes and threats, raising the risk of major escalation and broader regional conflict.
  • Gulf states, Saudi-led coalitions, and Iran-backed groups intensify fighting, threatening key maritime routes and regional security.
  • Israel's operations in Palestinian territories add to tensions while international warnings highlight severe humanitarian and economic risks from ongoing clashes.

Reporting shows an intense cycle of U.S.–Iran exchanges — repeated U.S. strikes and Iranian claims of attacks on American bases — while President Trump publicly weighs larger bombardments, keeping the prospect of major escalation alive. Gulf states and Saudi-led coalitions, along with reported covert strikes by Bahrain and Kuwait and Houthi operations from Yemen, have widened the battlefield and raised alarms about shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and the Red Sea. At the same time, Israel has launched major operations in the West Bank and conducted cross-border strikes into Lebanon and Gaza, producing casualties and inflaming regional tensions. International organisations, market analysts and diplomats warn of severe humanitarian consequences, energy-market disruption and a real risk that localized clashes will spill into a broader regional war.
