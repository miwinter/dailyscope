Regional escalation: US-Iran strikes, Houthi pressure and rising Gaza tensions

Middle East Conflict Escalation 2026-07-24 • 88 articles in total

In brief
  • US airstrikes and threats target Iran and its proxies, especially Houthis, to protect shipping lanes and deter aggression.
  • Iran and allies claim retaliation and vow continued attacks while intensifying tensions in Jerusalem, Gaza, and the Levant.
  • International warnings highlight risks of wider regional war with economic and humanitarian crises due to strikes on chokepoints and infrastructure.

A prolonged campaign of US airstrikes against Iranian targets and repeated threats from President Trump dominate reporting, with officials warning that a decision on a major strike could be imminent. Iran and the IRGC claim attacks, issue warnings to Western states, and pledge continued retaliation while reportedly supplying proxies such as the Houthis. Houthi operations and Yemen resistance actions, including attacks on shipping and border sites, are increasing concern about broader spillovers. Simultaneously, tensions around Jerusalem and Gaza — high-profile visits to Al-Aqsa, settler incursions and lethal incidents — risk opening additional fronts. International bodies and analysts warn that disruptions to Gulf chokepoints, strikes on infrastructure and mounting rhetoric could propel the crisis into a wider regional confrontation with economic and humanitarian consequences.
