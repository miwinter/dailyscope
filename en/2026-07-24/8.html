Youth-led unrest, exam scandals and symbolic dissent challenge Modi

India Modi Protests 2026-07-24 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Mass protests across India mix youth outrage over exam leaks with broader economic frustration and mockery of Prime Minister Modi.
  • The 'Cockroaches' label has become a viral symbol of dissent that challenges Modi’s image and influences political discourse.
  • Government responses include special courts and police control, but protests highlight deep economic problems and risk political consequences.

Mass protests across India combine youth-led anger over exam leaks and broader economic frustration with a spreading symbolic movement mocking Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Students and activists have mobilized nationwide — including hunger strikes and large demonstrations in New Delhi — demanding accountability for exam fraud while authorities establish special procedures and police manage large crowds. The 'Cockroaches' label has evolved into a viral emblem and satirical tool that has amplified dissent online and drawn political sensitivity. Together these developments pose reputational and political challenges to Modi’s government as economic strains and education scandals sustain pressure.
