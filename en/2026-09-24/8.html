Drone disruption at Berlin Brandenburg Airport

Berlin Drone Incident 2026-09-24 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • A drone near Berlin Brandenburg Airport caused a brief halt in air traffic and runway closures, leading to delays and diversions.
  • Authorities launched investigations into the drone's origin and initiated safety reviews while no injuries were reported.
  • Media highlighted security vulnerabilities in European airspace and advised travelers to monitor updates.

A drone was detected near Berlin Brandenburg Airport and temporarily halted air traffic, with runways briefly closed. The interruption caused flight delays and some diversions, though reports indicate the suspension lasted under an hour in several accounts. Authorities have opened investigations to determine the drone's origin and purpose, and safety measures and protocol reviews have been initiated. Coverage across outlets emphasized security vulnerabilities in European airspace, advised travelers to monitor updates, and noted no injuries were reported.
