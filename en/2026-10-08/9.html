Strengthening accountability: implementation, audits, and rule of law

EU Spotlight Accountability 2026-10-08 • 4 articles in total

In brief
  • The EU funds accountability initiatives focusing on implementation in partner countries and improving internal governance.
  • European Court of Auditors warns of persistent errors and weak controls in EU spending and calls for oversight reforms.
  • Experts stress strengthening rule-of-law mechanisms within member states to ensure judicial independence and uniform accountability.

EU-funded accountability work appears on two related fronts: on-the-ground implementation in partner countries and tightening internal EU governance. In Liberia, partners implementing the EU-funded Spotlight Initiative 2.0 report progress strengthening transparency, learning, and shared responsibility to improve program outcomes. At the EU level, the European Court of Auditors warns of persistent errors and weak controls in spending and urges reforms to balance oversight with administrative efficiency. Commentators like Marta Kos stress that reinforcing rule-of-law mechanisms within member states is also essential to ensure judicial independence and consistent accountability across the Union.

Countries covering this topic

Liberia: Spotlight 2.0 implementation

Local implementing partners highlight practical progress under the EU-funded Spotlight Initiative 2.0, emphasizing improved transparency, learning, and evidence-based decision-making. The perspective is implementation-focused: strengthening governance processes and shared responsibility to deliver better program outcomes.

EU auditors: spending errors and weak controls

The European Court of Auditors (Belgian and Austrian reports) warn that persistent errors and weak budgetary controls threaten efficient use of EU funds and call for reforms. This viewpoint prioritizes tighter oversight, better fiscal controls, and finding a balance between rigorous auditing and administrative efficiency to protect the EU budget.

  • The Court of Auditors fears persistence of errors in European spending: 'We must find a balance'
  • EU Budget Plans

Rule-of-law advocates: strengthen internal accountability

Voices such as Marta Kos argue that accountability requires stronger rule-of-law mechanisms within member states to safeguard judicial independence and uniform application of laws. This stance frames accountability as encompassing legal and institutional reforms as well as financial oversight to uphold EU credibility.
