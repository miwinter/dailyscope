Amazon cargo plane overruns Miami runway, at least five dead

Miami Runway Crash 2026-09-07 • 25 articles in total

In brief
  • An Amazon cargo plane overran the runway at Miami airport, hit ground vehicles, caught fire, and killed at least five people.
  • Emergency crews responded quickly, causing temporary airport shutdowns and flight disruptions.
  • Authorities launched investigations into causes including runway and mechanical factors.

A cargo aircraft operated for Amazon overran the runway at Miami International Airport, struck multiple ground vehicles and erupted in fire, killing at least five people and injuring others. Emergency crews, including dozens of firefighters, responded quickly and the airport experienced temporary shutdowns and flight disruptions. Video and eyewitness footage circulated showing the aircraft veering off the runway and the immediate rescue efforts on site. Local and federal authorities, including aviation investigators, have opened inquiries to determine causes ranging from runway excursion factors to mechanical or operational issues.
