Amazon Prime Air cargo plane crash near Miami

Amazon Prime Air Miami Crash 2026-09-07 • 28 articles in total

In brief
  • An Amazon Prime Air Boeing 767 overshot the Miami runway, struck ground vehicles, and caused a large fire with multiple fatalities and injuries.
  • Emergency crews responded amid widespread airport disruptions and flight diversions while federal and local investigations into the crash began.
  • Media coverage focused on the dramatic crash scenes, confirmed deaths, rescue efforts, operational impacts, and rising aviation safety concerns.

An Amazon Prime Air cargo Boeing 767 overshot the runway while landing at Miami International Airport, striking ground vehicles and igniting a large fire and plume of smoke. Authorities reported at least five people killed and multiple others injured as emergency crews responded and extracted victims from the wreckage. Runways and airport operations were disrupted, with some flights diverted as investigators from federal and local agencies opened probes into the cause. Video and photographic footage of the crash circulated widely, and Amazon said the situation was evolving as it coordinated with authorities. The incident has raised fresh questions about cargo flight safety and prompted regulatory and operational scrutiny of airport procedures.
