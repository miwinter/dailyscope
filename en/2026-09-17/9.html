Russia's Covert Reach: Sabotage, Cyberattacks and Influence

Russia Espionage Tactics 2026-09-17 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian covert activities span sabotage, cyber attacks, and contracted hitmen in Europe and the U.S.
  • These actions link to Russian military intelligence and oligarchs with political influence abroad.
  • Experts call for stronger international cooperation to counter hybrid threats and protect democracies.

Reporting from Europe and the Americas portrays a widening range of Russian covert activities, from alleged GRU-linked sabotage plots in Germany to cyber intrusions against Nordic institutions and claims of contracted hitmen operating on U.S. soil. Investigations tie individual attackers and networks to Russian military intelligence officers and patrons, while separate pieces raise concerns about oligarchs' financial ties to U.S. political figures. Analysts warn this behavior reflects a move beyond Cold War norms, using proxies, contracted operatives, and cyber tools to achieve deniable effects abroad. The pattern underscores urgent needs for enhanced international intelligence cooperation, law enforcement coordination, and policy responses to deter hybrid threats and protect democratic institutions.
