Vučić resigns to seek prime ministership, triggering political shake-up

Serbia Leadership Shakeup 2026-09-28 • 32 articles in total

In brief
  • Aleksandar Vučić resigned as Serbia's president to lead early parliamentary elections and seek the prime ministership.
  • His resignation followed protests and constitutional limits, prompting succession talks and an October 25 election that tests his party's power.
  • Vučić issued controversial pardons before resigning, and international observers see his move as a strategy to retain influence and alter Serbia's political course.

Aleksandar Vučić unexpectedly resigned as Serbia's president and announced he will lead his party into early parliamentary elections and seek the prime ministership, a move shaped by constitutional limits on a third presidential term. His departure after two terms and months of protests has prompted rapid discussion about succession, interim arrangements and an October 25 vote that will test the ruling party's hold on power. Multiple reports note he issued pardons and amnesties before stepping down, a detail that has drawn scrutiny and political reaction. International and regional observers portray the resignation as a strategic reshuffle that preserves Vučić's influence while reshaping Serbia's electoral timetable and regional posture.
