Global emergency responses: floods, storms, mountains and maritime rescues

Disaster Relief Operations 2026-09-28 • 85 articles in total

In brief
  • Global emergency operations surged due to floods, tropical storms, avalanches, maritime disasters, explosions, transport crashes, and seismic and volcanic events.
  • Authorities and aid agencies mobilized large-scale rescue missions, disaster declarations, evacuations, funding, vaccination campaigns, and preparedness drills worldwide.
  • Challenges include access difficulties, ongoing investigations, health hazards, and the need for enhanced interoperability and long-term resilience measures.

This set of reports shows a surge in emergency operations worldwide, dominated by extensive flooding in Bangkok that prompted disaster declarations, mass evacuations and service suspensions. Tropical storms and hurricanes—from Hurricane Polo in Mexico to Nolo in the Pacific—and a powerful nor'easter in the US Northeast produced heavy rain, power outages and search-and-rescue missions. High-altitude avalanches in the Himalayas and multiple maritime disasters, including ferry sinkings and migrant rescues, have stretched local rescue teams and highlighted access challenges. Simultaneously, international humanitarian funding, vaccination campaigns and preparedness drills were emphasized as authorities and aid agencies mobilized relief, while separate urban explosions and transport crashes drove intensive local rescue and recovery efforts.
