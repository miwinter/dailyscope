Rising heatwave deaths in Germany and the UK

Germany Heatwave Toll 2026-08-01 • 3 articles in total

In brief
  • Germany recorded about 9,800 heat-related deaths by July 19, the highest since 2016 according to official data.
  • England and Britain had roughly 2,880 excess deaths linked to heatwaves in May and June.
  • Reports highlight severe health impacts from heatwaves and the urgent need for better heat preparedness and adaptation.

Recent official reports show a severe human toll from early and mid‑season heatwaves in Europe. Germany recorded about 9,800 heat‑related deaths as of July 19, a total higher than any year since 2016, according to the Robert Koch Institute. Separately, government figures attribute roughly 2,877–2,887 excess deaths in England and Britain to heatwaves in May and June. Together the reports underscore mounting climate‑related health impacts, stress on public health systems, and an urgent need for improved heat preparedness and adaptation measures across countries.
