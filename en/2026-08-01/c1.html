France is currently grappling with severe wildfires exacerbated by a heatwave, leading to large-scale evacuations, power outages, and international firefighting support including teams from Ukraine and Australia. Additionally, France is confronting security issues including a knife attack in Paris, a foiled bank attack, and the expulsion of a Russian propagandist, alongside high-profile legal proceedings such as the extradition of Paul de România and the campaign finance trial of former president Nicolas Sarkozy.
