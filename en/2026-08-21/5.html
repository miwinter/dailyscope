Kyiv under fire; air-defence shortfalls and widening regional tensions

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-21 • 67 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian forces have increased missile, drone, and glide-bomb strikes on Ukrainian cities, hitting civilian infrastructure and causing many deaths and injuries.
  • Europe and NATO face growing regional security threats including airspace violations, naval drone incidents, and hybrid operations linked to the conflict.
  • Moscow projects power through military tests and domestic repression while the war impacts foreign fighters and disrupts global trade and food security.

Russian forces have intensified missile, drone and glide-bomb strikes on Kyiv and other Ukrainian cities, repeatedly hitting civilian infrastructure including pediatric hospitals and schools and causing dozens of deaths and injuries. Ukrainian leaders have highlighted air-defence gaps and appealed for more Western systems while making changes in defence leadership to better coordinate responses. European and NATO members report a spike in airspace incidents, naval drone encounters and hybrid threats that have at times spilled toward Moldova and Romania, raising regional security alarms. Meanwhile, Moscow is projecting power through missile tests and public rituals even as domestic repression and messaging (including court sentences and symbolic gestures) continue to shape the Kremlin’s wartime narrative. The conflict’s human toll extends beyond Europe, with reports of foreign fighters and recruitment affecting families in Africa and concerns about the broader disruption to trade and food security from Black Sea disruptions.
