AfD leaders re-elected amid large protests and heavy security

Germany Protests AfD 2026-07-05 • 15 articles in total

In brief
  • Germany's far-right AfD re-elected its leadership amid national political polarization.
  • Massive protests opposed the AfD, showing strong civic resistance and leading to clashes and roadblocks.
  • Police deployed heavily to secure the congress and maintain order during tense demonstrations.

Germany's far-right AfD successfully re-elected its leadership at an Erfurt congress, demonstrating continuity in the party's national standing. The vote occurred against the backdrop of widespread demonstrations, with tens of thousands taking to the streets to oppose the party. Several reports document clashes and roadblocks as well as a robust police presence deployed to protect the congress and manage public order. Together the coverage highlights a sharply polarized political climate in Germany, where a strengthened AfD faces broad civic resistance and concerns about democratic values.
