Contested gains, cross‑border strikes and mounting civilian toll

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-07-05 • 70 articles in total

In brief
  • Eastern Ukraine remains contested with conflicting claims over Kostyantynivka and ongoing military advances by both sides.
  • Ukraine expands long-range drone strikes into Russian territory targeting key infrastructure, while Russia intensifies missile and drone attacks on Ukrainian cities causing civilian casualties.
  • International actors engage diplomatically amid signs of internal strain in Russia and rising social hardships in Ukraine due to the prolonged conflict.

Reporting shows a contested battlefield in eastern Ukraine with Moscow repeatedly declaring the capture of Kostyantynivka while Kyiv and independent observers dispute those claims. Concurrently Ukraine has stepped up long‑range drone strikes into Russian territory—notably targeting oil terminals and ports around Saint Petersburg—demonstrating an expanded geographic reach of the fighting. Russia has launched large missile and drone assaults on Ukrainian cities, including Kyiv and Sumy, causing dozens of civilian deaths and widespread damage to infrastructure. International actors are engaged diplomatically and analytically — from offers to broker talks to warnings about regional escalation — even as signs of internal strain and elite discontent in Russia and social hardships inside Ukraine grow more visible.
