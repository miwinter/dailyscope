Bilateral Deals and Regional Diplomacy: A Snapshot

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-07-05 • 95 articles in total

In brief
  • Governments expand bilateral trade, investment, security, and infrastructure partnerships amid political tensions worldwide.
  • China and the EU use financing, aid, and policy tools to deepen cooperation across Africa, Asia, and Europe.
  • Diplomatic efforts include managing disputes, promoting cultural ties, and enhancing regional connectivity through visits and agreements.

Across regions governments are advancing bilateral agreements and practical cooperation while managing political friction. Southeast Asian states — notably Laos, Myanmar and Vietnam — are expanding trade, investment and green-growth ties, and Japan–India momentum on security and AI underscores deepening strategic partnerships in Asia. China’s outreach and financing appear across Africa, the Pacific and Europe, while the EU is negotiating targeted partnerships and policy instruments with partners from Armenia to Ethiopia. Transport, energy and infrastructure deals (air links, ports, rail, bridge projects and special economic zones) feature prominently, even as bilateral disputes and migration issues continue to strain relations in some corridors.
