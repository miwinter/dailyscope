Patchwork of Bilateral Deals: Security, Trade and Strategic Outreach

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-10-11 • 76 articles in total

In brief
  • States pursued diverse bilateral engagements including security cooperation, trade pacts, investment, and high-level visits to diversify partners and promote growth.
  • China and the EU focus on stabilizing trade and protecting strategic industries while balancing economic and geopolitical interests in various regions.
  • Bilateral diplomacy involves capacity-building, infrastructure projects, cultural exchanges, and normative considerations to enhance regional stability and development.

Over the period covered, states pursued a wide spectrum of bilateral engagements ranging from security cooperation and capacity-building to trade pacts, investment lines and high-level visits. Military and policing ties—training, equipment handovers and defense accords—feature prominently alongside trade and investment initiatives such as free-trade agreements, CEPA talks and credit lines that aim to diversify partners and fuels growth. China’s growing outreach in Africa and the Pacific, and coordinated EU–China steps to stabilise trade, sit alongside regional integration projects and digital/AI cooperation, showing competing economic and technological vectors. Smaller states secured development grants and tourism or education arrangements while human-rights concerns and geopolitical analyses tempered some deals, highlighting normative and strategic trade-offs in bilateral diplomacy.
