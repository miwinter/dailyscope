Nepal Urges International Climate Finance and Accountability

Nepal Climate Finance 2026-09-26 • 3 articles in total

In brief
  • Nepal urges the international community to provide urgent financial aid and technology to tackle escalating climate disasters.
  • Nepal demands wealthy nations fulfill climate finance commitments and take responsibility for aiding vulnerable countries.
  • Nepal presses the United Nations for concrete support and coordinated action ahead of global climate negotiations.

Nepal's leaders are issuing urgent appeals for international climate finance and technical support as the country confronts escalating climate-related disasters. Prime Minister Balendra Shah and other officials call for financial assistance, technology transfer, and coordinated global action to bolster adaptation and disaster risk reduction. After deadly glacial-collapse floods, Nepali ministers demanded that wealthy nations honor their climate finance commitments and take responsibility for assisting vulnerable countries. Nepal is also pressing the United Nations and global partners ahead of climate negotiations to secure concrete commitments for adaptation, resilience, and long-term support.
