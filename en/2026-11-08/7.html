Japan earthquake — central region shaken

Japan Earthquake 2026-11-08 • 19 articles in total

In brief
  • A magnitude 5.7 earthquake struck near Shizuoka Prefecture, injuring dozens and causing structural damage in central Japan.
  • The quake disrupted rail services, shattered windows, and caused power outages, with no tsunami warning issued.
  • Authorities warned of aftershocks, urged caution, and continue rescue and damage assessments amid varied injury reports.

A magnitude 5.7 earthquake struck central Japan, centered near Shizuoka Prefecture, injuring dozens and shaking communities in Aichi and neighboring prefectures. Reported injury totals vary across outlets, with initial counts ranging from about a dozen to more than two dozen as emergency teams conduct assessments. The quake disrupted rail services, shattered windows in some areas and caused localized outages, but authorities said there was no tsunami warning. Officials warned residents to expect aftershocks and urged caution while rescue and damage inspections continue.
