Three bodies found at suspect's home after Kraków shooting

Poland Shooter Case 2026-11-08 • 15 articles in total

In brief
  • Authorities found three bodies at the suspect’s home, likely the attacker’s relatives, after the Kraków mass shooting killing six.
  • Investigations focus on motives, including possible far-right links, analyzing evidence and surveillance while pursuing multiple leads.
  • A suspect has been detained; officials increased security, declared national mourning, and continue thorough inquiries.

Polish authorities discovered three bodies at the residence of a suspect in a mass shooting in Kraków, with the prime minister saying the victims were likely the attacker’s relatives. The market-square attack killed six people and injured many others, though some early accounts reported different casualty totals. Investigators are examining motives, including reports suggesting possible far‑right influences, and are reviewing surveillance and forensic evidence while pursuing leads. Officials have detained a suspect, declared a period of national mourning, and increased security around public spaces as inquiries continue.
