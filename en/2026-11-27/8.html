US–Russia Talks: Trump signals outreach

US–Russia Talks 2026-11-27 • 12 articles in total

In brief
  • Trump invited Putin to an event in Florida, signaling willingness for personal diplomacy despite ongoing tensions.
  • He urged Putin to halt military aggression in Europe, emphasizing coordinated appeals with allied leaders.
  • Trump believes Putin might seek a deal to end the Ukraine war; US envoys report no Russian nuclear use plans, but claims remain unverified.

Former US President Donald Trump has publicly suggested that Vladimir Putin might attend an event in Florida and said he would like him to be present. He has repeatedly urged Putin to 'pull back' or stop military aggression in Europe, often framing the appeals alongside allied leaders. Trump also described Putin as potentially open to a deal to end the Russia‑Ukraine war, and US envoys reportedly relayed assurances that Russia does not intend to use nuclear weapons. Reporting treats these comments as diplomatic overtures and claims rather than independently verified policy shifts, leaving the prospects for concrete de‑escalation uncertain.
