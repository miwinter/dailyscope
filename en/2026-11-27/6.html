US East Coast Thanksgiving Storm

US East Coast Storms 2026-11-27 • 26 articles in total

In brief
  • A powerful storm hit the US East Coast over Thanksgiving, causing widespread power outages and severe travel disruptions.
  • Hundreds of thousands lost electricity as crews worked to restore service amid coastal flooding and infrastructure damage.
  • Thousands of flights were delayed or canceled, and major events like New York’s parade were postponed for the first time.

A powerful storm struck the US East Coast over Thanksgiving, disrupting travel, damaging infrastructure and prompting widespread emergency responses. Hundreds of thousands — and by some counts nearly one million — households were left without power as utility crews worked to restore service. The system caused thousands of flight delays and cancellations and forced the postponement or cancellation of major public events, including New York’s parade. Coastal flooding, high winds and heavy rain trapped vehicles, closed bridges and stretched rescue services while officials issued warnings and urged caution.
