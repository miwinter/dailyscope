Great Power Rivalry: US, Russia, China

Great Power Rivalry 2026-11-27 • 25 articles in total

In brief
  • US, Russia, and China compete globally, impacting diplomacy, military strategies, and economics, especially in Europe, Asia, and the Global South.
  • US pushes direct talks with Russia despite hybrid threats; NATO faces security challenges from Russian operations in Europe.
  • US-China rivalry spans trade, infrastructure, and tech, raising tensions over Taiwan and Arctic, with China and Russia expanding influence in Africa and Indian Ocean.

Competition among the United States, Russia, and China is reshaping diplomatic norms, military postures, and economic influence across multiple regions. Washington’s high‑profile diplomacy and public appeals to Moscow play out alongside Kremlin hybrid operations and power projection in Europe and overseas, while Beijing expands its footprint through trade, infrastructure, and political influence. Technological and strategic contests—from hypersonic weapons to Arctic icebreakers—raise escalation risks and test alliance cohesion. The struggle for influence in the Global South, maritime domains, and critical supply chains will largely determine regional security and development trajectories.
