Russia–Ukraine attacks: escalation, spillovers and civilian toll

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-27 • 32 articles in total

In brief
  • Russia launches large missile and drone attacks on Ukraine's cities and infrastructure, causing civilian casualties and major disruption.
  • Ukraine strikes Russian energy and naval targets, including vessels in Libyan waters, escalating regional tensions and sparking warnings of retaliation.
  • Allies show divided responses amid fears of wider conflict, while reports highlight severe humanitarian impact and political rhetoric on both sides.

The reporting documents a renewed and intensifying phase of the Russia–Ukraine war marked by large missile and drone barrages, repeated warnings of imminent massive strikes, and targeted Ukrainian strikes on Russian assets—including long‑range and naval operations that have spilled into other regions. Energy and industrial infrastructure on both sides have been hit, raising concerns about wider economic effects and attacks on critical services. Kyiv’s assertions and Moscow’s accusations about widening the conflict, together with international alarm and divided allied responses, highlight the risk of escalation and diplomatic strain. Amid the military moves, personal stories and casualty reports underscore the sustained humanitarian cost across frontlines and occupied areas.
