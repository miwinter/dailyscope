Trump presidency: media wars, policy shifts and international scrutiny

US: Trump Presidency 2026-09-19 • 60 articles in total

In brief
  • Trump’s exclusion of major news outlets raises global concerns about press freedom and democratic norms.
  • The administration pursues wide-ranging policy changes impacting healthcare, visas, conservation, and the economy.
  • Internationally, Trump adopts aggressive rhetoric on Iran, engages North Korea, and faces scrutiny over finances and political setbacks.

Across international coverage, Donald Trump’s clashes with major news outlets dominate reporting, with many governments and media organizations flagging a widening confrontation over press access and allegations of 'fake news.' Beyond the media fight, the administration pushed domestic policy changes — from drug‑pricing moves and H‑1B visa reforms to endangered species rule changes and large stimulus proposals — that prompted debate about economic and regulatory impacts. Foreign and security matters also figure prominently, including aggressive rhetoric toward Iran, renewed outreach toward North Korea, and concerns about U.S. influence in regions like the Western Balkans and Canada–Europe relations. Simultaneously, repeated disclosures and claims about repayments linked to a Russian‑funded wedding and a string of political setbacks have intensified scrutiny of Trump’s finances and democratic norms.
