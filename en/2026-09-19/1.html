Bilateral diplomacy: Arctic security, Chinese outreach and major defence deals dominate

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-09-19 • 175 articles in total

In brief
  • US-Denmark ties focus on Arctic security and sovereignty, raising debates about military influence and regional governance.
  • China expands development and cultural cooperation across multiple regions to enhance economic integration and sovereignty respect.
  • Global diplomacy features defence deals reshaping security and economic pacts building parallel influence networks.

This collection of reports highlights two dominant threads of recent bilateral diplomacy: a high‑profile US‑Denmark agreement over Greenland that has animated coverage about Arctic security and sovereignty, and expanding Chinese bilateral engagement across the Pacific, Africa and Central Asia focused on development, infrastructure and consular ties. Parallel developments include major US defence sales and transfers that are prompting debate over regional security and arms‑control implications, as well as European efforts to deepen strategic and trade links with partners such as Canada. Numerous state visits, MoUs and economic cooperation pacts—from labor and technology agreements in Central Asia to investment and aid packages in Africa and the Pacific—underscore an active period of transactional diplomacy. Together these items reflect a global pattern: security arrangements and high‑value defence transactions are reshaping strategic alignments while economic, development and cultural agreements are building parallel networks of influence and capacity‑building.
