Beijing's drills and expanding unmanned capabilities raise regional tensions

China Military Drills 2026-09-19 • 4 articles in total

In brief
  • China plans military drills and increased security to advance strategic priorities with regional and market impacts.
  • Taiwan conducted its first joint attack-drone exercises to modernize its military and strengthen deterrence.
  • China unveiled a mothership for giant underwater drones, expanding maritime unmanned capabilities and affecting regional security.

China has announced plans to bolster security and conduct military drills, presenting them as part of broader strategic priorities with potential regional and market impacts. Taiwan carried out its first joint joint exercises using multiple attack drones, marking a milestone in its military modernization and efforts to strengthen deterrence. Beijing also unveiled a mothership for giant underwater drones, signaling an expansion of maritime unmanned capabilities that could enhance surveillance and operational reach. Analysts warn these moves may intensify signaling between Beijing and Washington, prompt neighbor-state recalibrations, and influence regional stability and markets.
