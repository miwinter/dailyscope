Spain train crash

Spain Train Crash 2026-11-04 • 22 articles in total

In brief
  • A high-speed train crash near Urda, Spain killed about 68–69 people and injured many, including foreigners, prompting national mourning and consular involvement.
  • Transport Minister Óscar Puente resigned, citing political responsibility and withdrawing from upcoming elections amid criticism and political pressure.
  • Investigations into the crash's causes and rail safety protocols are ongoing, sparking calls for reforms and improved emergency response in Spain.

A high-speed rail collision near Urda in central Spain killed around 68–69 people and left many injured, including several foreign nationals. Transport Minister Óscar Puente offered and subsequently submitted his resignation, framing it as political responsibility and withdrawing from the upcoming elections. Authorities have launched investigations into the causes and rail safety protocols amid scrutiny after multiple deadly railway incidents this year. The disaster has prompted national mourning, international consular involvement and renewed calls for reforms to Spain’s rail oversight and emergency response.
