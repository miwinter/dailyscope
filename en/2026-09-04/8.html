Repatriation of Ratko Mladić and Regional Reactions

Serbia War Crimes Repatriation 2026-09-04 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Serbia repatriated Ratko Mladić's remains with military honours and state funeral, highlighting ceremonial logistics and closure.
  • The event reveals Serbia's ongoing struggle with wartime accountability and impacts its EU integration efforts.
  • It reignites regional debates on justice, memory politics, reconciliation, and the Bosnian War's political legacy.

Serbia repatriated the remains of convicted war criminal Ratko Mladić to Belgrade on a government aircraft, with military honours and funeral arrangements drawing public attention. State-focused reports emphasize the logistics, ceremonial handling and closure the return represents for some, while noting officials' and victims' families' reactions. Regional and international commentators frame the repatriation as evidence that Serbia still struggles to confront its wartime past, raising questions about accountability even as the country pursues EU aspirations. The event reignited debates over collective memory, reconciliation and the political legacy of the Bosnian War across the Balkans.
