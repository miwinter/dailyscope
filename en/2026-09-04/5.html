Regional escalation: US–Iran exchanges, Israeli operations and wider spillovers

Middle East Conflict Escalation 2026-09-04 • 60 articles in total

In brief
  • The US and Iran are exchanging air, missile, and drone attacks in the Gulf, threatening shipping and energy markets.
  • Intense Israeli actions in Palestinian territories and Lebanon raise humanitarian alarms and risk wider regional conflict.
  • Yemen's drone and missile strikes continue amid multiple regional conflicts and international calls for restraint and investigations.

Fighting between the United States and Iran has resumed across the Gulf, with air strikes, missile and drone attacks reported against bases and shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, raising fears of disruption to energy markets and a broader regional war. Simultaneously, intensive Israeli operations in the West Bank, Gaza and along the Lebanon border — including raids, killings and settlement expansions — are driving local humanitarian alarms and international criticism. Separate threads of violence in Yemen, attacks on tankers and footage of drone strikes underline multiple active fronts beyond the Iran–US axis. International actors are reacting with condemnations, calls for restraint and discussions of troop contributions or deployments, while contentious reports of civilian deaths (notably at an Iranian wedding) have intensified scrutiny and demands for investigations.
