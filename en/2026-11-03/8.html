German pediatrician sexual abuse trial

Germany Doctor Sexual Abuse Trial 2026-11-03 • 6 articles in total

In brief
  • A German pediatrician faces over 130 sexual abuse charges involving more than 70 children across multiple hospitals.
  • The case reveals serious hospital safeguarding failures and prompts demands for stronger protections for minors in healthcare.
  • Victims' rights, emotional impact, and legal accountability dominate media coverage, urging systemic reforms and thorough investigations.

A German pediatrician is being prosecuted on accusations of sexually abusing more than 70 children, with prosecutors charging him in excess of 130 counts. Reports indicate the alleged offenses span many years and occurred at multiple medical facilities, raising questions about hospital safeguarding and oversight. The defendant reportedly admitted many of the assaults in some accounts, and the court proceedings are now focused on establishing the full scope of the crimes and delivering accountability. Victims' rights and the emotional and institutional fallout for the medical community have been central themes in coverage. The case has prompted calls for stronger protections for minors in healthcare settings and renewed scrutiny of investigative practices.
