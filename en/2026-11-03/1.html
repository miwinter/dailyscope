The midterm cycle has been defined by historic early turnout, an intense final push from former President Trump, and widespread polling that suggests Democratic advantages but leaves room for surprises. International observers and foreign governments are watching how results will shape U.S. policy and global balance, while markets and big donors respond nervously to the uncertainty. In short, turnout, legal contests and closing‑week campaign dynamics will determine whether Congress constrains or sustains the president's agenda.
