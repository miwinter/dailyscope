Tirana unrest: government department set ablaze amid violent clashes

Albania Protests: Department Fire 2026-10-15 • 6 articles in total

In brief
  • Protests in Tirana escalated to violent clashes with police and arson targeting government buildings including the Education Ministry.
  • Authorities used tear gas, detained dozens, restricted journalists, and aimed to restore order amid ongoing unrest.
  • The protests reflect both student grievances over education policy and wider political discontent risking further instability.

Protests in Tirana turned violent, with demonstrators clashing with police and setting government buildings — including a government department and the Education Ministry — on fire. Authorities deployed tear gas, detained dozens, and imposed security measures while some reporters faced restrictions. Eyewitness footage and reports describe attacks on police stations and vehicles, and at least one account lists 29 injured and 35 arrested. Coverage frames the unrest variously as student-led anger over education policy and as broader political discontent, highlighting risks to governance and the potential for further escalation.
