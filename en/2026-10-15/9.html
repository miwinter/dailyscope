Mass Protests Challenge De la Espriella's Government

Colombia Protests 2026-10-15 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • Thousands of diverse Colombian groups protested against President Abelardo de la Espriella's government over policies and economic issues.
  • Opposition warns protests may lead to a nationwide general strike in November to pressure the government.
  • Heavy security was deployed to manage large, tense demonstrations, showing strong public discontent.

Thousands of Colombians, including unions, Indigenous groups, students and feminists, have mobilized in early mass demonstrations against President Abelardo de la Espriella's new government. Opposition leaders warn the unrest could escalate into a nationwide general strike in November as organizers coordinate pressure tactics. Reports emphasize public grievances over recent policies and economic conditions and note heavy security deployments to manage demonstrations. Visuals such as red smoke and large street turnouts underscore the depth of public discontent.
