Scaling threats: drones, cyberattacks, espionage and great-power pressure

Security: Threat Scaling 2026-10-15 • 104 articles in total

In brief
  • Diverse regional threats include drone and maritime strikes, cyberattacks, espionage, and sabotage affecting military and civilian targets.
  • Major powers and alliances respond with heightened readiness, intelligence coordination, defence investments, and international security measures.
  • Rising criminal, insurgent, and digital security challenges drive tougher domestic policies, intelligence disputes, and calls for improved resilience and cooperation.

Across regions analysts and authorities report an expansion and cross-border coordination of diverse threats, from drone and maritime strikes in the Black Sea and Gulf to rising cyber operations targeting critical infrastructure and services. Intelligence and defence officials warn of growing espionage, internal security breaches and weapon tests — notably from North Korea and proxy actors in the Middle East — that are prompting exercises, deterrence postures and procurement drives. Satellite and communications disruptions, including repeated Starlink outages and targeted sabotage, are shown to have immediate operational and humanitarian consequences for Ukraine and other actors. NATO, regional militaries and security services are reacting with heightened readiness, counter-drone investments and international probes, while some states pursue hardline domestic security measures and prison models as part of broader efforts to cope with mounting threats.
