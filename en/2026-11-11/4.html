Impeachment bid against US defence secretary stalls amid partisan divide

US Defense Secretary Impeachment 2026-11-11 • 24 articles in total

In brief
  • House Republicans blocked impeachment efforts against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, exposing partisan divides and some Democratic abstentions.
  • Hegseth called the impeachment a politically motivated farce and defended his record amid ongoing procedural fights.
  • Democrats promised continued oversight when regaining Congress, while Republicans celebrated the stalled effort as a political victory.

An impeachment bid against U.S. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth was effectively blocked in the House, with Republicans moving to stall or refer the measure and the effort collapsing for now. The episode exposed sharp partisan divisions and some Democratic abstentions that helped prevent the measure from advancing. Hegseth publicly rejected the effort, calling it politically motivated and defending his record. Republican lawmakers who launched or threatened to force votes signalled continuing procedural fights, while Democrats vowed to pursue oversight again when they regain control of Congress.
