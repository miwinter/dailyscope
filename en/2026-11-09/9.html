A deadly apartment fire in northern China has killed at least 14 people, renewing scrutiny of urban fire safety and building code enforcement. Authorities and state media report rescue operations and investigations are underway, while casualty figures and causes remain under review. Several outlets situate the blaze in a pattern of recent deadly fires and point to gaps in regulation and enforcement, especially in high-rise housing. The coverage ranges from immediate casualty reporting to broader critiques of construction standards and policy responses.
