In brief

  • Eisenkot seeks a broad unity or emergency government, negotiating under tight legal deadlines to avoid new elections.
  • Coalition talks are critical for Netanyahu's future and Israel's political stability during a serious diplomatic crisis.

After a fragmented election left no clear majority, President Isaac Herzog has tasked former military chief Gadi Eisenkot with forming a government, triggering intensive coalition negotiations. Many outlets report Eisenkot’s formal mandate and the ceremonial transfer of responsibility, while other pieces highlight varying procedural deadlines and the clock on coalition-building. Eisenkot has publicly proposed an emergency or broad unity government, presenting an inclusive offer to multiple factions as he seeks partners. Analysts warn the path is complex and could reshape Netanyahu’s political future depending on whether a stable majority can be assembled.
