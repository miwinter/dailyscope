Krakow terror attack — security and response

Poland Krakow Terror Attack 2026-11-09 • 28 articles in total

In brief
  • Gunman in Krakow's Old Town killed and injured multiple people, triggering terrorism probes and concerns over far-right radicalization and security lapses.
  • Investigations focused on suspect's clean record, sport-shooting license, potential extremist ties, and national security implications.
  • Community rallied with bravery, public appeals, memorials, and international support amid debates on firearms, surveillance, and preparedness.

A gunman opened fire in Krakow’s busy Old Town, killing and injuring multiple people and shattering a sense of public safety across Poland. Authorities have launched terrorism investigations amid early indications of far‑right motivations, while investigators probe the suspect’s background and possible links to extremist networks. Graphic footage and eyewitness accounts have deepened public shock and prompted immediate security checks, medical appeals and support for victims and responders. The incident has sparked national mourning, international reactions and renewed questions about firearms licensing, radicalization and preparedness of security services.
