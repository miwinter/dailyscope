Egypt train collision

Egypt Train Collision 2026-11-09 • 27 articles in total

In brief
  • Two passenger trains collided south of Cairo near Giza, causing derailments and at least four to seven deaths with over 88 injuries.
  • Emergency responders evacuated passengers and hospitals treated the wounded amid ongoing rescue and investigations.
  • The crash intensified scrutiny of Egypt's rail safety, prompting calls for safety reviews and accountability.

Two passenger trains collided south of Cairo (Giza/al-Ayyat), causing multiple carriages to derail and producing a heavy casualty toll. Most international reports cite seven fatalities and well over 100 injured, though some official updates gave a lower figure of four dead and about 88 injured, reflecting early discrepancies in reporting. Emergency services, rescue teams and bystanders evacuated passengers and hospitals treated the wounded while investigators opened probes into causes and safety protocols. The crash has intensified scrutiny of Egypt’s rail infrastructure and prompted calls for safety reviews and accountability.
