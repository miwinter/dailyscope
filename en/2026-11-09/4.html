China–Nvidia espionage controversy

In brief

  • Nvidia launched an internal probe amid U.S. intelligence reports about recruitment attempts at a Singapore data center, raising national security and governance questions.
  • Market and political reactions were swift, with Nvidia shares dropping and conflicting reports causing ongoing uncertainty over the full facts.
