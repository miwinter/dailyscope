Peña detained amid Ueno Bank probe and political crisis

Paraguay Peña Detention 2026-11-07 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • Former Paraguayan president Santiago Peña was detained and placed under house arrest relating to investigations into Ueno Bank's collapse.
  • Authorities link Peña to financial irregularities through his past shareholder ties during the expanding probe.
  • His detention escalates political crisis amid impeachment, institutional conflicts, and concerns over social-economic fallout from frozen savings.

Former Paraguayan president Santiago Peña has been detained and placed under house arrest after being removed from office as prosecutors and police executed formal detention orders. Authorities link the actions to an expanding investigation into the collapse of Ueno Bank and Peña’s reported ties to the institution. The events have intensified an already volatile political crisis, including impeachment and power struggles between state institutions. Reports warn of broader social and economic fallout as authorities pursue accountability for frozen savings and alleged irregularities.

Detention orders and legal procedures

Paraguayan prosecutors and the Fiscalía have formally notified and executed detention orders against Peña, carrying out actions at his residence and arranging his house arrest pending presentation to the unit for economic crimes and anticorruption. Coverage details the procedural steps, late-night police actions and the involvement of Peña’s defense as the justice system moves the case forward.

Ueno Bank probe and financial links

Authorities have tied Peña’s arrest to the Ueno Bank affair, noting his past shareholder status and focusing on alleged financial dealings connected to the bank’s collapse. Reporting frames the bank investigation as the central legal basis for detention and as a widening probe into stakeholders and possible irregularities.

Political crisis, impeachment and social impact

Peña’s ouster, impeachment and subsequent detention are presented as a pivotal escalation in a state crisis, raising concerns about institutional tensions and domestic instability. Several reports emphasize the social consequences—millions in frozen savings and public demands for accountability—that have driven political reactions and intensified scrutiny.
