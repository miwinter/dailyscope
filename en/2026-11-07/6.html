Miller named White House chief of staff

US: Trump Staff Shakeup 2026-11-07 • 27 articles in total

In brief
  • Stephen Miller replaces Susie Wiles as White House chief of staff after Republican midterm losses.
  • Miller's appointment signals a harder line on immigration, continuing strict policies and asylum enforcement.
  • The move draws criticism from Latino groups and Democrats, predicting more confrontational governance and political challenges.

President Trump has appointed Stephen Miller as White House chief of staff, replacing Susie Wiles in a post-midterms reshuffle. The move consolidates a hardline adviser with deep influence over immigration policy into the center of the administration. It follows Republican midterm setbacks and has prompted swift criticism from Latino groups, Democrats and other observers who warn of renewed focus on deportations and confrontational governance. Analysts expect the change to harden policy priorities and reshape internal White House dynamics going forward.
