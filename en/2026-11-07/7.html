Paraguay Peña Impeachment and Political Crisis

Paraguay Peña Impeachment 2026-11-07 • 25 articles in total

In brief
  • Paraguay’s President Peña was impeached and placed under house arrest after the collapse of Ueno Bank, triggering political unrest and constitutional concerns.
  • Vice President Alliana was swiftly sworn in as president amid public clashes and heavy security in Asunción.
  • The banking scandal fueled investigations and raised questions about presidential immunity and the rapid impeachment process’s legality.

Paraguay’s president Santiago Peña was impeached by Congress and placed under house arrest amid fallout from the collapse of digital lender Ueno Bank. Vice President Pedro Alliana was quickly sworn in as president, prompting celebrations by opponents and clashes and heavy security in Asunción. Prosecutors have issued detention orders and opened investigations into alleged misuse of office tied to the banking scandal, raising constitutional questions about immunity and the speed of the removal. The rapid transition has provoked domestic unrest and regional concern over governance and financial oversight.
