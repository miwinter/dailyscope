US Measles Resurgence

US Measles Resurgence 2026-11-17 • 15 articles in total

In brief

  • Public health experts urge coordinated federal and local action to contain outbreaks and restore public trust in immunization.
  • The US tightens export restrictions on advanced AI chips to China, causing global supply chain concerns and allied reactions like Singapore's call for trusted access.

Measles has re-emerged in the United States after more than two decades without sustained transmission, prompting warnings from health officials about gaps in vaccination coverage and surveillance. The country faces the loss or risk of losing its measles-elimination certification, driving discussions of intensified vaccination campaigns, contact tracing, and containment measures. Public health authorities and experts emphasize that restoring elimination status will require coordinated federal and local action to boost coverage, improve outbreak detection, and rebuild public confidence in immunization. The resurgence underscores how quickly preventable diseases can return when immunization and surveillance weaken.
