Russia–Ukraine Attacks: infrastructure hits and escalating strikes

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-17 • 44 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian strikes heavily damage Kyiv bridges, causing widespread power and water outages, worsening civilian hardship.
  • Ukraine and Russia launch large-scale drone attacks on key bridges and regions, escalating military conflict intensity.
  • A deadly strike kills 72 people, highlighting severe humanitarian impacts amid escalating arms flows and tense diplomacy.

A sustained cycle of strikes has focused on Ukraine’s infrastructure and logistics, with Russian attacks hitting multiple bridges in Kyiv and causing power and water outages that worsen civilian hardship. Kyiv and Russian-held Crimea have both been targeted by large drone operations and strikes, including repeated hits on the Kerch/Crimea bridge and record-size drone barrages reported by both sides. A separate strike on a village resulted in a death toll of 72, underscoring the severe humanitarian cost. Reports of increased arms flows to Russia and political signals—ranging from threats of force to allegations about targeting leadership—suggest the conflict is intensifying and further complicating diplomatic prospects.
