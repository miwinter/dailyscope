This set of reports shows a global uptick in bilateral agreements spanning defence procurement, energy and infrastructure projects, trade pacts, and development assistance. Several stories also highlight diplomatic follow-through—state visits, high-level meetings and symbolic gestures—that underpin formal signings and implementation plans.
