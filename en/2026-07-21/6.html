Ortega ends elections and consolidates power

Nicaragua Democracy Erosion 2026-07-21 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • Nicaraguan President Daniel Ortega declared competitive elections will end, signaling a move to extend his rule indefinitely.
  • Ortega uses constitutional and legislative changes to block opposition and eliminate political competition.
  • This power consolidation draws international concern over democratic decline and regional stability risks.

Across reports, Nicaraguan President Daniel Ortega has declared that competitive elections will no longer be held, signaling an end to presidential alternation and moves to extend his rule. Articles describe constitutional and legislative maneuvers and explicit statements from Ortega that elections are 'never again' under the current arrangement. Observers, opposition groups, and human rights organizations warn this effectively centralizes power, removes meaningful political competition, and undermines civil liberties. The development has drawn regional and international concern about democratic backsliding and potential impacts on stability in Central America.
