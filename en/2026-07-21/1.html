US–Iran exchanges escalate, threatening wider regional war

Middle East Conflict Escalation 2026-07-21 • 156 articles in total

In brief
  • The U.S. and Iran are engaged in escalating airstrikes and missile attacks portraying a near full-scale war.
  • Iran-backed Houthis threaten maritime blockades disrupting key shipping routes in the Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz.
  • Regional states condemn Iranian aggression, strengthen defenses, and international efforts push for urgent de-escalation to avoid wider conflict.

A sustained campaign of U.S. airstrikes against Iranian targets across multiple consecutive nights has driven a sharp escalation in hostilities, prompted by the reported deaths of U.S. service members and public warnings from U.S. leaders. Iran and the IRGC have responded with missile and drone strikes on regional bases and infrastructure, and Iranian officials have framed the confrontation as a large- or full-scale war. Yemen’s Houthi movement and Iran-backed militias have announced naval blockades and attacks on shipping, threatening Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz routes and raising global energy and trade risks. International actors, mediators and analysts warn the conflict could spread further without urgent de‑escalation and ceasefire measures, as civilian infrastructure and regional allies are drawn into the fighting.
