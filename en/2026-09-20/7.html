Clashes with far-right protesters in The Hague

Netherlands Far-Right Protests 2026-09-20 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Far-right protests in The Hague turned violent with attackers using projectiles and Molotov cocktails against police.
  • Police used water cannons and riot gear to restore order and made dozens of arrests.
  • The unrest involved antisemitic slogans and reflects broader tensions over immigration and rising extremist activity in Europe.

Multiple reports describe violent confrontations between far-right demonstrators and police in The Hague, with authorities using dispersal tactics including water cannon and riot police to restore order. Demonstrations escalated into attacks on officers and the throwing of projectiles, fireworks and Molotov cocktails, prompting dozens of arrests. Several outlets highlight the ideological dimension, noting antisemitic language and Nazi slogans among some protesters. Officials implemented emergency measures and investigated the unrest amid wider concerns about rising far-right activity in Europe. Coverage also situates the demonstrations within debates over immigration and asylum policy that mobilized the groups involved.
