NATO selects German general Breuer for top military post

NATO Leadership Selection 2026-09-20 • 4 articles in total

In brief
  • NATO appointed German General Breuer to lead its Military Committee to reinforce deterrence and operational readiness.
  • Breuer's warnings about a possible Russian invasion highlight NATO's focus on preparedness and European leadership.
  • The selection over rival Carignan reflects NATO's political choices and alliance decision-making dynamics.

NATO has appointed German General Breuer to lead its Military Committee, marking another leadership change in the alliance's command structure. Coverage highlights Breuer’s background and frames his selection as part of ongoing reforms to strengthen NATO’s deterrence and readiness. Several reports note Breuer’s warnings about a potential Russian invasion, underscoring the alliance’s focus on preparedness. Canadian reporting emphasizes that NATO preferred Breuer over rival candidate Carignan, illustrating the political dimensions of personnel choices. Observers present the appointment as reinforcing European leadership within NATO while prioritizing operational posture against Russian threats.
