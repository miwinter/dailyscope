Stalemate and high stakes in EU budget talks

EU Budget Politics 2026-10-16 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • EU leaders face a deadlock on the multiannual budget due to conflicting priorities like agriculture, defense, energy, and Ukraine support.
  • National politics, especially electoral fears in France, are strongly influencing member states' budget positions.
  • António Costa leads efforts to propose a new budget plan aiming for a year-end deal amid continuing resistance.

EU leaders and institutions are locked in fraught negotiations over the next multiannual budget, with deep divisions on priorities such as agriculture, defense, energy and support for Ukraine. Several reports describe member states digging in and setting red lines, while pressure from national leaders and the European Parliament complicates compromise. The impasse has put a timely agreement in doubt and risks delaying funding for long-term programs and urgent assistance. Political calculations at home — notably concerns about French politics — are shaping positions on allocation decisions. Meanwhile, António Costa has assumed responsibility for preparing a new proposal aimed at breaking the deadlock and securing an agreement by year-end, though pushback persists.

Entrenched divisions and stalled negotiations

Calls to increase Ukraine funding

Some leaders argue the budget must be adjusted to close financing gaps for Ukraine, framing increased support as an urgent security and reconstruction priority. This view positions Ukraine assistance as a non-negotiable line that should shape overall allocations.

  • EU will need to increase Ukraine funding to plug gap — Merz

Domestic politics shaping allocation choices

Commentary stresses that national electoral dynamics, especially fears about the rise of figures like Marine Le Pen in France, are influencing how states approach EU funding decisions. Political risk is seen as distorting negotiations and could bias allocations toward defensive or strategic priorities.

  • Sigrid Melchior: Fear of Le Pen could decide the contest for the EU's funds

Costa-led proposal and year-end push

Reports from the presiding leadership focus on António Costa taking charge of budget talks and drafting a new multiannual proposal intended to secure agreement by year-end. These pieces note both the ambition to close talks quickly and specific objections from partners that may complicate that timeline.
