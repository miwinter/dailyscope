Clashes over university funding and crowd-control in Argentina

Argentina Protests Clashes 2026-10-16 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • Mass protests in Buenos Aires targeted President Milei's university funding cuts and education reforms, involving students, staff, unions, and opposition activists.
  • Violent clashes occurred between some demonstrators and security forces, leading to tear gas, water cannon use, and multiple detentions.
  • Coverage highlights political tensions and social unrest that may undermine Milei's support despite economic improvements, with similar unrest seen regionally.

Mass protests erupted in Buenos Aires against President Javier Milei's cuts to public university funding and education reforms, drawing thousands of students, staff, unions and opposition activists. While many marches remained peaceful, smaller groups of hooded demonstrators clashed with security forces, prompting the use of tear gas, water cannons and multiple detentions. International coverage varies: some outlets highlight a heavy-handed security response and allegations of repression, while others emphasize that most demonstrations were orderly with isolated violent elements. Commentators also warn that social unrest over spending cuts could undercut Milei's political standing despite reported macroeconomic improvements, and similar dispersals have been noted elsewhere in the region.
