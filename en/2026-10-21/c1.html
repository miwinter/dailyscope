France in International News

Summary of articles about France in the international press on 2026-10-21, based on 116 articles collected.

In brief
  • France faces widespread unrest with protests, riots, government-imposed curfews, and military deployments to restore order.
  • The unrest stems from economic struggles, youth dissatisfaction, political divides, and sparks shifts favoring the far right.
  • President Macron balances domestic crisis management with international diplomacy to secure energy amid political and social turbulence.

France is experiencing significant social unrest marked by widespread protests, riots, and looting across major cities, prompting the government to impose curfews and deploy troops to restore order. The unrest is driven by complex grievances including economic hardship, youth discontent, and political divisions, with the government blaming radical left groups and facing criticism over security failures. Meanwhile, President Macron is engaging in international diplomacy to secure energy supplies amid the crisis, while the political landscape shifts with the far right gaining influence amid calls for stronger law enforcement and public order.
