King Harald V: state funeral and nationwide mourning

Norway: Royal Funeral 2026-08-31 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • King Harald V of Norway died at age 89, marking an end of an era and prompting national mourning.
  • A state funeral is set for September 9 at Oslo Cathedral with coordinated national and royal family arrangements.
  • Domestic memorials and international condolences highlight widespread tribute and diplomatic solidarity.

King Harald V of Norway has died at age 89, prompting national mourning and reflections on his reform-minded, stabilizing role in modernizing the monarchy. The royal household has set a state funeral for September 9 at Oslo Cathedral, with arrangements coordinated by officials and the royal family. Norwegians gathered for masses and remembrance services, including packed church services and public participation in tribute events. International leaders have sent condolences, highlighting diplomatic ties and solidarity during Norway’s period of loss. Crown Prince Haakon has publicly paid tribute as the country prepares ceremonial and state protocols for the succession and funeral.
