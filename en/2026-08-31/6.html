Lake Ontario renaming dispute on Google Maps

Canada–US Mapping Dispute 2026-08-31 • 16 articles in total

In brief
  • Google Maps shows 'Lake America' for Lake Ontario to U.S. users following a decision linked to President Trump.
  • The renaming aligns U.S. maps with official U.S. geographic sources but causes Canadian protests and diplomatic objections.
  • The split labeling raises issues in cross-border mapping consistency and highlights political influence on digital map data.

Multiple news outlets report that Google Maps displays the name 'Lake America' (or similar variants) for Lake Ontario on maps shown to users in the United States after a decision attributed to President Donald Trump. The change is region‑specific: many reports note the traditional 'Lake Ontario' label remains visible to Canadian users, producing a split in digital cartography across the border. Canadian authorities, users and some media have protested, and at least one incident showed Canadian government pages pulling map data that adopted the U.S. label. Google and some reports say the update aligns U.S. official geographic sources (such as GNIS) or resulted from platform data rules, raising questions about the political influence of naming on global mapping services.
