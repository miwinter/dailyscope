Regional bilateral deals advance security, trade and practical cooperation

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-10-07 • 72 articles in total

In brief
  • Bilateral agreements cover defence, trade, infrastructure, air links, and sectoral cooperation to boost security and economic ties.
  • Diplomatic efforts include high-level visits, credential exchanges, and cooperation on cross-border crime, humanitarian aid, and development.
  • Disputes over sovereignty and resource management coexist with deeper ties to external powers and strategic policy adjustments.

Across the region, bilateral agreements reported this period span defence and security pacts, trade and connectivity projects, and pragmatic sectoral cooperation. Governments formalized deployments, joint-defence commitments and defence aid while also concluding trade, digital and infrastructure deals and expanding air links to boost commerce and mobility. High-level visits, credential exchanges and sectoral memoranda reinforced diplomatic ties, even as customs training, policing cooperation and a small number of humanitarian accords addressed immediate cross-border challenges. Simultaneously, disputes over sovereignty, resource management and diplomatic expulsions — and the growing role of outside powers — underline how strategic calculations continue to shape bilateral diplomacy.
