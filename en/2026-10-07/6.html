Mass student protests in France trigger clashes and government crisis response

France Protests: Clashes 2026-10-07 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • Mass student protests in France and Belgium led to violent clashes with police using tear gas and stun grenades.
  • President Macron held emergency meetings as the government defended police actions amid national debate on force use.
  • The unrest spread regionally, drawing international attention and comparisons with similar violent demonstrations elsewhere.

Mass student-led protests have spread across France, drawing hundreds of thousands into the streets and even spilling over into Belgium. Demonstrations repeatedly clashed with riot police, who used tear gas, stun grenades and charges; reports describe numerous injuries, including a 15-year-old who lost a hand. President Macron convened urgent and crisis meetings as ministers coordinated a response, while the government has defended police actions and rejected charges of repression. The events have intensified a national debate over policing practices and political fallout, while attracting international attention and comparisons with unrest elsewhere.
