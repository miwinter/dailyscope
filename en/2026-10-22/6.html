Midterms: Tactics, Turnout and Influence

US Elections: Midterms 2026-10-22 • 10 articles in total

In brief
  • The midterm campaign uses increasingly negative tactics, including personal attacks and fear-based foreign unrest imagery.
  • Trump's rallies are energetic and central to the GOP, with the congressional vote critical to his political future.
  • Polls show declining Trump approval and Hispanic support, while billionaires' spending and dark money heavily influence the election.

The midterm campaign has seen increasingly negative and controversial tactics, from personal attacks alleging childhood abuse to offensive political ads and the use of foreign unrest footage to stoke fear. Donald Trump’s rallies remain energetic and central to the Republican effort, while the outcome of the congressional vote is widely framed as decisive for his political future. Polls show Trump approval slipping and Hispanic voters drifting away from Republicans, creating significant headwinds for GOP hopes. Early voting in pivotal states such as Texas is being closely watched for its potential to shape national outcomes. At the same time, record spending by billionaires and opaque 'dark money', coupled with threats from powerful private figures, highlight worries that wealth and influence will heavily shape both messaging and post-election consequences.
