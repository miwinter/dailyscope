Pope to Undergo Lung Surgery; Vatican Calls It Precautionary

Pope Health Surgery 2026-10-12 • 33 articles in total

In brief
  • The pope will have routine surgery to remove an early-stage lung nodule detected during a checkup.
  • The operation's timing may affect his upcoming Latin America trip, but recovery is expected to be short.
  • Church leaders are calling for prayers and support as the Vatican prepares to provide updates.

Vatican officials announced that the Pope will undergo surgery to remove a small pulmonary nodule detected during routine checks. Authorities describe the lesion as early-stage and the procedure as precautionary, offering few medical details while stressing the pontiff remains in good spirits. Several reports note an expected short recovery — about a week — though sources differ on whether the operation will affect a planned Latin America trip. Catholic leaders and bishops have called for prayers and solidarity as the Holy See prepares to provide updates as needed.
