Netanyahu's Coalition Prospects

Israel Elections: Netanyahu Coalition 2026-10-29 • 76 articles in total

In brief
  • Netanyahu's right-wing bloc leads but lacks majority; Arab parties gained influence, complicating coalition formation.
  • President urges negotiated coalition to avoid new elections amid fragmented political landscape.
  • Final government depends on small parties, late vote counts, and strategic alliances across blocs.

Israel’s election produced a fragmented Knesset: Benjamin Netanyahu’s right‑wing bloc leads many tallies but falls short of the 61‑seat majority needed to govern outright. Arab parties performed strongly and may become kingmakers, amplifying the leverage of centrist and opposition blocs. That leaves Netanyahu likely to remain caretaker prime minister while protracted coalition talks and bargaining over portfolios unfold, with the president pressing to avoid another vote. Analysts outline multiple scenarios — from a right‑leaning alliance with swing partners to a unity government or fresh elections — with outcomes hinging on small parties and late ballot counts.
