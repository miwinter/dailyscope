Flávio Bolsonaro's US-based presidential claim

Brazil: Bolsonaro Presidency Claim 2026-10-29 • 33 articles in total

In brief
  • Senator Flávio Bolsonaro fled to the US and declared himself Brazil's rightful president, alleging election fraud with no evidence.
  • He revised his claims from "president-elect" to "leader," while Brazilian authorities treated his escape and claims as disruptive.
  • Reactions varied internationally; some supported him, others distanced themselves, and Brazil's government dismissed his claims as absurd.

Brazil’s political crisis escalated as Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, after leaving the country, declared from the United States that he is the legitimately elected president or ‘‘leader’’ of Brazil, denouncing the vote as stolen without providing evidence. He published and then revised open letters, at times downgrading his title from ‘‘president-elect’’ to ‘‘leader,’’ while family members and allies publicized his departure and reunion in the US. The moves prompted mixed international reactions — some political figures and religious groups signaled support, while others, including former US President Trump, publicly avoided direct endorsement or distanced themselves. Brazil’s government and legal authorities have treated the episode as disruptive, with reports of flight, visa arrangements and possible legal consequences intensifying the standoff.

The Lula administration dismissed the claims as absurd and transitory, and international media highlighted the diplomatic and regional implications of the Bolsonaro camp’s maneuvers.
