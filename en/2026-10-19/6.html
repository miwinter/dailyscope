Priest killed by falling crucifix during Mass in Guayaquil

Ecuador Priest Death at Mass 2026-10-19 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • A priest in Guayaquil died when a large wooden crucifix fell on him during Mass, and the event was recorded or broadcast live.
  • Emergency services responded promptly, investigations were opened, and communities called for safety reviews in places of worship.
  • The deceased was identified as Patricio Alfredo Sánchez Molinari of the Redemptorists at Perpetuo Socorro church.

A priest in Guayaquil, Ecuador, died when a large wooden crucifix or cross fell on him while he was leading a Mass. Multiple reports note the incident was captured on video or broadcast live, and footage circulated as emergency responders arrived. Authorities have opened investigations and local communities expressed shock, grief and calls to review safety and installation protocols in places of worship. One report identified the priest as Patricio Alfredo Sánchez Molinari of the Redemptorists serving at Perpetuo Socorro church.
