Monaco bombing: suspect found dead amid cross-border probe

Monaco Bombing Investigation 2026-07-08 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • A woman suspected of bombing in Monaco that injured a Ukrainian oligarch was found shot dead in Ukraine.
  • Monaco and Ukrainian authorities arrested accomplices and discovered a torture chamber linked to the attack.
  • Investigations continue across borders to uncover motives and networks behind the bombing.

Reports converge that a woman suspected of carrying out a bombing in Monaco that gravely injured a Ukrainian oligarch has been found shot dead in Ukraine. Monaco and Ukrainian investigators say the main suspect — named in some reports as Anastasiia Berezovska — was killed and that arrests of alleged accomplices and the discovery of a torture chamber have followed. Other international outlets offer briefer accounts, noting the suspect’s death, possible links to an attack on oligarch Vadym/Vadim Yermolaiev and continuing manhunts, while some sources report a separate 25-year-old found dead and identify a shooter. Authorities in Monaco and Ukraine continue to probe motives, connections and the wider network behind the attack, with many details still emerging.

Monaco and Ukrainian investigative perspective

Monaco-based reporting focuses on concrete investigative developments: the alleged main suspect (named in some pieces), arrests of two alleged accomplices including an emergency services member, and the uncovering of a torture chamber in Ukraine. These sources emphasize evidence gathering, victim conditions (the oligarch’s recovery and another person critically injured) and efforts to map the network and motives behind the Monaco bombing.

Lithuanian follow-up on cross-border killings

Lithuanian outlets highlight follow-up developments in Ukraine tied to the Monaco attack, reporting that a Ukrainian woman was found dead and that investigators have identified who shot the suspect, with mention of a 25-year-old also found dead. Their coverage stresses the cross-border trajectory of violence and ongoing investigative links between events in Monaco and Ukraine.

International brief reports: suspect found dead, few details

A range of international outlets provide concise accounts that a suspect in the Monaco bombing — often described as a woman — was found shot dead in Ukraine, offering limited detail and no consistent identification. These reports underline the international implications, the manhunt after a bomb injured a Ukrainian tycoon, and that probes by Monaco and Ukrainian authorities are ongoing.
