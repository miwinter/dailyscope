Sword attack at Swedish high school leaves one dead; suspect held

Sweden School Attack 2026-08-22 • 31 articles in total

In brief
  • An 18-year-old attacker struck Brinell High School in Sweden with a sword, killing one and injuring several as students returned to class.
  • Police shot and detained the suspect at the scene while emergency services evacuated and treated multiple victims.
  • Investigators are probing the motive and possible extremist links amid renewed debate on school security and emergency preparedness.

A sword-wielding attacker struck Brinell High School in Fagersta, central Sweden, killing one person and injuring several others as students returned to class. Reports identify the suspect as an 18-year-old and many outlets describe them as a former student; authorities arrested or detained the individual at the scene. Police used force in some accounts (the attacker was reportedly shot and then taken into custody) and emergency services evacuated and treated multiple victims, with some described as seriously injured. Journalists and witnesses described chaotic rescue scenes, including injured people being helped through windows, while investigators continue to probe motive and any possible links to extremism. The incident renewed national and international debate about school safety, security measures and emergency preparedness in Sweden.
