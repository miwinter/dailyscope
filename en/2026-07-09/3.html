Coverage of Donald Trump’s conduct at the Ankara NATO summit and related policy moves is dominated by tensions with European allies over burden‑sharing, a high‑profile dispute with Spain and renewed talk about Greenland. At the same time Trump announced concrete defense measures for Ukraine — including licenses for Patriot production — which many outlets framed as a win for Kyiv even as some warned about credibility and follow‑through. Reporting also focused on his travel and security choices, repeated verbal gaffes, and abrupt shifts on Middle East policy such as delisting Syria and ending ceasefires. Domestic legal and immigration developments (damages awards, birthright citizenship disputes, and new green card rules) were widely reported as adding political strain. Overall the coverage portrays a presidency oscillating between confrontational tactics with allies, transactional defense support for partners, and ongoing domestic and legal controversies.
