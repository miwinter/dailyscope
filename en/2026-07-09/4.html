Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-07-09 • 84 articles in total

In brief
  • Bilateral ties focus on security partnerships, economic agreements, and development projects amid ongoing border disputes and migration issues.
  • Trade relations involve both cooperation through agreements and tensions from unilateral threats, affecting economic predictability.
  • Diplomatic efforts combine military cooperation, regional infrastructure, technology transfers, and soft-power initiatives to strengthen partnerships and regional integration.

Recent bilateral activity highlights a mix of security partnerships, economic agreements and persistent cross‑border frictions. Western ties with Ukraine and Turkey dominate defence diplomacy, with licenses and arms cooperation advancing alongside broader NATO discussions. Trade and investment pacts proceed even as transatlantic trade tensions and unilateral threats complicate economic relations. Regional connectivity projects, energy deals and people‑to‑people initiatives underpin development diplomacy across Africa, Asia and the Pacific, while border disputes and migration issues continue to test neighbourly relations.
