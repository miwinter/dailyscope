Italy approves Meloni's electoral law

Italy Electoral Law Approval 2026-10-09 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • Parliament approved a new electoral law with a plurality bonus to create clearer majorities and stabilize governance before Italy's elections.
  • Supporters say the law strengthens Prime Minister Meloni's coalition, while critics warn it distorts representation and entrenches power.
  • The reform sparked intense debate and is expected to reshape election campaigns and political balance.

Parliament has approved a new electoral law that introduces a plurality bonus and other mechanisms to produce a clearer parliamentary majority ahead of Italy's next general elections. Proponents and some analysts say the reform will stabilize governance and consolidate Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni’s governing coalition, strengthening her position before the vote. Critics argue the bonus—awarded to parties surpassing a high threshold—distorts representation and is designed to entrench the incumbent government. The passage followed intense debate and negotiation, with opponents calling it a controversial power play while supporters call it a milestone reform. Observers expect the change to reshape campaign strategies and the balance of power in the run-up to the election.
