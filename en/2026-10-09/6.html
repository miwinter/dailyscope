U.S. indicts Maduro for alleged torture of opponents

Venezuela: Maduro Torture Plot 2026-10-09 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • U.S. prosecutors indicted Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife for ordering torture and illegal detention of political opponents.
  • The charges include narcotics trafficking and carry severe legal penalties, escalating U.S.-Venezuela diplomatic tensions.
  • International observers and opposition call for accountability amid concerns of systemic repression and political motives.

U.S. prosecutors have filed a superseding indictment accusing Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife Cilia Flores of ordering the torture and illegal detention of political opponents, including U.S. citizens. The expanded charges form part of broader criminal cases that also reference narcotics trafficking and carry severe potential penalties. International and regional observers, opposition figures, and human rights advocates are calling for accountability and transparency, while Caracas rejects the allegations. The developments risk escalating diplomatic tensions between the United States and Venezuela and could have significant legal and political consequences for Maduro's government.
