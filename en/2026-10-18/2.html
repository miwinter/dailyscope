Nationwide cost-of-living protests in France escalate into violent clashes

France Protests Clashes 2026-10-18 • 95 articles in total

In brief
  • Hundreds of thousands protested in France against rising living costs, causing widespread violence and property damage.
  • Authorities condemned the unrest, detained many protesters, and debated policing methods amid reports of injuries.
  • The protests reflected deep social discontent and sparked broader ideological and political discussions.

Mass demonstrations over rising living costs, initially driven by students and broader social movements, drew hundreds of thousands of people across France. Many rallies escalated into violent confrontations with police, producing widespread arson, looting and damage to public buildings — notably the Limoges city hall. Authorities detained hundreds to more than a thousand protesters and reported numerous police injuries, while government leaders publicly condemned the violence and pledged prosecutions. The events also sparked scrutiny of policing tactics after multiple reports of severe injuries to demonstrators, deepening political debate over public order and proportionality of force.
