Across regions, states reported a spate of bilateral agreements and commitments spanning infrastructure upgrades, energy and trade ties, security cooperation, and cultural exchanges. Numerous visits and signed documents signal renewed diplomatic momentum, with leaders emphasizing implementation and follow-through on economic, environmental and technological initiatives.
