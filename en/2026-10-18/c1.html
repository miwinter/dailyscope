France in International News

Summary of articles about France in the international press on 2026-10-18, based on 132 articles collected.

In brief
  • France faces widespread violent protests over rising living costs causing arrests and property damage including historic buildings.
  • French leaders condemn violence, pledge strict legal action, and emphasize peaceful protests and public order.
  • Israeli settlers attacked French and European officials in the West Bank, triggering diplomatic tensions and calls for accountability.

France is experiencing extensive nationwide protests primarily driven by rising living costs, leading to violent clashes, significant property damage including historic buildings set on fire, and thousands of arrests. The government, led by President Macron and other officials, has condemned the violence and vowed strict legal action against perpetrators while emphasizing the importance of peaceful demonstrations. Concurrently, diplomatic tensions have escalated due to attacks by Israeli settlers on a French mayor and European diplomats in the occupied West Bank, prompting international condemnation and calls for accountability. These events highlight deep social, economic, and political challenges facing France domestically and in its foreign relations.
