Former anti-corruption prosecutor killed amid concerns over political-criminal links

Guatemala Prosecutor Killings 2026-10-18 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • A former Guatemalan anti-corruption prosecutor was shot dead while playing football, sparking national concern over violence against judicial figures.
  • His murder may be linked to his accusations of ties between politicians and criminal gangs and highlights risks faced by officials exposing corruption.
  • Authorities have arrested two suspects and continue investigating, but motives and suspect details remain undisclosed.

A former Guatemalan anti-corruption prosecutor was shot dead while playing football, a killing that has drawn national shock and renewed concern about violence targeting judicial figures. Several reports highlight his prior accusations of ties between politicians and criminal gangs, suggesting the murder could be connected to his anti-corruption work. Authorities have opened investigations and, in one report, announced the arrest of two suspects, though motives and identities remain largely undisclosed. The incident underscores a pattern of threats against prosecutors and raises urgent questions about protection, accountability, and impunity in Guatemala.
