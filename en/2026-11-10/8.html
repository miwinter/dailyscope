France accuses Iran of supermarket fridge cyberattack

France–Iran Refrigeration Cyberattack 2026-11-10 • 18 articles in total

In brief
  • French authorities accuse Iran and the IRGC of cyberattacks targeting supermarket refrigeration, risking food spoilage and safety.
  • France calls the attacks unprecedented, urges coordinated EU sanctions, linking them to broader Iran-West cyber tensions.
  • The events highlight vulnerabilities in connected infrastructure and the urgent need for international cyber norms.

French authorities have accused Iran, and in some statements the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, of orchestrating a large-scale cyberattack that disrupted supermarket refrigeration systems and spoiled perishable goods. Paris called the campaign unprecedented, warned it could threaten food supplies and consumer safety, and said it would pursue coordinated European sanctions. Reporting links the incidents to similar outages in other European countries and places them in the context of escalating cyber tensions between Iran and Western states. Many pieces emphasize diplomatic and security implications but excerpts generally do not provide detailed forensic evidence for the attribution. The episode underlines growing concerns about the vulnerability of everyday connected infrastructure and the need for international cyber norms.
