Impeachment proceedings have been launched in the U.S. Congress targeting the Defense Secretary, with reports variously identifying the official as Pete Hegseth or Lloyd Austin. The effort was initiated by Republican lawmakers amid allegations tied to U.S. operations in Iran, Yemen and Venezuela, but coverage stresses procedural uncertainty about whether the bid will advance. Some outlets describe alternative remedies such as censure or recall-like motions, while others emphasize the vote timetable and potential consequences for national security leadership. The dispute is framed as part of wider partisan tensions within the GOP and draws attention to Trump-aligned reactions and intra-party divisions.
