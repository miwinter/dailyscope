Flydubai diversion after cockpit attack — passengers and crews avert crash

Flydubai Diversion Incident 2026-10-01 • 58 articles in total

In brief
  • A Flydubai flight was diverted to Saudi Arabia after a crew member stabbed another and tried to seize control, but passengers and crew restrained the attacker.
  • Israeli authorities called it a foiled hijacking attempt and launched security investigations, while Saudi authorities began their own probe after the emergency landing.
  • Flydubai suspended flights to Israel and reviewed safety procedures amid ongoing investigations and public debate about cockpit security.

A Flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv was diverted to Saudi Arabia after a violent cockpit incident in which one crew member stabbed another and an apparent attempt was made to seize control of the aircraft. Passengers, reserve crew and at least one wounded pilot intervened to restrain the attacker, and the plane landed safely with no confirmed passenger fatalities. Israeli authorities and security services described the episode as a foiled attempt to crash or hijack the aircraft, while Saudi investigators and local authorities opened probes after the emergency landing in Tabuk. Flydubai temporarily suspended flights to Israel and said it was reviewing safety procedures as international media and experts replayed footage, passenger testimony and debated measures such as cockpit recording. Many reports emphasize heroism and urgent questions remain about motive, verification of claims and procedural safeguards for future flights.
