Regional diplomacy: a patchwork of bilateral deepening and strategic alignments

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-10-01 • 102 articles in total

In brief
  • Global bilateral agreements span infrastructure, trade, security, education, and capacity building across multiple regions and partners.
  • Diplomatic engagement involves cooperation alongside tensions, migration issues, and legal disputes, reflecting complex international relations.
  • Key actors include China, Gulf states, Europe, and regional powers pursuing economic growth, defense ties, and technological partnerships.

This set of reports shows a broad spate of bilateral agreements and cooperative initiatives across regions, from infrastructure and investment to security and training. China, Gulf states and European partners are prominent actors expanding economic, educational and defence ties with smaller partners, while Central Asian governments pursue connectivity and technology partnerships with Europe and Russia. African states focused on trade facilitation, major industrial projects and anti‑fraud or governance cooperation, and Pacific and small island states deepened training and development links with regional powers. At the same time several articles show diplomatic friction and migration or legal disputes alongside high‑profile cultural and ceremonial exchanges, underscoring that bilateral engagement mixes cooperation, competition and bilateral tensions.
