Tel Aviv-bound flight diverted after cockpit attack and emergency landing

Aviation: Tel Aviv Emergency Landing 2026-10-01 • 11 articles in total

In brief
  • A Dubai-to-Tel Aviv flight diverted to Saudi Arabia after a violent cockpit incident involving one pilot attacking the captain.
  • Israeli officials called the attack deliberate and praised crew members for preventing disaster and ensuring passenger safety.
  • Investigations are underway by aviation authorities and the airline amid security and procedural concerns.

A Dubai-to-Tel Aviv passenger jet made an emergency diversion and landed in Saudi Arabia following a violent cockpit incident. Multiple reports describe a confrontation in which one pilot allegedly attacked or stabbed the captain and attempted to take control, producing a large and rapid descent. Israeli officials, including the prime minister, have characterized the act as deliberate and credited the co-pilot who intervened or the other crew members with averting disaster; passengers and most crew were reported safe. Aviation authorities and the airline have opened investigations and are cooperating while security and cockpit-procedure questions are being raised.
