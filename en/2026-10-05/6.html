Nationalist Surge in Bosnia's Elections

Bosnia Elections: Nationalism Rise 2026-10-05 • 17 articles in total

In brief
  • Bosnia's elections installed a tripartite presidency with nationalist leaders across ethnic lines and strong social democrat support.
  • Milorad Dodik and allies won in Republika Srpska, expressing gratitude to foreign leaders, indicating external influence on Bosnia's politics.
  • Observers warn that nationalist gains and foreign backing threaten reforms, deepen ethnic divisions, and complicate Bosnia's EU accession.

Bosnia and Herzegovina's elections saw strong performances from nationalist parties across ethnic lines while social democrats maintained significant support, producing a new tripartite presidency of Cvijanović (Serb), Bećirović (Bosniak) and Filipović (Croat). Milorad Dodik and allied candidates celebrated victories in Republika Srpska and publicly thanked foreign figures such as Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Aleksandar Vučić, underscoring external influence in domestic politics. Observers warn that nationalist gains and foreign backing could stall reforms, deepen ethnic divisions and complicate Bosnia's EU accession prospects. Preliminary counts leave some outcomes unresolved, and the death of Bosnian Serb leader Biljana Plavsic adds historical resonance to the current nationalist turn.
