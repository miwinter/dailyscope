Deadly church shooting in Karak, Jordan

Jordan Church Shooting 2026-10-24 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • Armed men attacked a Christian church during a funeral in Karak, Jordan, killing at least seven and wounding dozens.
  • Jordanian authorities secured the area, launched investigations, and vowed strong responses to the unprecedented attack.
  • The incident heightened concerns about sectarian tensions and regional security around religious sites.

Armed men opened fire at a Christian church in Karak, Jordan—reports say the attack occurred during a funeral and left at least seven people dead with dozens more wounded, though some outlets gave slightly different casualty counts. Jordanian security forces cordoned off the area, launched investigations, and pledged to pursue the perpetrators. King Abdullah II and officials vowed a strong response, and authorities increased protections around religious sites amid heightened concern. The incident is described as rare and unprecedented for the kingdom and has raised broader worries about sectarian tensions and regional security.
