Bilateral Deals: security pacts, China outreach and connectivity gains

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-10-24 • 82 articles in total

In brief
  • Recent bilateral agreements focus on security pacts, China-led development, and trade and connectivity initiatives.
  • The Libya–Turkey maritime deal caused regional opposition and highlighted geopolitical conflicts.
  • Bilateral relations include defense cooperation, economic growth efforts, mobility facilitation, and diplomatic tensions.

Recent bilateral agreements show a mix of security deepening, China-led development outreach, and efforts to boost trade and connectivity. Governments signed defense and security pacts across regions while launching infrastructure, education and environmental cooperation to strengthen ties. The Libya–Turkey maritime deal prompted strong regional pushback and highlighted geopolitical fault lines in the eastern Mediterranean. Simultaneously, multiple states advanced mobility and trade initiatives — from new air links and free-trade talks to logistics and energy commitments — alongside targeted development projects in small island and neighbouring states.
