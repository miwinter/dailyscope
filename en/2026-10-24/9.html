7.2-magnitude quake off Vanuatu prompts tsunami alerts

Vanuatu Earthquake Tsunami Warning 2026-10-24 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • A magnitude 7.2 earthquake struck off Vanuatu about 200 km from nearby islands.
  • Tsunami warnings were issued within a 300 km radius and coastal communities were advised to monitor.
  • No immediate casualties or damage were reported as authorities continue monitoring aftershocks and impacts.

A powerful earthquake struck off Vanuatu, with several outlets reporting a magnitude around 7.2 and an epicentre roughly 200 km from nearby islands including Port-Olry. Regional agencies, including the Pacific Tsunami Warning Center, issued tsunami warnings and advised coastal communities to monitor for possible waves. One report highlighted a sequence of strong tremors (magnitudes 6.9 and 6.3) and specified warnings within a 300 km radius, underscoring concerns about aftershocks and localized impacts. No immediate casualties or damage were reported, and authorities continue to monitor and update assessments.
