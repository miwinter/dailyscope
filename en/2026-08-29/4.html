Escalation, strikes and international alarm over the war in Ukraine

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-29 • 64 articles in total

In brief
  • Ukraine is escalating drone strikes and offensive operations to pressure Russia and acknowledges peace is unlikely soon.
  • Russia intensifies attacks, tightens domestic controls, and faces manpower shortages amid ongoing conflict and information efforts.
  • International actors monitor nuclear risks, address humanitarian impacts, warn of escalation, and note use of autonomous weapons and covert operations.

Reports show a marked escalation in the conflict: Ukrainian authorities, led by President Zelenskyy, have ordered a massive expansion of long‑range drone strikes—up to 1,000 sorties per day—while Kyiv has continued attacks on Russian infrastructure and refineries. Russia has intensified strikes across Ukraine, damaging food storage and civilian facilities and prompting humanitarian concern and displacement. International monitors including the IAEA continue to track risks at sites like Zaporizhzhia and report no abnormal radiation while urging ongoing vigilance. Western leaders and intelligence officials warn of possible further escalation and strain on military stocks, and analysts highlight mounting manpower pressures, the rise of autonomous weapons, and broader geopolitical ripple effects.
