France probes Normandy train derailment as potential sabotage

France Rail Sabotage Probe 2026-09-13 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • A passenger train derailed on the Rouen–Caen route, injuring 44 of 186 passengers and triggering a mass emergency response.
  • French investigators launched a criminal probe considering possible sabotage after an object was found on the tracks, emphasizing rail security concerns.
  • Authorities have not named suspects and state there is no clear proof of an attack as investigations and service suspensions continue.

A passenger train on the Rouen–Caen route derailed in Normandy, injuring 44 of the 186 people on board and triggering a mass emergency response. French investigators have opened a criminal probe and are treating a malicious act as a possible cause after fragments or an object were reportedly found on the track. Several reports emphasize heightened concerns about rail security and the implications for critical infrastructure, while authorities have not named any suspects. At least one outlet notes officials saying there are currently no clear indications of an attack, underscoring that inquiries remain ongoing as services are paused and evidence is analyzed.
