Powerful Panama earthquakes: major shocks, widespread damage, active aftershocks

Panama Earthquake Seismicity 2026-10-10 • 17 articles in total

In brief
  • Powerful earthquakes around magnitude 7.7 struck Panama with numerous significant aftershocks and caused widespread structural damage and evacuations.
  • Seismologists monitored shallow quakes near plate boundaries, issued and adjusted tsunami alerts based on ongoing aftershock activity.
  • Emergency teams conducted rescue operations and safety checks while media covered live reactions and authorities communicated threat levels.

A series of powerful earthquakes struck Panama, with major events widely reported around magnitude 7.7 and multiple significant aftershocks including a reported 6.6. Reports describe widespread structural damage — collapsed houses, damaged roads and buildings — and large-scale evacuations while emergency teams assess impacts. The USGS and seismologists tracked dozens to hundreds of aftershocks, issued and then adjusted tsunami alerts, and noted the quakes occurred at shallow depths near plate boundaries. Media coverage captured both dramatic first-hand scenes and live aftershock footage, while authorities coordinated relief and continuity checks (including on the Panama Canal). Casualty totals remained unclear in early reports as assessments continued across affected communities.
