Threats Scale Across Domains: Aviation plots, regional wars and military build-up

Security: Threat Scaling 2026-10-10 • 74 articles in total

In brief
  • Global security threats are rising, including aviation attacks, Horn of Africa conflict escalation, and heightened military readiness with advanced weapons testing.
  • Non-kinetic risks grow with cyberattacks, AI misuse, and vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure impacting supply chains and services.
  • Domestic unrest manifests as political violence, prison riots, and crises while states address transnational influence and local security challenges.

Recent reporting shows a multi‑vector increase in security threats worldwide: high‑profile aviation plots and in‑flight attacks have prompted international aviation and law‑enforcement scrutiny, while the Horn of Africa conflict risks spilling over as Eritrean involvement and drone strikes escalate. Major powers and regional actors are accelerating military readiness and weapons testing — from hypersonic trials to force deployments — as governments cite deterrence and prevention. Cyber incidents, AI misuse and emerging risks to undersea and surveillance infrastructure highlight expanding non‑kinetic vulnerabilities that affect supply chains and critical services. Meanwhile domestic political violence, prison riots and hostage crises underscore that internal security pressures are simultaneous and interacting with broader geopolitical tensions.
