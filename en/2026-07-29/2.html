In brief
  • U.S.-hosted talks aligned allies on Iran, regional security, and Ukraine’s air defense while managing differences over military options.

  • Diplomatic efforts included managing maritime incidents, regional summits, and bilateral tensions to sustain cooperation amid political turmoil.

World leaders converged in Washington and other capitals for a series of high‑level talks, with U.S. meetings hosting Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu and Ukrainian President Zelensky to coordinate on Iran, regional security and Patriot air‑defense cooperation. Meanwhile, China continued to expand influence through training and development initiatives, prompting parallel capacity‑building and counter‑influence moves by other states. Regional summits, multilateral mediation and ad‑hoc diplomatic contacts sought to manage flashpoints from maritime incidents to bilateral political spats, underscoring a mix of cooperation and tension in contemporary diplomacy.
