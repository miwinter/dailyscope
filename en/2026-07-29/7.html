Heightened terror risks and security debates across Europe

Europe Terror Threat 2026-07-29 • 15 articles in total

In brief
  • A deadly attack at Berlin Pride linked to ISIS prompted investigations and tougher counter-extremism measures in Germany.
  • Europe faces rising regional tensions with drone incursions near NATO borders and security threats in Poland.
  • Far-right activism and political instability increase threats to public safety and social cohesion across Europe.

Europe is confronting a wave of high-profile security incidents, highlighted by a deadly attack at Berlin's Pride event where investigators say the suspect pledged allegiance to ISIS and was killed after a police confrontation. German authorities are pursuing investigations while announcing tougher counter-extremism measures that have prompted debate about surveillance and civil liberties. Security experts warn the overall terror threat remains active and note practical limits to continuous monitoring of large numbers of suspects. At the same time, regional tensions have risen with drone incursions near NATO borders and a series of political threats and unexploded devices in Poland, set against broader warnings about far-right activism and political instability across the continent.
