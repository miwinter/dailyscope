Surge in bilateral deals across security, infrastructure and diplomacy

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-07-29 • 87 articles in total

In brief
  • Multiple regions report increased bilateral agreements focusing on security, infrastructure, energy, and trade partnerships amid geopolitical competition.
  • Several territorial disputes are resolving while diplomatic representation and high-level visits deepen international ties and economic cooperation.
  • Smaller states pursue diverse donors and partners, balancing major power influences and emphasizing pragmatic economic growth and stability.

This set of articles shows a broad uptick in bilateral agreements and diplomatic engagement across regions, from Pacific security and development partnerships to Eurasian defense-industrial cooperation and African border settlements. Key themes include security cooperation and arms or defense-industrial ties, infrastructure and energy projects backed by foreign partners, and financial measures to boost investment and trade. Several states are resolving territorial disputes and deepening ties through high-level visits and new consulates, while smaller states pursue diverse donors and partners. Geopolitical competition between major powers (notably the US, China and Russia) underscores many agreements, even as regional players pursue pragmatic deals for economic growth and stability.
