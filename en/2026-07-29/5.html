Escalation, strikes and global diplomatic pressure in the Russo‑Ukrainian war

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-07-29 • 48 articles in total

In brief
  • Ukraine and Russia continue fighting with long-range strikes causing rising civilian harm and economic damage in Ukraine and Russia.
  • Western and regional actors increase diplomatic support for Ukraine while tensions and security concerns grow in neighboring countries.
  • Russia sustains its war effort via global logistics and recruitment amid fears of conflict escalation involving Iran and other external actors.

Fighting continues across multiple fronts as Ukraine and Russia exchange strikes, including high‑profile long‑range attacks deep inside Russian territory and attacks on Ukrainian ports and infrastructure. Civilian harm and economic damage are rising, prompting humanitarian concern and reports of the highest civilian casualties since 2022 alongside disruptions to everyday life in places like Odessa. Diplomacy and strategic planning intensify: Western governments and leaders are publicly backing Ukraine, the US is reviewing its European military posture, and regional actors such as Kazakhstan call for negotiations while Iran‑linked tensions raise fears of wider escalation. Russia is sustaining global logistics and recruitment efforts beyond the battlefield, and regional political frictions (notably involving Romania, Poland and neighboring states) add to the risk of further confrontation.
