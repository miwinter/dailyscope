Russia–Ukraine Attacks

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-26 • 18 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian strikes damage Ukrainian cities and infrastructure, causing civilian injuries and worsening energy shortages.
  • Ukraine targets Russian military and industrial sites, impairing equipment and supply chains amid ongoing conflict.
  • New drone and naval weapons heighten escalation risks, increasing dangers of regional spillover and diplomatic tensions.

Russian air and missile strikes continue to hit Ukrainian cities and critical infrastructure, causing civilian injuries and material damage. Ukraine has mounted cross‑border attacks on Russian military and industrial targets, including air bases, refineries and fertilizer plants, and claims substantial equipment losses. Energy systems on both sides are strained — with Ukrainian blackout risks and Russian fuel shortages — intensifying humanitarian needs. The growing use of drones and novel weapons raises the danger of further escalation and regional spillover.
