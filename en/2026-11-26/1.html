Great Power Rivalry — Contest for Order and Influence

Great Power Rivalry 2026-11-26 • 20 articles in total

In brief
  • The US, Russia, and China are reshaping global security through military, diplomatic, and economic actions, causing regional tensions and alliance shifts.
  • Moscow pursues prolonged conflict and annexation with high military budgets, while Sino-Russian cooperation grows amid some strategic rivalry.
  • Western and local powers in Indo-Pacific adjust force posture, raising concerns on militarisation and sovereignty, while smaller states face insecurity from great-power contests.

The three-way competition among the United States, Russia and China is reshaping regional security and the global order through military posturing, diplomatic realignments and economic signaling. Moscow’s preparations for a prolonged conflict and aggressive regional probing coexist with deepening Sino‑Russian cooperation and contrasting views of Beijing’s influence. In the Indo‑Pacific and Indian Ocean, Western powers and local states are recalibrating force posture and alliances, raising questions about militarisation and double standards. At the same time U.S. domestic actors and shifting policy tools complicate allied coordination, while peripheral partners and frontline European states face growing insecurity and strategic dilemmas.
