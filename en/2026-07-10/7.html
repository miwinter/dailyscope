Palestine schedules first legislative vote in 20 years

Palestine Elections Milestone 2026-07-10 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas set legislative elections for November 28, 2026, the first in over 20 years.
  • The elections aim to restore electoral legitimacy and stabilize Palestinian governance amid questions about logistics and Hamas participation.
  • International observers view the vote as a historic step for political renewal with uncertain impacts on regional relations and the peace process.

Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas has issued a presidential decree setting November 28, 2026 for legislative elections, the first such vote in more than two decades. The announcement has been framed as a milestone for political renewal, with international observers welcoming a step toward restoring electoral legitimacy. Major questions remain about logistics, eligibility, and whether Hamas will participate, as well as the role of external influences in shaping the contest. Regional and global actors are watching for impacts on governance, intra-Palestinian reconciliation, and the broader peace process.
