Escalating strikes and maritime clashes

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-23 • 50 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian strikes cause heavy civilian casualties and damage Kyiv's infrastructure, with ongoing rescue efforts amid repeated missile and drone attacks.
  • The conflict targets strategic energy and transport sites, including the Crimea bridge, raising regional stability and nuclear safety concerns.
  • Naval attacks on Russian ships and cross-border airspace breaches escalate regional tensions, with political disputes over Western arms supplies intensifying.

Russian offensive strikes continue to inflict heavy civilian casualties and damage to critical infrastructure across the Kyiv region, with multiple building collapses and rescue operations underway. The conflict has expanded beyond land strikes into maritime and cross-border incidents, including suspected drone and unmanned-boat attacks on Russian vessels and breaches of neighbouring airspaces. Attacks on energy and transport infrastructure — and retaliatory strikes such as those linked to the Crimea bridge — are raising broader concerns about regional stability and nuclear safety. Political rhetoric and disputes over Western arms deliveries accompany the fighting, underscoring the conflict’s strategic and humanitarian dimensions.
