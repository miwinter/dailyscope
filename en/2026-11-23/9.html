Israel coalition talks: marathon negotiations and recurring deadlocks

Israel Coalition Talks 2026-11-23 • 12 articles in total

In brief
  • Netanyahu and Eisenkot engage in marathon, exploratory talks to form a coalition amid tight deadlines.
  • Negotiations face repeated deadlocks, with no agreement reached and uncertainty about Netanyahu's future.
  • Fragile parliamentary math and opposition to minority government with Arab support risk prolonging stalemate.

Marathon negotiations between Benjamin Netanyahu and former IDF chief Gadi Eisenkot have dominated post-election efforts to form a government. Some outlets report invitations and the start of extended talks with all options on the table, while others describe resumed negotiations under explicit deadlines. Numerous reports record repeated deadlocks and sessions ending without a deal, keeping government formation uncertain and Netanyahu’s future in question. Observers warn the fragile parliamentary arithmetic — including opposition to a minority government reliant on Arab support — could prolong the stalemate.
