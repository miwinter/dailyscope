Capture of 'El Chapo Isidro'

  • Mexican forces captured cartel leader Fausto 'El Chapo Isidro' Meza Flores using advanced surveillance, international cooperation, and a high-profile Kingpin Removal operation.
  • The raid caused multiple deaths among soldiers, suspects, and civilians, highlighting the violent risks of targeting top cartel figures.
  • The arrest disrupts cartel dynamics but sparks ongoing violence, including reprisals against relatives of rival kingpins like Rafael Caro Quintero, amid US-Mexico law enforcement collaboration.
