New book revives Diana-era accusations as palace denies claims

UK Royal Family Scandal 2026-09-18 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • Earl Spencer's memoir alleges King Charles III reacted elatedly to Princess Diana's death, sparking international media controversy and palace denials.
  • Coverage extends to royal conspiracies involving a viral video and a suspicious banker’s death, keeping scrutiny on the monarchy.
  • Prince Harry's recent public appearances attract media attention, potentially diverting focus from palace controversies.

A new memoir by Earl Spencer and widespread international reporting have reignited allegations that King Charles III reacted with apparent elation to Princess Diana’s 1997 death, a claim circulated by multiple outlets. Buckingham Palace has issued firm denials, and several pieces frame the dispute as part of a broader contest between the press and the monarchy over narrative control. Separate coverage of a mysterious banker’s death and a viral video has reopened long-running conspiracies about possible royal links, keeping scrutiny on the institution beyond the book. Meanwhile, Prince Harry’s recent public appearances are receiving attention that may divert some public focus from palace controversies.
