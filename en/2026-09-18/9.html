Tači conviction and polarized regional reactions

Kosovo Tači Accountability 2026-09-18 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • Former Kosovo president Hashim Thaçi was convicted of war crimes and sentenced to 25 years by an EU-backed court.
  • The verdict caused deep divisions, with protests and political condemnation in Kosovo and among Albanians abroad.
  • The case highlights tensions in Kosovo and Europe, reflected in unrest and increased regional security concerns.

Former Kosovo president Hashim Thaçi was convicted of war crimes and sentenced to 25 years, in a case brought by the EU-backed Kosovo Specialist Prosecutor's Office in The Hague. The verdict has produced sharply divided responses at home and abroad, ranging from calls for justice to warnings that it will deepen ethnic and political tensions. Albanian lawmakers and prominent diaspora figures publicly denounced the ruling, while protests in Pristina turned violent, including attacks on the EU mission. Some analysts argue the decision should not be read as an indictment of the Kosovo Liberation Army as a whole. Separately, a NATO shootdown of a drone over Lithuania in coverage accompanying the story underlines heightened regional security anxieties that could amplify the fallout from the verdict.
