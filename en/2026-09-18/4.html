Conflict sharpens: strikes, NATO alarms, and sustained Western support

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-09-18 • 52 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian attacks on Ukrainian cities cause civilian casualties and infrastructure damage while threatening NATO allies and prompting military readiness.
  • European and NATO members increase military support and defense budgets to sustain Ukraine's resistance against Russian aggression.
  • Hybrid warfare and diplomatic tensions escalate, with Russian warnings, espionage, and international repercussions affecting European security and relations.

Russian strikes and drone attacks continue to hit Ukrainian cities, infrastructure and civilians, producing casualties and damaging industry and shipping. Poland and other NATO members are publicly warning of possible Russian strikes beyond Ukraine, scrambling jets and urging allied readiness, while NATO leaders call for sustained deterrence. The European Union and partners are stepping up military financing and Ukraine is planning record defence budgets to sustain the fight. Moscow maintains a combative posture—public warnings from senior officials, domestic electoral consolidation and reported military losses—while hybrid and cyber operations and foiled intelligence plots heighten European security concerns.
