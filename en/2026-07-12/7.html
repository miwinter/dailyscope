Shooting at Toronto street festival kills two

Canada Shooting Incident 2026-07-12 • 12 articles in total

In brief
  • A shooting at a Latin/Salsa street festival in Toronto killed two people and injured several others.
  • Authorities launched an active investigation with a large emergency response and appeals for witnesses while the attacker may be at large.
  • Media coverage differs, focusing on community impact, police response, or framing the incident as terrorism.

Two people were killed and multiple others injured in a shooting at a street festival in Toronto, widely reported as occurring at a Latin/Salsa cultural event. Police and emergency services mounted a large response, launched an active investigation, appealed for witnesses and reviewed surveillance footage while pursuing suspects. Authorities have released few details about motives or the shooter, and some accounts say the attacker remains at large. Coverage varies in emphasis, with most reports focusing on public-safety and community impact while one outlet frames the attack as terrorism.
