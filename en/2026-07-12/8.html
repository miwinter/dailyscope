Subpoenas of NYT Journalists Over Air Force One Reporting

US Press Subpoena 2026-07-12 • 12 articles in total

In brief
  • The U.S. government subpoenaed New York Times journalists over reporting on Air Force One security flaws, sparking legal action.
  • News organizations see subpoenas as threats to press freedom and efforts to intimidate critical reporting.
  • The administration justifies legal measures as necessary to protect sensitive information and national security.

Multiple international outlets report that the U.S. government has subpoenaed New York Times journalists and pursued legal action after reporting on alleged security flaws in the new Air Force One. Coverage highlights a clash between national security claims from the administration and concerns from news organizations about source protection and press freedom. Some outlets frame the subpoenas and lawsuit as attempts to intimidate or silence investigative reporting, warning of chilling effects on journalists. Others focus on the legal and procedural aspects, noting the government’s rationale that sensitive material must be protected and investigated.
