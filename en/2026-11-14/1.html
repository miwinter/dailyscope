Historic U.S.–China–Russia Trilateral Summit

US–China Summit Diplomacy 2026-11-14 • 33 articles in total

In brief
  • Historic trilateral meeting confirmed between Trump, Xi, and Putin at APEC in Shenzhen on Nov. 19, focusing on nuclear arms reduction and Iran.
  • Ukraine's presence on the agenda is unclear, with differing reports on whether it will be discussed.
  • Meeting symbolizes a delicate diplomatic balance for China as host amid shifting global alliances, with limited expectations for immediate deals.

A historic trilateral meeting between U.S. President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin has been confirmed on the sidelines of the APEC summit in China, with many outlets reporting it will take place in Shenzhen on Nov. 19. Coverage emphasizes nuclear arms reductions and Iran as the central agenda items, though officials and analysts expect limited near‑term deals. Reports diverge on whether Ukraine will be discussed, with some sources saying it is off the agenda and others indicating it could feature. Commentators highlight the diplomatic significance and the delicate position this places on China as host and mediator amid shifting global alignments.
