In brief

  • Security concerns include arms transfers, defence cooperation, and maritime arrangements amid strategic competition.
  • Economic ties involve free trade deals, capacity-building, development aid, and increasing regional connectivity and cooperation.

Recent reporting shows a flurry of bilateral agreements and engagements spanning security pacts, trade deals, infrastructure and development finance, and diplomatic exchanges. Gulf and regional security issues—including maritime arrangements and defence cooperation—feature alongside arms transfers and defence procurements that underscore growing strategic competition. Economic agreements range from free trade deals, credit lines and connectivity projects to sectoral trade boosts in agriculture, automotive and energy, while development aid and capacity-building initiatives target education, policing and logistics. Across Africa, Asia and the Pacific, high-level visits, ambassadorial appointments and new coordination bodies signal an active diplomacy aimed at deepening practical cooperation and managing regional challenges.
