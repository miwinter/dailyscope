Trump's presidency: media clashes, military recalibration and legal pressures

US: Trump Presidency 2026-08-18 • 62 articles in total

In brief
  • Trump's presidency features frequent public clashes with the press, abrupt military shifts, and growing legal troubles.
  • International concern rises over his provocative rhetoric, especially toward Oman, and inconsistent signals on North Korea, risking allied trust.
  • Polls show approval dropping to the low 30s amid fears of extended conflict and erratic, unorthodox governance harming political and diplomatic stability.

Coverage portrays a presidency marked by frequent public confrontations with the press, abrupt strategic shifts in military posture toward South Korea, and accumulating legal setbacks. International outlets highlight alarm at provocative rhetoric toward Oman and uneven signals on North Korea that worry allies about deterrence and U.S. reliability. Polling shows a sustained fall in approval to the low 30s amid fears of prolonged conflict and policy uncertainty. Across reports critics point to an unorthodox, vindictive governing style and volatile optics that compound political and diplomatic challenges.
