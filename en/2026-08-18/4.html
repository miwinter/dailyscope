Trump presidency: media clashes, hawkish rhetoric and tumbling support

US: Trump Presidency 2026-08-18 • 88 articles in total

In brief
  • Trump's presidency is marked by frequent public clashes with journalists and aggressive foreign policy threats in the Middle East.
  • Legal challenges and declining approval ratings reflect growing domestic and international concerns about his leadership.
  • Controversial defense policies and stalled diplomatic efforts highlight tensions in U.S. strategic and regional relations.

International coverage of the Trump presidency coalesces around a few recurring themes: frequent public clashes with journalists, aggressive rhetoric toward regional actors (notably threats involving Oman and Iran), and a sustained slide in approval ratings. Multiple outlets report legal setbacks for Trump at the Supreme Court and continuing scrutiny over his personal and financial conduct. Analysts and foreign governments also highlight consequential policy choices on defense — from aircraft carrier plans to reduced joint exercises and cyber authorities — alongside stalled diplomatic initiatives on Gaza and tense outreach to North Korea. Together the stories sketch a presidency marked by confrontational communication, controversial foreign-policy postures and growing domestic and international concern about his leadership.
