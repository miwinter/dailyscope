Escalation, diplomatic friction and domestic strain around the Russo‑Ukrainian war

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-18 • 82 articles in total

In brief
  • Kyiv and Moscow exchange deep strikes and mass drone attacks targeting military, energy, and commercial sites, expanding the conflict beyond front lines.
  • Russia faces rising political repression and economic strain while warning Western countries over drone supplies to Ukraine amid NATO airspace incursions.
  • The war causes significant civilian casualties and humanitarian crises, with evolving weaponry and logistics shaping the conflict's next phase.

Reporting shows a marked intensification of cross‑border strikes and mass drone campaigns, with Kyiv and Moscow exchanging deep strikes that have hit military, energy and commercial targets and sparked fires near major Russian cities. Diplomatic tensions have risen as Moscow publicly warns the United Kingdom and other Western suppliers over drone deliveries to Ukraine, while NATO and European states report spillovers and probe incursions into allied airspace. Inside Russia, authorities are sidelining and punishing critics and dissenting officials amid economic strains and secrecy around military losses. Parallel coverage stresses the humanitarian toll on civilians and foreign nationals, alongside evolving weaponry, ammunition shortages and shifting mobilization and logistics that will shape the coming phase of the war.
