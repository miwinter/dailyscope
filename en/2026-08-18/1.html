Leaders in Dialogue: Gaza diplomacy, Korea outreach and great-power friction

World Leaders Dialogue 2026-08-18 • 114 articles in total

In brief
  • World leaders focus on Gaza reconstruction linked to Hamas disarmament, cautious Korean Peninsula peace talks, and heightened great-power geopolitical competition.
  • U.S. emphasizes verification of Hamas disarmament and tech rivalry with China, while regional and global actors pursue varied diplomatic and security agendas.
  • International diplomacy includes crisis engagement in Sudan, Afghanistan, and Ethiopia, alongside routine statecraft, cultural ties, and emerging space cooperation.

World leaders and envoys engaged intensively on three overlapping fronts: a US-led push to condition Gaza reconstruction on Hamas disarmament, renewed overtures across the Korean Peninsula and increasing friction in great-power diplomacy. Jared Kushner’s shuttle diplomacy with Israel, Hamas and regional intermediaries produced no decisive breakthrough but set tight timelines and conditional frameworks for demilitarization and reconstruction. U.S.–North Korea exchanges and South Korean proposals for formal peace efforts signalled cautious momentum toward talks even as allies debated preparedness for worst-case scenarios. Elsewhere, Moscow’s assertive steps over the Kuril Islands and its messaging to regional partners, Turkey’s recalibration of priorities, and U.S. pressure on partners in the AI race underscored widening geopolitical competition alongside routine ceremonial and cooperative visits.
