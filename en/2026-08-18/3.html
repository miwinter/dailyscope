Escalation, drone warfare and widening international rifts

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-18 • 94 articles in total

In brief
  • The conflict features intensified long-range and drone strikes deep into Russian territory amid ongoing Ukrainian offensives.
  • Moscow accuses Western states, especially the UK, of escalating the war by supplying drones, heightening diplomatic tensions.
  • The war causes severe civilian casualties, infrastructure damage, capability shortages, and involves technological, political, and cultural dimensions across multiple countries and alliances.

Across the coverage, the conflict is marked by intensified long-range and drone strikes that have reached deep into Russian territory and repeatedly struck Russian cities and infrastructure, while Ukraine continues offensive operations. Moscow has publicly threatened Western states — especially the United Kingdom — over alleged transfers of British-made drones to Kyiv, raising diplomatic tensions and risk of broader confrontation. Numerous reports document heavy civilian casualties and damage in Ukrainian regions such as Kharkiv, as well as spillovers into neighboring areas, compounding humanitarian and infrastructure crises. Observers also flag shortages and capability gaps (air-defences, missile stocks) and a growing role for new technologies and external partners, while information campaigns and cultural losses add a political and societal dimension to the war.
