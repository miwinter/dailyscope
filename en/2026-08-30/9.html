Norway mourns King Harald V

Norway: Royal Death 2026-08-30 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • King Harald V of Norway died at 89 after a 35-year reign, ending a significant era for Norway's monarchy.
  • His son Haakon VIII succeeded him and made early public remarks expressing gratitude and leadership.
  • Authorities declared official mourning and will announce funeral arrangements soon.

King Harald V of Norway has died at 89 after a 35-year reign, prompting national and international tributes. The palace confirmed his passing and authorities said funeral arrangements will be announced in the coming days while a period of official mourning is observed. His son, Haakon VIII, has succeeded him and has delivered early public remarks expressing gratitude and leading the transition. Coverage emphasizes Harald's long service, his role modernizing the monarchy, and the symbolic end of an era for Norway's constitutional monarchy.
