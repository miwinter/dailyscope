Italy pushes to suspend Spain from Schengen

EU Schengen Policy Debate 2026-07-31 • 6 articles in total

In brief
  • Italy's government wants to suspend or exclude Spain from the Schengen Area due to migrant surge and border-control issues.
  • Spain protested by summoning the Italian ambassador and EU officials warned of risks to cooperation and Schengen's integrity.
  • The dispute reflects broader EU tensions over migration and may trigger debates on Schengen reforms and border management.

Italy's government, led by Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni, has publicly called for suspending or excluding Spain from the Schengen Area in response to a surge in migrant arrivals and perceived border-control failures. The proposal would reinstate internal border checks between two EU members and could disrupt travel, trade, and existing Schengen rules. Madrid has protested diplomatically, summoning the Italian ambassador, while EU officials have responded cautiously, warning of risks to cohesion and cooperation on asylum. The dispute highlights wider tensions over migration policy and could prompt renewed debate on Schengen reforms and collective border management.
