Colombia explosives attacks

Colombia Explosives Attacks 2026-11-24 • 12 articles in total

In brief
  • A major car bombing in Villavicencio injured 12-20 people and damaged buildings near the Meta governor's office, highlighting urban security risks.
  • Drone strikes on police bases caused civilian injuries, including an infant, and damaged homes, indicating violent actors' expanded tactics.
  • Separate clashes killed one soldier and several alleged dissidents; investigations continue with no clear responsibility claims.

A series of explosives attacks in Colombia has wounded and killed both security personnel and civilians, revealing persistent instability. A major car bombing in Villavicencio near the Meta governor’s office damaged buildings and left a dozen to two dozen people injured across multiple reports. Drone strikes on police bases have also harmed civilians and damaged homes, with at least one infant among the injured, suggesting an expansion of tactics by violent actors. Separate clashes left a soldier and several alleged dissidents dead in distinct incidents; investigations into motives and perpetrators remain ongoing, and no clear claims of responsibility have been widely reported.

Villavicencio car-bombing

Multiple outlets report a vehicle bomb detonated near the Meta Governorate in Villavicencio, causing significant material damage and injuring between about a dozen and twenty people. Authorities have cordoned off the area and launched investigations, while no group had immediately claimed responsibility, underscoring broad concerns about urban security.

Drone attacks on security sites

Reports describe drone strikes targeting a Colombian police base that injured civilians and damaged homes, including a baby among the wounded. Coverage highlights the growing use of drones in attacks and suggests possible links to gang feuds, raising alarms about new tactics that endanger non-combatants.

Clashes and targeted killings

Separate incidents left one soldier dead and five alleged dissidents killed, alongside another explosives attack that wounded about a dozen people. Reporting provides limited detail on perpetrators or motives, but these events point to ongoing armed confrontations and unresolved security challenges in affected regions.
