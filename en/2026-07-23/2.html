US–Iran Exchange Intensifies with Regional Spillovers

Middle East Conflict Escalation 2026-07-23 • 113 articles in total

In brief
  • US strikes and threats against Iran have escalated conflict across the Gulf and regional neighbors.
  • Iran and allied groups respond with retaliatory attacks threatening regional security and shipping routes.
  • The confrontation causes civilian harm, economic strain, market volatility, and risks involving external powers.

A sustained campaign of US strikes on Iran, accompanied by repeated threats from President Trump to target Iranian infrastructure, has driven a sharp escalation across the Gulf and beyond. Tehran has answered with vows of retaliation, public warnings of an 'eye for an eye' doctrine and forceful actions that have hit or threatened neighbouring Gulf states. Yemen’s Houthi forces and other non-state actors have intensified attacks on shipping and tankers, compounding risks to global trade and prompting further US operations. Gulf states and regional militaries report active air-defence engagements and damage to facilities, while clashes along Israel’s borders and raids in the occupied West Bank continue to produce civilian suffering. The confrontation is producing mounting human and fiscal costs, market volatility, and questions about possible outside involvement that could widen the conflict further.
