Social Media Ban for Under-15s

France has enacted a pioneering law banning social media access for children under 15, aiming to protect minors from online risks such as harmful content and privacy violations. This move makes France the first EU country to implement such a blanket restriction, sparking debates about enforcement challenges, digital rights, and the role of platforms. The legislation is part of a broader push for stricter digital regulation and child protection in the online environment.
