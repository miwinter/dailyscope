Zelensky replaces army chief amid protests and calls for reform

Ukraine Military Commander Appointment 2026-07-23 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • Zelensky replaced commander Oleksandr Syrskyi with Mykhailo Drapaty due to public protests and demand for reform.
  • Drapaty is a seasoned figure expected to modernize Ukrainian military strategy and command culture.
  • International views focus on political motives, operational changes, and Russia's reaction to the leadership shift.

President Volodymyr Zelensky dismissed long-time commander Oleksandr Syrskyi and appointed Mykhailo Drapaty as the new commander-in-chief during a period of sustained public protests and political pressure. Domestic coverage emphasizes the protests and calls for younger, reform-minded leadership as a key driver of the change. Profiles and regional reporting portray Drapaty as a seasoned, sometimes legendary figure whose appointment signals an intent to modernize doctrine and military management. Analysts and international outlets remain cautious about how quickly the leadership change will affect battlefield performance or alter the wider strategic balance with Russia.

Countries covering this topic

Public protests forced the leadership change

Sources from Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the United States frame the replacement primarily as a response to sustained domestic protests and public dissatisfaction with military leadership. These reports stress the political pressure on Zelensky and treat the firing as a high-profile shake-up prompted by civic unrest rather than a purely operational decision.

A conciliatory, reform-minded appointment

Coverage from Austria, Spain and Belgium interprets the appointment as a conciliatory and reform-driven move intended to mend internal divisions and address long-standing institutional problems. These outlets emphasize the political calculation behind choosing a reformer and question whether the change will resolve Ukraine's deeper military and governance challenges.
