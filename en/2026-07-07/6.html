China's Pacific Missile Tests Heighten Regional Tensions

China Pacific Missile Test 2026-07-07 • 27 articles in total

In brief
  • China conducted long-range missile tests in the Pacific, some nuclear-capable, causing alarm across the Indo-Pacific.
  • The tests sparked strong opposition from the US, allies, and regional leaders who see them as provocative and destabilising.
  • China claims the launches are routine military exercises, while analysts warn they escalate regional tensions and arms competition.

China carried out submarine-launched and other long-range missile tests that landed in the Pacific, some described as nuclear-capable, prompting alarm across the Indo-Pacific. The United States and several allies called the launches destabilising and urged restraint, while Beijing defended the exercises as routine training conducted in designated areas. Australia and Pacific island leaders framed the tests as provocative breaches of regional security norms, and Japan and South Korea raised concerns about ties to Taiwan and broader deterrence dynamics. Analysts warn the launches accelerate military modernisation, complicate arms-control prospects and increase the risk of miscalculation, prompting repeated calls for de-escalation and dialogue.
