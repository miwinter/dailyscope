Escalation around Kyiv, deep strikes into Russia and Allied responses

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-07-07 • 76 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian missile and drone attacks have increased sharply, causing civilian casualties and damage around Kyiv.
  • Ukraine has expanded strikes into Russian territory, targeting key infrastructure and conducting mass drone raids near Moscow.
  • International and NATO leaders are debating increased military aid amid Ukraine's urgent calls and Russia's broader strategic pressures.

Recent coverage shows a sharp uptick in violence: repeated Russian missile and drone barrages have struck Kyiv and surrounding areas, causing dozens of civilian deaths and widespread damage. At the same time Ukraine has struck deep into Russian territory — including refineries, airports and ports — and launched mass drone raids toward Moscow and bordering regions, raising fears of escalation. Ukrainian leaders and many partners are urgently pleading for more air‑defence and military aid as shortages leave cities vulnerable, while NATO and Western capitals debate additional support ahead of a summit. Parallel reporting highlights Moscow’s narratives, regional coercion (notably around Belarus and Moldova), and analyses of wider economic and strategic consequences for Russia and allied cohesion.
