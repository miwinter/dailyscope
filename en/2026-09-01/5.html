Strikes, diplomacy and Kremlin pressure in the Russia–Ukraine war

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-09-01 • 63 articles in total

In brief
  • Russia signals openness to talks while preparing strikes on Ukraine's infrastructure and emphasizing security grievances.
  • Ukrainian cities face repeated attacks causing civilian casualties, power outages, and disruption to daily life.
  • International actors press for negotiations amid growing stress in Russia and evolving security challenges involving NATO and regional dynamics.

Reporting shows a war that is simultaneously violent on the ground and active in diplomatic channels: Russia publicly says it is open to talks while also threatening and preparing large strikes on Ukrainian infrastructure. Ukrainian cities—especially Kyiv—continue to suffer heavy attacks, civilian deaths, power outages and widespread disruption to daily life and commerce. International actors from India to NATO members press for negotiations and monitor shifts in alliances and disinformation campaigns, even as major powers (Russia and China) avoid deep public engagement on the conflict. Many outlets also highlight growing signals of stress inside Russia—domestic repression, mobilization and rumors of elite unease—which analysts warn could shape Moscow’s strategic choices going forward.
