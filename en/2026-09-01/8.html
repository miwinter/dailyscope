Europe watch: Russian meddling, regional security strains and domestic controversies

Europe Watchlist 2026-09-01 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Russia exerts malign influence in Europe through corruption, disinformation, and gray-zone tactics affecting multiple countries.
  • NATO-area security tensions rise due to incidents and intelligence activity prompting defensive actions in eastern and Baltic regions.
  • EU internal divisions and political polarization hinder unified responses to external threats and complicate domestic security strategies.

Across Europe reporting highlights growing concerns about external malign influence, regional security frictions, and contentious domestic policies. Several pieces point to Russian activity as a driver of corruption, disinformation and gray-zone pressure that is reshaping responses from Baltic states to Moldova and Spain. NATO-area incidents and intelligence visits have prompted unusual security decisions and allegations of aircraft escorts, underscoring elevated tensions in the eastern and Baltic theaters. At the same time, EU internal fragmentation and broad collections of suspicious events complicate a coordinated external strategy. Domestic controversies—such as Austria’s contested deradicalization plan and Iceland’s missed strategic opportunity—reflect political polarization within states as they navigate these wider threats.
