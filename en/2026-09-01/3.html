Summit Diplomacy, Shifting Alignments and Regional Tensions

World Leaders Dialogue 2026-09-01 • 85 articles in total

In brief
  • China and Russia strengthened strategic and economic cooperation at the SCO summit to counter Western influence.
  • India urged a diplomatic resolution to the Ukraine war while balancing relations with major powers.
  • Global and regional tensions rose over Russia's global re-engagement, Taiwan's forum participation, and ongoing mediation efforts in conflict zones.

World leaders converged in Bishkek for the SCO summit, where China and Russia showcased deepening strategic cooperation and engaged with a range of regional partners on security and economic issues. India pressed Moscow for a diplomatic end to the Ukraine war, highlighting New Delhi’s balancing act between major powers. Western capitals reported friction over Russia’s re-engagement in global forums and debated defence and diplomatic responses, while Pacific states confronted tensions over Taiwan’s participation and Beijing’s warnings. Beyond the summit, bilateral talks, regional mediation efforts and cultural diplomacy continued worldwide, from Lebanon–Israel contacts to Venezuela’s government–opposition dialogue and a spate of condolences and intergovernmental outreach.
