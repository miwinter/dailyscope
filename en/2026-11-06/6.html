Peña resigns amid Ueno Bank crisis

Paraguay Peña Resignation 2026-11-06 • 33 articles in total

In brief
  • Ueno Bank's collapse froze millions' accounts, igniting protests and political crisis that forced President Santiago Peña's resignation.
  • Parliament swiftly initiated impeachment and legal proceedings amid party splits and debates over interim governance.
  • The scandal revealed entrenched power networks, raising investor concerns and destabilizing Paraguay's fragile political transition.

Paraguay’s president Santiago Peña resigned after a collapse at Ueno Bank sparked widespread financial disruption and mass protests. The bank failure, which left millions unable to access accounts, quickly translated into political pressure, loss of party backing and calls for accountability. Lawmakers moved rapidly to open impeachment and criminal proceedings even as the constitutional succession and interim leadership were debated. The episode exposed entrenched power networks and raised regional investor and governance concerns as Paraguay navigates a fragile transition.
