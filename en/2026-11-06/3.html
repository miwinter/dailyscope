US diplomatic strategy: Kushner's push and allied caution

US Diplomacy Strategy 2026-11-06 • 20 articles in total

In brief
  • Jared Kushner leads Abu Dhabi peace talks as the US aims to reassert Middle East diplomacy amid strained US-Israel relations.
  • White House hints at a grand US-Russia deal alarm NATO allies, causing caution despite likely policy continuity.
  • US uses sanctions, security projects, and diplomacy to blend pressure and cooperation globally, while allies watch for policy signals.

Jared Kushner’s scheduled Abu Dhabi peace talks epitomize a US push to reassert diplomatic leadership in the Middle East even as U.S.-Israel relations strain. At the same time, senior US officials’ public hints of a sweeping ‘grand deal’ with Russia to reframe European security have alarmed NATO partners and prompted cautious reactions across allied capitals. Washington is combining pressure tactics (sanctions, congressional measures) with cooperative tools such as infrastructure projects and regional security initiatives, reflecting a mixed toolkit. Analysts generally predict continuity rather than wholesale reversal, but allies are closely watching signals—from rhetoric to visits—to anticipate potential policy shifts.
