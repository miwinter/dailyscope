Republican Power Struggle After Midterms

US Republicans Power Struggle 2026-11-06 • 18 articles in total

In brief
  • Republican Party faces intense internal conflict post-midterms, with leadership challenges, factional blame, and policy disputes threatening unity and future direction.
  • Key figures like Rubio deny exit plans while Vance seeks post-Trump positioning, reflecting strategic realignments and leadership jockeying.
  • Scandals pressure officials like Defense Secretary Hegseth; Trump's combative stance and House speaker's resignation worsen party instability ahead of 2028 elections.

The Republican Party is locked in a fierce internal struggle after a damaging midterm defeat, with factions blaming leadership, strategy, and specific policies. Prominent figures are repositioning—some denying exit plans, others distancing from Donald Trump or staking claims as his successor—while others face calls to resign or even impeachment. The turmoil spans personnel battles (from the House speakership to cabinet figures like the defense secretary) and public confrontations that risk deepening party fractures. How the GOP resolves these fights will shape its messaging, policy priorities, and electoral prospects heading into 2028.
