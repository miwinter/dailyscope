In brief

A diverse set of security warnings and incidents shows multiple threat vectors intensifying simultaneously. Iranian statements and reported capabilities—ranging from ship‑killing missiles to plans against undersea cables and explicit bans on US naval presence—have elevated regional risk and prompted travel and airspace caution. Western intelligence agencies and allied militaries are signaling heightened readiness, issuing warnings about plots targeting diplomats and citizens and conducting joint drills. At the same time Europe confronts hybrid and domestic threats, while cyber and critical‑infrastructure vulnerabilities (notably PHP flaws and undersea cable concerns) are prompting urgent mitigations. Regional responses — from military deployments in Haiti to tougher law‑and‑order measures in the Sahel and political crackdowns at home — reflect governments racing to contain a widening security picture.
