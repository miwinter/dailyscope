Russia–Ukraine Attacks — escalation and spillover

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-21 • 67 articles in total

In brief
  • Russian missile and drone strikes heavily damage Ukrainian cities and infrastructure, causing high civilian casualties and escalating conflict around Crimea Bridge.
  • Missile and drone incidents in Poland prompt NATO investigations and heightened fears of wider regional escalation amid intensified military and political tensions.
  • International responses include UN action calls, humanitarian appeals, and increased scrutiny of covert attacks near embassies and maritime routes, complicating diplomacy and security.

Russian forces have mounted large, sustained missile and drone offensives across Ukraine, striking cities, transport links and energy infrastructure and inflicting heavy civilian casualties. Several munitions and drones have crossed into Polish territory, prompting investigations, NATO scrambling and heightened fears of wider escalation. The strikes follow and fuel tit‑for‑tat operations around the Crimea Bridge, with Moscow promising harsh responses and Kyiv warning of continued attacks. International leaders and organisations have responded with condemnation, calls for emergency UN action, political rhetoric and intensified humanitarian appeals.

Analysts and officials warn the bridge incident raises the stakes strategically and politically, reducing prospects for near‑term ceasefire talks.

Humanitarian impact: children, shelters and legal fallout

UNICEF and UN monitors report that more than a thousand children have been killed or injured this year, and civilians continue to shelter in metro stations as strikes hit residential areas. The conflict’s humanitarian ripple effects include legal disputes over evacuated children and urgent winter and relief assistance needs across affected oblasts.
