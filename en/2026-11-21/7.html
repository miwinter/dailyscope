U.S. federal data breaches

US FBI Data Breach 2026-11-21 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • The FBI is investigating a cyber intrusion into its nationwide criminal information database with limited public details available.
  • The U.S. Department of Agriculture faced a cyberattack affecting producers' payments and disrupting applications.
  • Both incidents raise concerns over law enforcement operations, privacy, and federal service disruptions amid ongoing investigations.

Multiple news reports describe fresh cyber intrusions into U.S. federal data systems, most notably an apparent breach of the FBI’s nationwide criminal information database that is under active investigation. Details remain sparse: officials have not publicly disclosed the scope, origin, or whether sensitive records were exfiltrated, though some accounts suggest attackers may have leveraged arrest warrants and criminal records. Separately, the U.S. Department of Agriculture was reportedly hit this week, with attackers accessing a producers’ payments platform and forcing application interruptions. Investigations are ongoing in both cases amid growing concerns about impacts on law enforcement operations, privacy, and federal service delivery.

FBI criminal database breach

Multiple outlets report the FBI is investigating an intrusion into its nationwide criminal information database, with officials providing few concrete details while inquiries continue. Coverage highlights potential operational and privacy risks for federal, state, and local law enforcement and notes this follows other recent intrusions into federal systems.

USDA cyberattack affects payments platform

NBC-based reports indicate the U.S. Department of Agriculture suffered a cyberattack this week that accessed a producers’ payments platform and forced interruptions to some applications. Authorities have notified employees and launched an investigation, but have released limited specifics on method, scope, or attribution.
