Peru quake felt across borders

Peru Earthquake Cross-Border 2026-11-21 • 19 articles in total

In brief
  • A strong 6.7-6.9 magnitude earthquake struck Junín, Peru, causing blackouts, road blockages, and structural damage with few confirmed casualties.
  • The quake was felt across Andean countries including Ecuador and Colombia, prompting alerts and precautionary measures from authorities.
  • Emergency services responded swiftly, monitoring aftershocks and emphasizing the importance of regional seismic preparedness and coordination.

A strong earthquake centered in Junín, Peru (reported magnitudes between 6.7 and 6.9) was widely felt across the Andean region, including Quito, Ecuador, and cities in Colombia. Initial reports indicate few confirmed casualties, but footage and local accounts describe blackouts, road blockages and some structural damage in affected areas. Emergency services have mobilized, authorities are monitoring aftershocks, and preliminary damage assessments are ongoing. The event underscores the cross-border nature of seismic risk in the region and the need for coordinated preparedness and information sharing.

Cross-border shaking: Ecuador and Colombia felt it

Multiple reports emphasize that the tremor was felt across borders, notably in Quito and in Colombian cities, prompting public alarm and alerts. Authorities in neighboring countries monitored effects and advised residents to take precautions while Peruvian agencies assessed the epicenter and impacts.
