Southern France wildfires force mass evacuations

France Wildfires Evacuations 2026-07-03 • 8 articles in total

In brief
  • Large wildfires in southern France forced mass evacuations from campsites and towns, displacing thousands of people.
  • Firefighters deployed up to 2,000 personnel to battle fires driven by strong winds, high heat, and dry conditions.
  • The fires followed a severe European heatwave, stressing emergency services and raising climate-related risks.

Large wildfires across southern France have forced mass evacuations from campsites, towns and residential areas, with reports citing thousands of people displaced. Fire crews — numbering from hundreds up to around 2,000 personnel — are battling fast‑spreading blazes driven by strong winds, high temperatures and very dry conditions. Authorities report significant damage, with tens of thousands of hectares burned and evacuation figures ranging from about 1,500 to 3,000 people in different locations. The fires followed a severe European heatwave, highlighting climate‑linked risks and placing heavy strain on emergency services, while containment efforts and investigations into causes continue as officials update safety advisories.
