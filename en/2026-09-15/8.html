NATO downs drone over Lithuania; origin disputed

NATO Baltic Drone Shootdown 2026-09-15 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • NATO jets intercepted and downed a drone entering Lithuanian airspace with no casualties, showing strong Baltic air-defense readiness.
  • Sources differ on the drone's origin, with Lithuanian officials suggesting Russia and others citing Belarus as possible sources.
  • The incident raises regional tensions amid the Ukraine war and prompts ongoing investigations and monitoring.

NATO fighter jets intercepted and shot down an unmanned aerial vehicle that entered or approached Lithuanian airspace, with authorities reporting no casualties. Officials emphasized a rapid air-defense response and highlighted NATO and Lithuania's vigilance in the Baltic region. Initial accounts diverge on the drone's provenance: some reports cite preliminary data pointing to Belarus, while Lithuania's president suggested a possible Russian origin. The incident triggered alarms in Vilnius, underlining heightened regional tensions amid the broader war in Ukraine and prompting ongoing investigations and monitoring.
