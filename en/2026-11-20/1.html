Russia–Ukraine Attacks

Russia–Ukraine Attacks 2026-11-20 • 39 articles in total

In brief
  • Intense fighting sees Kyiv striking strategic Russian targets, including Crimea Bridge, while Moscow retaliates with missile and drone attacks on Kyiv's infrastructure.
  • Ukrainian operations target Russian industrial sites deep in occupied territory, aiming to disrupt war-sustaining resources despite escalation risks.
  • Conflict impacts extend internationally, damaging foreign diplomatic sites and shipping routes, raising global security concerns and diplomatic tensions.

Fighting remains intense across multiple fronts as both Moscow and Kyiv strike strategic targets and critical infrastructure. Repeated attacks on the Crimea Bridge and cross-border strikes into Russia demonstrate Kyiv’s growing reach and Moscow’s determination to retaliate. Russian missile and drone barrages continue to damage Kyiv’s bridges, diplomatic buildings and civilian facilities, heightening humanitarian and diplomatic tensions. The conflict increasingly involves foreign nationals, shipping routes and regional security, prompting international concern and high-level rhetoric that raises risks of further escalation.
