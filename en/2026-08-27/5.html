War grinds on: strikes, escalation and diplomatic deadlock

Russo-Ukrainian War 2026-08-27 • 46 articles in total

In brief
  • The conflict continues with heavy civilian harm, cross-border attacks, and military strain while Moscow prepares for escalation and NATO expands support to Ukraine.
  • Ukrainian sources stress ongoing Russian strikes and national security threats amid concerns over Kremlin mobilization and control measures.
  • Diplomatic efforts remain stalled with limited prisoner exchange talks, while regional tensions rise and Western allies fortify deterrence and strategic realignments.

Coverage shows a war that continues to inflict heavy civilian harm and strain military capacities on both sides, with repeated missile and drone strikes hitting cities, infrastructure and front-line units. Reports also document cross-border actions and attacks on Russian facilities, while Moscow signals measures to shore up control and prepare for further escalation. NATO and Western actors are issuing deterrent warnings and expanding military support, including long-range strike capabilities for Kyiv. Diplomatic efforts appear stalled in many accounts, though some reporting points to limited channels such as potential prisoner exchanges and humanitarian appeals.
