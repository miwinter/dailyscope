Genoa Morandi bridge: convictions and accountability

Genoa Morandi Verdicts 2026-07-17 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • An Italian court convicted 32 people for the 2018 Morandi bridge collapse that killed 43, highlighting serious negligence in maintenance and oversight.
  • The former head of the motorway company received a 12-year prison sentence, emphasizing individual criminal accountability for infrastructure failures.
  • The verdicts sparked debates on public trust, corporate responsibility, and the adequacy of safety systems in transport infrastructure.

An Italian court convicted 32 people over the 2018 Morandi bridge collapse that killed 43, marking a major legal reckoning for the disaster. The former head of the motorway company received a high-profile 12-year prison sentence as judges found serious negligence in maintenance and oversight. Coverage highlights both individual criminal responsibility and broader systemic failures in infrastructure governance. The verdicts have reopened debates about public trust, corporate accountability and the adequacy of safety regimes for critical transport infrastructure.
