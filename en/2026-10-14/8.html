France education unrest: protests, unions and government response

France Education Reform Protests 2026-10-14 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • Student and union protests against education reforms have caused widespread disruptions in French high schools and call for a general strike.
  • Authorities have increased security measures and cleared blockades to maintain public order and access to schools.
  • President Macron urges respect and dialogue, framing the unrest as a test of political leadership amid ongoing negotiations.

Nationwide student and union-led protests over proposed education reforms have persisted across France, with organizers reporting large turnouts and around 200 high schools affected. Labor groups such as the CGT are escalating demands, calling for a general strike and denouncing proposed budget cuts, frozen scholarships, and reduced university funding. Authorities have tightened security and taken preventive measures to keep order, while police cleared blockades and limited access to some schools. President Macron has urged protesters to show respect and return to dialogue, framing the unrest as a test of political leadership. The situation remains fluid as unions continue mobilization and officials seek to balance public order with negotiations.
