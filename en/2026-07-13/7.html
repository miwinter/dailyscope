Qatar Emir Death 2026-07-13 • 33 articles in total

In brief
  • Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani died at 74, prompting national mourning and official tributes in Qatar.
  • He is credited with modernizing Qatar and enhancing its economic growth and international influence.
  • Foreign leaders and analysts highlighted his impact on regional diplomacy and signaled continuity under his successor.

Qatar confirmed the death of former Emir Sheikh Hamad bin Khalifa Al Thani at age 74, with the Amiri Diwan announcing national mourning and official tributes. Coverage emphasizes his role as the architect of modern Qatar, credited with rapid economic growth, diplomatic expansion and a lasting international profile. Leaders, governments and Islamic organizations across regions offered condolences, underscoring close bilateral ties and the symbolic weight of his passing. Analysts and commentators noted the event as the close of a formative era for Qatar and flagged continuity under his son alongside possible shifts in regional diplomacy and domestic priorities.
