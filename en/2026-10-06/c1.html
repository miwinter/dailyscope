France in International News

Summary of articles about France in the international press on 2026-10-06, based on 46 articles collected.

In brief
  • France faces large student protests against education reforms causing arrests and severe injuries.
  • The government responds with school closures, new laws, and faces political and economic criticisms.
  • France engages in international defense cooperation and handles legal investigations on terrorism financing and asset disputes.

France is currently experiencing significant social unrest, primarily driven by widespread student protests against education reforms and poor school conditions, which have led to thousands of arrests and serious injuries, including a teenager losing a hand due to a police grenade. The government is responding with measures such as school closures and proposed legislation to hold rioters financially accountable, while political tensions and economic critiques of President Macron's policies persist. Additionally, France is engaged in international defense cooperation, notably with Ukraine, and is involved in legal and security matters including investigations into alleged Hamas financing and disputes over ill-gotten assets linked to Gabonese political figures.
