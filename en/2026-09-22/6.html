US networks suspend Trump coverage in show of solidarity

US Media Solidarity: Trump Coverage Suspension 2026-09-22 • 13 articles in total

In brief
  • Major U.S. networks coordinated to limit live Trump coverage in solidarity with CNN over White House access disputes.
  • The boycott crossed political lines, involving Fox News and major newspapers, emphasizing broad media unity for press freedom.
  • Trump responded with alternative broadcasts and legal actions while the dispute highlights rising tensions between the administration and the press.

Major U.S. television networks coordinated to limit or suspend live coverage of President Donald Trump in a show of solidarity with CNN after disputes over White House access. Reports emphasize the unusual cross-network unity—spanning outlets across the political spectrum—and note that some newspapers and Fox News also participated or signaled support. Several pieces frame the move as a defense of press freedom and a protest against alleged restrictions or vetoes on certain news organizations. Other coverage highlights Trump’s alternative media response, including a new televised platform recycling past speeches, and legal pushback by outlets barred from access. Observers warn the episode underlines rising tensions between the administration and the press and could reshape how campaign events are covered.
