Trump presidency: media war, sagging approval and policy pivots

US: Trump Presidency 2026-09-22 • 62 articles in total

In brief
  • Trump's administration confronts major news outlets through bans, lawsuits, and launching 'Trump TV', raising press freedom concerns internationally.
  • His approval ratings hit historic lows due to economic issues and foreign policy, while pursuing aggressive technology and foreign strategies.
  • International coverage highlights transactional politics, economic policies, and symbolic acts shaping his image and influence.

International coverage of the Trump presidency centers on an escalating clash with major news outlets — including bans, lawsuits and the launch of a White House-backed 'Trump TV' — that many outlets frame as a threat to press freedom. Polling across multiple countries shows Trump at career-low approval ratings, driven by inflation, cost-of-living pressures and foreign policy strains. At the same time, his administration is pushing assertive technology and security initiatives (AI forces, military-technology proposals) and staking out forceful foreign-policy positions on the Middle East, the Gulf and Ukraine. Other strands of reporting highlight transactional politics and surprising cross-partisan meetings at home, economic proposals (tariffs and large stimulus checks), and continued scrutiny of insider perks and presidential symbolism.
