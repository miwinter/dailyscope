Moscow restaurant blast prompts terrorism probe

Russia: Elite Restaurant Blast 2026-08-03 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • A deadly explosion in a Moscow restaurant killed 3 to 5 people and injured about 21.
  • Officials call the blast a terrorist attack and vow to increase security amid ongoing investigations.
  • An unconfirmed report claims the target was a Russian general linked to the Bucha killings.

A deadly explosion tore through a restaurant in central Moscow, killing between three and five people and injuring around 21 according to differing reports. Russian authorities have launched an investigation; some officials, including the mayor, described the blast as a terrorist attack and pledged to step up security. At least one international outlet reported that the apparent target may have been a Russian general accused in the Bucha killings, a claim not yet confirmed by investigators. Investigations continue amid conflicting casualty figures and heightened security concerns as authorities seek perpetrators and motive.
