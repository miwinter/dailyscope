Bilateral Diplomacy: Deals, Visits and Strategic Realignments

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-09-12 • 142 articles in total

In brief
  • Bilateral diplomacy features major defence, economic, and capacity-building agreements amid regional rivalries and diverse strategic partnerships.
  • Countries pursue multifaceted security, trade, and infrastructure cooperation with multiple partners, reflecting pragmatic and autonomous engagement.
  • Diplomatic relations experience both strengthening ties through visits and agreements and ruptures due to regional tensions and policy disagreements.

This collection of reports captures a busy period of bilateral diplomacy featuring long-term security pacts, investment agreements, state visits, and occasional ruptures. Major defence and security commitments (notably Canada–Ukraine and broader Western support for Ukraine) sit alongside development and infrastructure deals driven by China, Saudi and Western funds. Several Pacific and small island states are diversifying partners—engaging China, India, the UAE and Western donors—for infrastructure, training and maritime cooperation, while North African and Gulf tensions produced a high-profile diplomatic break. Overall, the items show states using bilateral arrangements to secure strategic, economic and capacity-building goals even as regional rivalries and domestic politics shape outcomes.
