Huawei Convicted Over Trade-Secret Theft

Huawei IP Theft Conviction 2026-11-25 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • A U.S. jury convicted Huawei of stealing trade secrets and committing bank fraud related to its Iran business, marking a major legal setback.
  • The verdict highlights escalating U.S.–China tensions over technology, intellectual property, sanctions enforcement, and may impact global telecom sectors.
  • Huawei denies allegations and plans to appeal, with outcomes potentially reshaping enforcement and market access for Chinese tech firms.

A U.S. jury convicted Huawei of stealing trade secrets and related offenses, with several reports also noting findings of bank fraud and deception tied to its Iran business. The verdict underscores mounting U.S.–China tensions over technology, intellectual property and sanctions enforcement and represents a major legal setback for the company. Analysts warn the decision could carry significant legal, financial and competitive implications for Huawei and for the wider global telecoms and technology sectors. Huawei has indicated it will contest the rulings, and appeals or penalties could reshape future enforcement and market access for Chinese tech firms.
