China's Shenzhou-24 launch

China Shenzhou-24 Launch 2026-11-25 • 14 articles in total

In brief
  • China's Shenzhou-24 mission launched successfully, supporting Tiangong space station operations and experiments.
  • A Pakistani pilot joined as the first foreign astronaut on a Chinese mission, marking historic international cooperation.
  • The mission underscores China's growing space capabilities and promotes bilateral space collaboration with Pakistan.

China's Shenzhou-24 mission launched successfully and inserted into orbit, with officials describing the liftoff and separation as a complete success. The flight will support ongoing operations and experiments aboard the Tiangong space station. For the first time on a Chinese mission a foreign astronaut — a Pakistani pilot — is participating, marking a historic milestone and enabling short-duration scientific work. The mission highlights both Beijing's growing orbital capabilities and an expanding pattern of international cooperation in human spaceflight.
