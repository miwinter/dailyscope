Alleged breach of FBI employee data

FBI Data Breach 2026-09-23 • 5 articles in total

In brief
  • Hackers claiming to be ShinyHunters say they stole over 2TB of FBI employee data by exploiting an Oracle PeopleSoft flaw on FBIjobs.gov.
  • The breach claims are unverified and not confirmed by the FBI as of the reports, but authorities and experts are investigating.
  • The incident raises serious privacy, security, and national-security concerns for federal personnel and agencies amid growing cyber threats.

Multiple news outlets report that hackers claim to have obtained personal data of thousands of FBI employees, with some naming the group ShinyHunters. One report alleges the breach targeted the FBI jobs site (FBIjobs.gov), exfiltrating over 2TB of data and exploiting an Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability. Other coverage emphasizes the potential privacy and security implications for affected employees and for U.S. federal agencies. Most accounts note the claims have not been independently verified and the FBI had not publicly confirmed a breach at the time of reporting. Cybersecurity experts and authorities are cited as investigating the assertions and warning that leaked personnel data could enable further misuse.
