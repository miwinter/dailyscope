US diplomacy under strain: UN confrontations, China summit and shifting partnerships

US Diplomacy Watch 2026-09-23 • 59 articles in total

In brief
  • U.S. diplomacy shows confrontational UN rhetoric from Trump while maintaining strategic rivalry and cordiality with China.
  • Washington deepens regional military ties and engages discreetly with Venezuela amid mixed reactions to assertive tactics.
  • European and international views warn U.S. overreach risks alliance trust and call for balanced multilateralism.

Recent coverage shows U.S. diplomacy marked by confrontational UN rhetoric from President Trump that provoked walkouts, sharp rebukes from allies and renewed attacks on institutions such as the ICC. At the same time high-level engagement with China produced mixed assessments — public cordiality paired with deep strategic rivalry, Taiwan pressure and allied defense cooperation. Regional diplomacy is active and uneven: Washington is deepening military and security ties in places from Georgia and Somalia to the Pacific while quietly meeting with Venezuelan figures and pressing migration and aid deals in Africa. European and other commentators worry that assertive U.S. tactics risk unilateralism, erode multilateral norms and fuel perceptions of declining credibility even as bilateral partnerships and defense relationships continue to evolve.
