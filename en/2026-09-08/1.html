Bilateral Deals Shift Regional Alignments and Investments

Region: Bilateral Deals 2026-09-08 • 116 articles in total

In brief
  • Russia and North Korea deepen ties with a new road bridge and military cooperation signaling shifting strategic alignments.
  • Europe intensifies diplomatic and investment efforts in Greenland amid US Arctic interest while Russia escalates tensions with Germany and expands outreach in Africa and the Global South.
  • Bilateral agreements globally advance infrastructure, trade, security partnerships, and sectoral cooperation to boost regional influence and economic ties.

This set of reports shows an active period of bilateral diplomacy driven by infrastructure, security partnerships and targeted investment. High-profile infrastructure links and military cooperation — notably the new Russia–North Korea road bridge and a series of arms and training partnerships — signal shifting strategic alignments. Europe has mounted a concerted diplomatic investment push in Greenland while Russia’s punitive moves against Germany and growing Russia/China outreach across Africa and the Global South highlight deepening geopolitical competition. Parallel threads of trade, development finance, state visits and small‑scale cooperation (from Pacific policing to carbon markets) underscore that bilateral deals are shaping regional influence, economic ties and security arrangements worldwide.
