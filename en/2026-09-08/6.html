Mladić funeral deepens Balkan divisions

Mladic War Crimes Funeral 2026-09-08 • 18 articles in total

In brief
  • Thousands in Belgrade publicly mourned Ratko Mladić with state tributes despite his war criminal conviction.
  • EU institutions and Western media condemned the glorification and warned it hinders Serbia's EU accession and regional reconciliation.
  • Croatia and Bosnia condemned the funeral, fearing it risks Western Balkans stability and demands stronger diplomatic responses.

Thousands gathered in Belgrade to mourn Ratko Mladić, with his body lying in state, long queues to view the coffin and a state tribute preceding his burial. The ceremonies exposed a sharp divide: domestic scenes of honor and reverence contrasted with strong condemnation from EU institutions and Western commentators. Regional neighbours, particularly Croatia and Bosnia, expressed outrage and called for firmer diplomatic and legal responses, warning that the event risks destabilizing the Western Balkans. Analysts warn the funeral underlines persistent nationalist memory politics in Serbia and could be politically leveraged, complicating reconciliation and Serbia's European prospects.
