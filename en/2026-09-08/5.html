AfD historic win shakes Germany and alarms Europe

Germany AfD Campaigns 2026-09-08 • 64 articles in total

In brief
  • The far-right AfD won a historic state election in Saxony-Anhalt, shocking Germany's mainstream parties and threatening national political stability.
  • Thousands protested the AfD's rise, revealing deep social polarization and strong public opposition across German cities.
  • International actors expressed concern over the AfD victory's impact on European security and political dynamics.

A far-right surge in Saxony-Anhalt produced a historic AfD victory that many outlets call the first state-level breakthrough of its kind since World War II, forcing national soul‑searching about party dynamics and voter anger. Chancellor Friedrich Merz and other mainstream leaders described the result as a tectonic shock to the party system, even as the AfD insists it has a clear mandate to govern and explores coalition options. Thousands of protesters and civil society groups mobilized across German cities, underscoring deep social polarization and broad public pushback. International reactions ranged from EU concern and warnings about regional stability to opportunistic rejoicing from geopolitical rivals, while analysts probe the economic and cultural drivers behind the party’s gains and what it means for Germany’s future politics.
