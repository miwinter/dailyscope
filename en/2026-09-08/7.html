King Charles formalises Harry and Meghan's non-working status

UK Monarchy: Prince Harry Status 2026-09-08 • 9 articles in total

In brief
  • King Charles III confirmed Prince Harry and Meghan Markle remain non-working royals and cannot use 'Their Royal Highnesses' officially.
  • Buckingham Palace and the Lord Chamberlain issued formal communications defining the couple's restricted status and duties.
  • The clarification aims to manage public expectations and protect the monarchy but may heighten family tensions and media scrutiny.

King Charles III has made clear that Prince Harry and Meghan Markle will remain non-working members and effectively outside the official royal family. Buckingham Palace communications and a Lord Chamberlain letter spell out that the couple will not carry out royal duties and will be restricted from using the style 'Their Royal Highnesses' in official contexts. The clarification followed the couple’s return to the UK and has been reiterated across multiple statements to government, the armed forces and the royal household. Analysts interpret the move as an effort to manage expectations, reduce institutional ambiguity and protect the monarchy’s public role, while observers warn it may prolong tensions within the family and keep media scrutiny high.
