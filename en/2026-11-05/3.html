US–Iran Strikes Watch

US–Iran Strikes Watch 2026-11-05 • 24 articles in total

In brief
  • Washington signals readiness to continue or escalate military strikes against Iran despite political and electoral divisions in the US.
  • Iran warns that further attacks could trigger wider regional conflict, emphasizing risks of escalation beyond the Gulf.
  • Analysts present scenarios from diplomacy to full-scale war, highlighting deep uncertainty about the conflict's future direction.

Washington’s public signals and leadership rhetoric suggest a continued readiness to use military force against Iran, even as some limited strikes have been reported. Tehran has responded with warnings that renewed attacks could broaden the conflict beyond the Gulf, raising fears of regional escalation. Domestically, politicians and analysts are divided: some defend or urge continued action while others call for de‑escalation after electoral fallout. Independent analyses outline possible paths ranging from diplomacy to full-scale war, underscoring deep uncertainty over how the situation will unfold.
