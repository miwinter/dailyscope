China launches fitness drive; international coverage spotlights Colombia quake

China Fitness Campaign 2026-08-11 • 6 articles in total

In brief
  • China launched a nationwide fitness campaign to increase physical activity and prevent disease across urban and rural areas as a long-term health policy.
  • A magnitude-7.4 earthquake struck Colombia, with initial reports confirming at least two residents safe amid ongoing monitoring.
  • Experts explain Colombia's seismic risks, how deep earthquakes cause surface shaking, and emphasize the need for resilient infrastructure and preparedness.

China has launched a nationwide fitness campaign aimed at increasing physical activity across urban and rural areas, framed by authorities as a long-term public health and disease-prevention policy. Separately, international reporting focused on a magnitude-7.4 earthquake in Colombia, with immediate safety updates confirming at least two residents were safe. Scientific and risk-analysis pieces explain why Colombia is seismically vulnerable and how deep quakes can still produce strong surface shaking depending on geology, soil conditions and building standards. Eyewitness accounts convey the human impact and fear on the ground, underlining the need for preparedness, resilient infrastructure and coordinated monitoring.
