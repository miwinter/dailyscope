Hurricane Polo: Track, Intensification, and Cross‑Border Rain Threat

Polo Hurricane Track 2026-09-27 • 4 articles in total

In brief
  • Hurricane Polo rapidly intensified to Category 5 and shifted toward Mexico, threatening heavy rain, strong winds, and flooding along Mexican coasts.
  • Rainfall from Polo may extend into parts of the United States, prompting cross-border advisories and calls for preparedness.
  • A separate Pacific disturbance passed Guam heading toward Japan, highlighting broad storm activity in the region.

Hurricane Polo has rapidly intensified into a major hurricane and shifted its track toward Mexico, with at least one report upgrading it to Category 5. Forecasts warn of heavy rainfall, strong winds, and flooding along Mexican coastlines, and meteorologists say the associated rains could extend into parts of the United States. Authorities across the region have issued advisories and urged residents to monitor updates and prepare for possible disruptions as the storm's exact path and impacts remain uncertain. Separately, a Pacific disturbance passed Guam and is heading toward Japan, highlighting active storm patterns across the basin though not directly related to Polo's Mexico–U.S. threat.
