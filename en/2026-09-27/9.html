Spain protests: Political and housing grievances fuel calls for Sánchez to go

Spain Protests: Sánchez Resignation 2026-09-27 • 3 articles in total

In brief
  • Thousands protested across Spain demanding Prime Minister Sánchez's resignation and showing deep political dissatisfaction.
  • Some protests focus on social issues like housing instability and evictions, linking local grievances to broader unrest.

Mass demonstrations across Spain have seen thousands call for Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez to resign, highlighting deep political dissatisfaction and strong opposition mobilization. Government spokespeople have issued responses while protesters and organizers press demands for accountability and change. Some actions are explicitly tied to social issues, notably a Madrid encampment protesting the eviction of an 87‑year‑old and broader housing instability. Local figures such as Maricarmen have publicly addressed Sánchez amid the unrest, underscoring a mix of partisan and socioeconomic grievances that could shape domestic politics.
