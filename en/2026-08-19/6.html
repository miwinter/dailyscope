Putin tightens control through purges, secrecy and economic pressure

Russia Power Consolidation 2026-08-19 • 10 articles in total

In brief
  • Russia is consolidating power through political repression, detaining dissenters, and silencing critics amid elite reshuffles.
  • The country faces economic instability marked by mass bank withdrawals and growing financial anxiety.
  • Russia is enhancing its secretive military posture abroad, raising regional security concerns and emphasizing external threats.

Recent reporting shows a multi-pronged consolidation of power in Russia: senior figures in industry and dissenting technocrats are being removed or detained while opposition voices face lengthy prison terms. Financial anxiety has spurred mass withdrawals amid fears of state intervention in deposits, reflecting widening economic and social instability. Military secrecy and allegations of expanding strike capabilities abroad accompany opaque commemorations of senior officers, signalling a tightened security posture and messaging control. Moscow also emphasizes external threats, alleging provocations against Russia, even as routine bureaucratic resignations hint at broader elite reshuffles. Together these developments point to domestic repression, economic strain, and an emboldened but clandestine military posture with risks for regional stability.
