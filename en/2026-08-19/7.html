Landmark U.S. Lawsuit Accuses Meta of Hooking Children

Meta Addiction Lawsuits 2026-08-19 • 16 articles in total

In brief
  • A U.S. trial accuses Meta of designing Facebook and Instagram to addict children and mislead the public.
  • Plaintiffs seek damages and structural changes like stricter age limits and removal of infinite scroll.
  • Meta denies the allegations and claims it prioritizes safety and compliance.

Across international coverage, a landmark U.S. federal trial brought by 29 state attorneys general accuses Meta of designing Facebook and Instagram to hook children, mislead the public, and maximize engagement through addictive features. Prosecutors and plaintiffs describe persuasive design, endless scrolling, and targeted ads as central harms, while some filings seek both massive damages and structural remedies such as stricter age limits and removal of infinite scroll. Meta denies the claims, says it prioritizes safety and compliance, and challenges the characterization of its data practices and intent. The outcome could reshape platform design, regulatory standards for children’s online safety, and how tech companies are held accountable for harms to minors.
