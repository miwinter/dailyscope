Poland Constitutional Crisis

Poland Constitutional Crisis 2026-10-31 • 41 articles in total

In brief
  • Poland's Constitutional Tribunal faces turmoil after its president's suspension, sparking street clashes and heavy police guarding the court building.
  • Opposition accuses the government of a coup while officials claim they are restoring legal order amid tense confrontations and forced entries.
  • The crisis prompts President Duda's abrupt return, international concern over judicial independence, and risks straining Poland's European relations.

A confrontation over control of Poland’s Constitutional Tribunal has escalated into street unrest and a heavy security presence after the suspension or removal of the court’s president. Riot police were deployed to guard the tribunal while opposition figures and some officials accused the government of staging a coup, and video and eyewitness reports documented tense clashes and forced entries. President Andrzej Duda and other senior figures abruptly cut short foreign trips and returned amid the turmoil, as domestic leaders traded claims about restoring the rule of law. The crisis has drawn diplomatic attention and raised wider concerns about judicial independence and democratic norms in Poland.
