Israel coalition talks: hung parliament and kingmakers

Israel Coalition Talks 2026-10-31 • 24 articles in total

In brief
  • Netanyahu’s bloc leads but lacks the 61 seats needed, causing a fragmented Knesset and intense coalition negotiations.
  • Arab parties have become kingmakers, facing political pressure as their support could decide the next government.
  • No clear majority raises risks of prolonged deadlock or new elections; President Herzog’s role may be crucial in resolving the impasse.

The election left Israel without a clear governing majority: Benjamin Netanyahu’s bloc leads in many counts but consistently falls short of the 61-seat threshold, producing a fragmented Knesset. Parties are engaged in intensive post-election negotiations as analysts map out coalition options, possible kingmakers, and the risk of prolonged deadlock. Arab parties have emerged as pivotal players, attracting both offers and political pressure as their potential support could determine the next government. President Isaac Herzog’s role and legal and political maneuvering may be decisive if blocs cannot cobble together a stable coalition, keeping domestic politics and regional diplomacy in flux.
