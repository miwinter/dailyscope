Russia-linked security threats in Germany and Europe

Russia Security Plot in Germany 2026-09-03 • 26 articles in total

In brief
  • Germany and allies accuse Russia of escalating hybrid warfare through explosive-drone attacks and covert sabotage preparations.
  • European institutions and NATO plan stronger diplomatic and security responses targeting Russian actions in Europe.
  • Russia denies all accusations and labels Western countermeasures as grave mistakes, heightening geopolitical tensions.

Allegations that Russia was behind an attempted explosive-drone attack at Leipzig/Halle airport and other covert operations have prompted German authorities and allies to describe the incidents as an escalation of Moscow’s hybrid warfare. Danish intelligence has publicly revealed highly specific preparations for sabotage targeting defense-industry firms, triggering regional alerts and calls for heightened protections. European institutions and NATO are coalescing around firmer diplomatic and security responses, including scrutiny of Russian-linked sites in Germany and potential retaliatory measures. Moscow rejects the accusations, with President Putin calling recent German actions a grave mistake, leaving tensions high as investigations proceed.
