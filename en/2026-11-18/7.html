Opposition-backed Knesset speaker weakens Netanyahu

Israel Knesset Speaker Vote 2026-11-18 • 16 articles in total

In brief
  • Matan Kahana was elected Knesset speaker with 66 votes, backed by Gadi Eisenkot’s bloc and opposed by Prime Minister Netanyahu.
  • Kahana's election marks a political setback for Netanyahu, weakening his control over parliamentary procedures and legislative agenda.
  • The change signals shifts in Israeli politics, affecting coalition stability, lawmaking priorities, and government dynamics.

The Israeli Knesset elected Matan Kahana as its new speaker with 66 votes, a result engineered by Gadi Eisenkot’s bloc and opposed by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. The appointment is widely seen as a significant political setback for Netanyahu, undermining his control over parliamentary procedure and the legislative agenda. Analysts warn the change could alter the pace and priorities of lawmaking and complicate coalition dynamics as parties jockey for influence. The outcome highlights broader realignments in Israeli politics and raises uncertainty about upcoming policy fights.
