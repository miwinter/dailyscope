Alaska Senate: Peltola's Historic Win

US Elections: Alaska Senate Victory 2026-11-18 • 15 articles in total

In brief
  • Mary Peltola won Alaska's Senate seat, becoming the state's first Indigenous senator and flipping a Republican seat to Democrat.
  • Her victory expanded the Democratic Senate majority to 56-44, marking the largest midterm gain in decades.
  • The result involved prolonged ranked-choice counting and is expected to influence legislative dynamics and Democratic leverage.

Mary Peltola, a Democrat, won the Alaska U.S. Senate seat, defeating incumbent Dan Sullivan and becoming Alaska’s first Indigenous senator. Her victory flipped a Republican-held seat and helped expand the Democratic majority in the Senate to 56-44. International coverage framed the result as the decisive final seat that produced the party's largest midterm gain in decades. The outcome followed prolonged counting and ranked-choice procedures that delayed the final result, and it is expected to shape legislative dynamics and strengthen Democratic leverage in the coming Congress.
