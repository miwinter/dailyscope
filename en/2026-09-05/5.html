Trump presidency: escalation abroad and legal, political turbulence at home

US: Trump Presidency 2026-09-05 • 50 articles in total

In brief
  • Trump's foreign policy includes aggressive threats to Iran and claims of military control over strategic waterways, raising risks of regional escalation.
  • Domestically, Trump's legal challenges to executive orders face judicial pushback amid controversies over pardons and personnel misconduct.
  • Symbolic acts and political messaging remain key to Trump's influence, highlighted by commemorative coins, public stunts, and major midterm campaign spending.

Coverage of Donald Trump’s actions and statements shows two dominant strains: aggressive foreign-policy posturing—particularly repeated threats and claimed strikes against Iran—and sustained domestic controversies including legal challenges to executive orders, pardons, and personnel scandals. International reporting highlights rhetoric about military readiness, control of strategic chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz, and contingency plans for the Middle East, while many outlets note risks of escalation. At home, courts continue to block or suspend Trump-backed decrees on mail-in voting and birthright citizenship, and stories of alleged misconduct by officials fuel political scrutiny. Cultural and symbolic moves—coins, monuments, place renamings, and publicity stunts such as themed games—underscore how image, messaging, and patronage remain central to his influence ahead of the midterms.
