Leaders Mobilize Diplomacy Across Crises and Rivalries

World Leaders Dialogue 2026-09-05 • 77 articles in total

In brief
  • World leaders engage in high-level diplomacy and mediation to address the Ukraine war and manage strategic competition.
  • Europe faces challenges to cohesion and values amid regional tensions and security concerns.
  • Regional and smaller states pursue pragmatic diplomacy on development, stability, ceasefires, and institutional representation.

World leaders are intensifying high-level diplomacy across multiple hotspots, with renewed shuttle diplomacy and mediation efforts focused on ending the war in Ukraine. Great-power summitry and bilateral engagement — from APEC sidelines to SCO meetings and planned US–China interactions — reflect competitive strategic jockeying as well as attempts to manage shared risks. Europe is wrestling with questions of values, cohesion and regional tensions, particularly over the Balkans and internal political shifts, even as NATO and EU institutions consider security measures. At the same time regional and smaller states are pursuing pragmatic engagement on development, stability and normative issues, from African multilateral coordination and Caribbean forums to Middle East ceasefire appeals and Syria’s tentative disarmament steps.
