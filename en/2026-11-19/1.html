Xi–Putin–Trump Trilateral Summit

Xi–Putin–Trump Trilateral Summit 2026-11-19 • 86 articles in total

In brief
  • Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin, and Donald Trump met in Shenzhen to discuss nuclear arms, Ukraine, Iran, AI, and trade amidst ongoing security tensions.
  • The summit focused on symbolic gestures and testing alignment, with low expectations for immediate major agreements or broad consultations.
  • European observers expressed concern about potential exclusive deals reshaping global power, while Russia and the US pursued strategic gains and managed public expectations.

A historic trilateral meeting in Shenzhen brought Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump together, mixing intense policy stakes with highly choreographed optics. Leaders traded gestures, bilateral pre-meetings and guarded statements while public expectations for immediate breakthroughs remained low. The agenda covered nuclear arms, the Ukraine and Iran wars, AI and trade, set against fresh security incidents such as strikes on the Crimean bridge and regional attacks. European allies and watchers voiced anxiety about possible great‑power realignments even as Beijing emphasized dialogue and ‘world peace.’
