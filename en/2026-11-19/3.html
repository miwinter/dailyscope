Great Power Rivalry: Multipolar Contest and Regional Security

Great Power Rivalry 2026-11-19 • 39 articles in total

In brief
  • US, China, and Russia are reshaping global power with summit diplomacy promoting multipolarity and challenging Western-led institutions.
  • Military actions by Russia and technological and nuclear advances by China heighten security tensions across Europe, Asia, and the Indo-Pacific.
  • Strategic rivalry extends to technology control, covert operations, and alliances, impacting export controls, intelligence, and European roles amid the Ukraine conflict.

Leaders of the United States, China and Russia are jockeying to reshape post‑Cold War institutions and regional balances, with summit diplomacy underscoring a push toward a multipolar order. Beijing and Moscow are coordinating rhetoric on protectionism and sanctions while the United States faces questions about leverage and strategy. Moscow’s military signaling and Beijing’s technological and nuclear advances are recalibrating security calculations across Europe, Asia and the Indo‑Pacific. Hybrid operations, covert influence campaigns and the war in Ukraine add volatility, forcing allies to adapt force posture, export controls and diplomatic alignments.
