Highway gains and renewed strikes in central Sudan

Sudan Security Update 2026-07-28 • 6 articles in total

In brief
  • The Sudanese army claims to have retaken a major highway linking Omdurman and El-Obeid amid ongoing clashes.
  • Drone strikes on El-Obeid continue despite the army's claimed advances, showing ongoing instability.
  • The RSF paramilitary leader declared he would no longer restrain his forces, increasing risks for civilians and humanitarian aid.

Recent reports describe shifting control of key transport routes in central Sudan amid renewed violence. The Sudanese army has publicly claimed it retook a major highway linking Omdurman and El-Obeid and a central axis, assertions that remain difficult to independently verify. Almost concurrently, multiple accounts detail drone strikes on El-Obeid after the army's claimed advances, underscoring the fragility of any security gains. Separately, a paramilitary leader signalled he would no longer restrain his forces, raising the risk of further escalation and threats to civilian movement and humanitarian access. Together these developments point to fluid frontlines, contested transport routes, and heightened risks for civilians and relief operations.
