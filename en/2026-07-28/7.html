Senegal president launches Kiiraay, splitting with Sonko

Senegal Party Formation 2026-07-28 • 7 articles in total

In brief
  • Senegalese President Bassirou Diomaye Faye founded the political party Kiray/Kiiraay — Les Patriotes Républicains, breaking from former ally Ousmane Sonko.
  • The party aims to establish a distinct political identity and mobilize supporters with a new platform and organization.
  • The split reshapes opposition alliances and may influence strategies for the 2027 local and 2029 presidential elections.

Senegalese President Bassirou Diomaye Faye has launched a new political party, variously called Kiray/Kiiraay — Les Patriotes Républicains. The move formalizes a break with former ally and ex-prime minister Ousmane Sonko and has attracted some former Pastef members. Observers say the split reshapes the opposition, could realign alliances and influence strategies ahead of the 2027 local elections and the 2029 presidential race. Coverage highlights the party's stated objectives and platform while noting competing reactions from supporters and opponents as Senegal's political landscape shifts.
