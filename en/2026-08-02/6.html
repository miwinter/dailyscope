Tourist plane crash near Peru's Nazca Lines kills 13

Peru Plane Crash 2026-08-02 • 32 articles in total

In brief
  • A small tourist plane crashed near the Nazca Lines in Peru, killing all 13 people aboard, including 11 passengers and two crew members.
  • Rescue teams and investigators arrived at the remote crash site to recover victims and begin examining causes and safety procedures.
  • The victims included several foreign nationals, prompting international consular notifications and raising concerns about aviation safety for regional tourist flights.

A small sightseeing plane en route to the Nazca Lines crashed in southern Peru, killing all 13 people aboard, including 11 passengers and two crew. Rescue teams and investigators reached the remote scene and began recovering victims while probing the causes of the accident. Several reports note that some victims were foreign nationals, prompting consular notifications and international concern. Coverage highlights immediate questions about aviation safety for tourist flights in the region and the operator's procedures. Authorities have opened an official investigation and said more details will be released as inquiries proceed.
